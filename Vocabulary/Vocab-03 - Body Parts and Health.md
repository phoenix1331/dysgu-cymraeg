## Emergency Medical Phrases (Ymadroddion Meddygol Brys)

| Welsh | Pronunciation | English |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **Help!** | HELP | Help! |
| **Brys!** | BREESS | Urgent!/Emergency! |
| **Galwch ambiwlans!** | GAL-ookh am-BEE-oo-lans | Call an ambulance! |
| **Dw i angen meddyg** | DOO ee ANG-en METH-ig | I need a doctor |
| **Mae e'n argyfwng** | MY en ar-GUV-oong | It's an emergency |
| **Mae rhywun wedi brifo** | MY rhee-oo-in WED-ee BRIV-oh | Someone is hurt |
| **Dw i'n sâl** | DOO een SAHL | I'm ill |
| **Dw i'n teimlo'n sâl** | DOO een TAME-lohn SAHL | I feel ill |
| **Mae poen gyda fi** | MY POYN GUH-dah vee | I have pain (South) |
| **Mae gen i boen** | MY gen ee POYN | I have pain (North) |
| **Ble mae'n brifo?** | BLEH mine BRIV-oh | Where does it hurt? |
| **Mae'n brifo fan hyn** | MINE BRIV-oh van HEEN | It hurts here |
| **Dw i'n alergaidd i...** | DOO een al-ER-geyeth ee | I'm allergic to... |
| **Dw i'n cymryd moddion** | DOO een CUM-rid MOTH-yon | I take medicine |
| **Dw i ar bressgripsiywn** | DOO ee ar press-GRIP-see-oon | I'm on prescription |
| **Mae gen i gyflwr meddygol** | MY gen ee KUV-loor meth-IG-ol | I have a medical condition |
| **Dw i'n disgwyl** | DOO een diss-GWEEL | I'm pregnant |
| **Dw i ddim yn gallu anadlu** | DOO ee theem un GAL-ee an-AD-lee | I can't breathe |
| **Dw i wedi cwympo** | DOO ee WED-ee KWIM-poh | I have fallen |
