## Common Directions Phrases (Ymadroddion Cyfarwyddiadau)

| Welsh | Pronunciation | English |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **Ble mae...?** | bleh MY | Where is...? |
| **Sut mae cyrraedd...?** | seet MY kuhr-EYETH | How do I get to...? |
| **Pa mor bell yw...?** | pah mor BELl you | How far is...? |
| **Ydych chi'n gwybod y ffordd i...?** | uh-DEEKH kheen GWUH-bod uh FORTH ee | Do you know the way to...? |
| **Ydych chi'n gyfarwydd â'r ardal?** | uh-DEEKH kheen guv-AR-with ahr AR-dal | Are you familiar with the area? |
| **Dw i ar goll** | doo ee ar GOLL | I'm lost |
| **Dw i wedi colli fy ffordd** | doo ee WED-ee KOL-ee vuh FORTH | I've lost my way |
| **Ewch yn syth ymlaen** | EH-ookh un SEETH um-LEYEN | Go straight ahead |
| **Trowch i'r chwith** | TROW-kh eer KHWEETH | Turn left |
| **Trowch i'r dde** | TROW-kh eer THEH | Turn right |
| **Cymerwch y tro cyntaf ar y chwith** | kum-ER-ookh uh TROH KUN-tav ar uh KHWEETH | Take the first turn on the left |
| **Dilynwch y ffordd hon** | dil-UN-ookh uh FORTH HON | Follow this road |
| **Mae e/hi ar y gornel** | MY eh/hee ar uh GOR-nel | It's on the corner |
| **Mae e/hi gyferbyn â'r banc** | MY eh/hee guv-ER-bin ahr BANK | It's opposite the bank |
| **Mae e/hi wrth ymyl y siop** | MY eh/hee oorth UM-il uh SHOP | It's next to the shop |
| **Does dim yn bell** | DOYSS dim un BELL | It's not far |
| **Mae'n bell iawn** | MINE BELL yown | It's very far |
| **Tua phum munud i ffwrdd** | TEE-ah pheem MIN-id ee FFOOR-th | About five minutes away |
| **Cerddwch am ddeg munud** | KER-thookh am THEG MIN-id | Walk for ten minutes |
| **Ewch heibio'r eglwys** | EH-ookh HAY-byoh-r EG-loo-iss | Go past the church |
| **Ar ôl y goleuadau traffig** | ar OHL uh gol-EYE-ad-eye TRAF-ig | After the traffic lights |
| **Wrth y groesffordd** | oorth uh GROYSS-forth | At the crossroads |
| **Wrth y cylchfan** | oorth uh KULKH-van | At the roundabout |

|---------|---------------|---------|

| **Llan-** | LHAN | church/enclosure |
| **Caer-** | KEYER | fort |
| **Pen-** | PEN | head/top/end |
| **Pont-** | PONT | bridge |
| **Tre-/Tref-** | TREH/TREV | town/homestead |
| **Nant** | NANT | stream/valley |
| **-wy/-ydd** | -WEE/-eeth | river (suffix) |
| **Afon** | AV-on | river |
