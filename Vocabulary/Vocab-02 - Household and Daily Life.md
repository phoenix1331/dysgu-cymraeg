# Household and Daily Life Vocabulary

> [!info] Home and Daily Living
> This module covers essential vocabulary for describing your home, furniture, appliances, and daily routines. These words are fundamental for everyday conversation in Welsh.

## Common Household Phrases

|-------|---------------|---------|

### Exercise 1: Room Identification

Match the Welsh room name with its English translation:

1. cegin
2. ystafell wely
3. ystafell ymolchi
4. ystafell fyw
5. swyddfa

3. I brush my teeth
4. I make breakfast
