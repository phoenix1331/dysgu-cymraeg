# Welsh Reading Practice - Graded Texts

> **Purpose:** Build reading comprehension skills through carefully graded Welsh texts from A1 (beginner) to B1 (intermediate) level, with vocabulary support and comprehension questions.

---

## How to Use This System

### **Daily Reading Practice**

**Recommended Schedule:**
- **A1 Texts (Weeks 1-4):** 5-10 minutes daily
- **A2 Texts (Weeks 5-8):** 10-15 minutes daily
- **B1 Texts (Weeks 9-12):** 15-20 minutes daily

### **Reading Strategy**

1. **First Read:** Read through without stopping, try to understand main idea
2. **Second Read:** Look up unknown words (vocabulary lists provided)
3. **Third Read:** Read for full comprehension
4. **Answer Questions:** Test your understanding
5. **Read Aloud:** Practice pronunciation and fluency

### **Don't:**
- ❌ Translate every word immediately
- ❌ Get frustrated by unknown words
- ❌ Skip the comprehension questions

### **Do:**
- ✅ Try to guess meaning from context first
- ✅ Focus on understanding main ideas
- ✅ Use vocabulary lists as needed
- ✅ Re-read texts multiple times
- ✅ Track your progress

## Text Levels Explained

### **A1 Level (Beginner)**
**Characteristics:**
- Very simple sentences
- Present tense mainly
- Common, everyday vocabulary
- 50-100 words per text
- Familiar topics (family, home, food, daily routine)

**Skills Developed:**
- Basic vocabulary recognition
- Simple sentence comprehension
- Present tense understanding
- Building reading confidence

**Files:** A1-01 through A1-05

---

### **A2 Level (Elementary)**
**Characteristics:**
- Short paragraphs
- Present, past, and simple future tenses
- Wider vocabulary range
- 150-250 words per text
- Everyday situations (shopping, travel, work)

**Skills Developed:**
- Paragraph comprehension
- Multiple tense recognition
- Inference skills
- Reading for specific information

**Files:** A2-01 through A2-05

---

### **B1 Level (Intermediate)**
**Characteristics:**
- Longer passages (400-600 words)
- Complex sentences
- All tenses, including compound
- Idiomatic expressions
- Varied topics (culture, stories, opinions)

**Skills Developed:**
- Extended text comprehension
- Understanding different text types
- Reading between the lines
- Cultural understanding
- Independent reading skills

**Files:** B1-01 through B1-05

## Extension Activities

### **For A1 Texts:**
1. **Highlight verbs** - identify all verb-nouns
2. **Find mutations** - circle all mutated words
3. **Rewrite in past tense** - change "dw i'n..." to "es i..."
4. **Draw the scene** - visualize and illustrate

### **For A2 Texts:**
1. **Summarize in English** - check understanding
2. **Summarize in Welsh** - practice writing
3. **Find all past tense verbs** - grammar practice
4. **Continue the story** - creative writing

### **For B1 Texts:**
1. **Write a response** - opinion essay in Welsh
2. **Analyze structure** - how is the text organized?
3. **Identify idioms** - cultural expressions
4. **Present to someone** - explain main ideas in Welsh

## Tips for Successful Reading Practice

### **Building Speed**
- Time yourself (but don't rush!)
- Re-read the same text multiple times
- Track words per minute improvement
- Aim for fluency, not just accuracy

### **Dealing with Unknown Words**
- Try to guess from context first
- Look at word parts (prefixes, roots, suffixes)
- Check if it's a mutation of a known word
- Only look up words blocking comprehension
- Keep a vocabulary notebook

### **Staying Motivated**
- Choose texts that interest you
- Don't force yourself to finish boring texts
- Celebrate finishing each level
- Share progress with language partners
- Join online Welsh reading groups

### **Active Reading**
- Ask questions as you read
- Predict what comes next
- Visualize the scenes
- Connect to your own experiences
- Discuss with others

## Assessment Rubric

### **A1 Reading Competency**
**You can:**
- ☐ Understand very short, simple texts
- ☐ Find specific information in simple materials
- ☐ Understand short, simple personal messages
- ☐ Recognize familiar names, words, and basic phrases
- ☐ Read very short, simple descriptions

**If you can do all these:** Move to A2!

---

### **A2 Reading Competency**
**You can:**
- ☐ Understand short, simple texts on familiar topics
- ☐ Find specific information in everyday materials
- ☐ Understand simple personal letters
- ☐ Understand basic instructions and signs
- ☐ Read short narratives in simple language

**If you can do all these:** Move to B1!

---

### **B1 Reading Competency**
**You can:**
- ☐ Understand texts on familiar topics
- ☐ Understand descriptions of events and feelings
- ☐ Read straightforward factual texts
- ☐ Understand the main points in news articles
- ☐ Understand contemporary literary prose with occasional dictionary use

**If you can do all these:** Congratulations! You're an independent reader in Welsh!
