## Text: Yn y Siop

Mae Gareth angen mynd i'r siop. Mae e'n edrych ar ei restr siopa.

Mae angen:
- Bara
- Llaeth
- Wyau
- Caws
- Ffrwythau
- Llysiau

Mae Gareth yn mynd i'r archfarchnad fawr yn y dre.

Yn gyntaf, mae e'n prynu bara ffres a llaeth. Yna, mae e'n prynu chwech wy a chaws caled.

Mae e'n mynd at y ffrwythau. Mae e'n dewis pedwar afal coch a thri banana. Mae e hefyd yn prynu dwy oren.

Yn olaf, mae e'n prynu llysiau - tatws, moron, a thomatos.

Mae popeth yn y fasged. Mae Gareth yn mynd at y til.

"Pymtheg punt a deg ceiniog, os gwelwch yn dda," mae'r siop-wr yn dweud.

Mae Gareth yn talu gyda'i gerdyn. "Diolch yn fawr! Pob hwyl!"

Mae e'n rhoi popeth mewn bagiau ac yn mynd adref.

## Grammar Focus: Numbers with Nouns

**Notice how numbers work with nouns:**

| **Welsh** | **English** | **Notes** |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| chwech wy | six eggs | "wyau" → "wy" (singular after number) |
| pedwar afal coch | four red apples | Masculine number for masculine noun |
| tri banana | three bananas | Masculine number |
| dwy oren | two oranges | Feminine number "dwy" (not "dau") |

**Key Rule:** After numbers 2-10, Welsh nouns become singular (not plural)!

**Masculine vs Feminine "Two":**
- **dau** (masculine): dau fachgen (two boys)
- **dwy** (feminine): dwy ferch (two girls)

## Cultural Note

**Shopping in Wales:**

**Types of shops:**
- **Archfarchnad** (supermarket): Large chains like Tesco, Sainsbury's, Morrisons
- **Siop gornel** (corner shop): Small local convenience stores
- **Marchnad** (market): Traditional markets in many Welsh towns
- **Siop y pentref** (village shop): Small shops in rural areas

**Welsh language in shops:**
- Many supermarkets in Welsh-speaking areas have bilingual signs
- In places like Caernarfon, Aberystwyth, or Carmarthen, you can shop entirely in Welsh
- Traditional markets often have Welsh-speaking vendors
- Co-op and some independent shops actively promote Welsh

**Popular Welsh products to look for:**
- **Caws Cymreig** (Welsh cheese): Caerphilly, Y Fenni (with mustard seeds)
- **Bara brith** (Welsh fruit loaf)
- **Cacennau Cymreig** (Welsh cakes)
- **Cig oen Cymru** (Welsh lamb)
