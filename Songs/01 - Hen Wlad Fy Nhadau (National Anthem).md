# Hen Wlad Fy Nhadau (Land of My Fathers)
## The Welsh National Anthem

---

## Full Lyrics with Phonetics and Translation

### Verse 1

**Mae hen wlad fy nhadau yn annwyl i mi,**
*(My hen wlad vuh NHAD-eye un AN-oo-il ee mee)*
*The old land of my fathers is dear to me,*

**Gwlad beirdd a chantorion, enwogion o fri;**
*(Gwlad BAY-rth ah khan-TOR-ee-on, en-WOG-ee-on oh vree)*
*Land of poets and singers, famous people of renown;*

**Ei gwrol ryfelwyr, gwladgarwyr tra mâd,**
*(Eye GOO-rol ruh-VEL-oo-ir, gwlad-GAR-oo-ir trah mahd)*
*Her brave warriors, very splendid patriots,*

**Dros ryddid collasant eu gwaed.**
*(Dross RUH-thid kol-LAS-ant eye gwy-ed)*
*For freedom they lost their blood.*

---

### Chorus (Cytgan)

**Gwlad, gwlad, pleidiol wyf i'm gwlad.**
*(Gwlad, gwlad, PLAID-ee-ol oov eem gwlad)*
*Country, country, I am devoted to my country.*

**Tra môr yn fur i'r bur hoff bau,**
*(Trah mohr un veer eer beer hoff bye)*
*While the sea is a wall to the pure beloved land,*

**O bydded i'r hen iaith barhau.**
*(Oh BUH-thed eer hen yaith BAR-high)*
*Oh may the old language endure.*

---

### Verse 2

**Hen Gymru fynyddig, paradwys y bardd,**
*(Hen GUM-ree vun-UH-thig, par-AD-wees uh barth)*
*Old mountainous Wales, paradise of the bard,*

**Pob dyffryn, pob clogwyn, i'm golwg sydd hardd;**
*(Pohb DUH-frin, pohb KLOG-win, eem GOL-oog seeth harth)*
*Every valley, every cliff, is beautiful to my sight;*

**Trwy deimlad gwladgarol, mor swynol yw si**
*(Troo DAYM-lad gwlad-GAR-ol, mohr SWIN-ol yoo see)*
*Through patriotic feeling, how enchanting is the sound*

**Ei nentydd, afonydd, i fi.**
*(Eye NEN-tith, av-ON-ith, ee vee)*
*Of her streams and rivers, to me.*

---

### Verse 3

**Os treisiodd y gelyn fy ngwlad tan ei droed,**
*(Oss TRAY-shoth uh GEL-in vuh NGWLAD tan eye droyd)*
*If the enemy has trampled my land under his foot,*

**Mae hen iaith y Cymry mor fyw ag erioed,**
*(My hen yaith uh KUM-ree mohr veew ag ER-ee-oed)*
*The old language of the Welsh is as alive as ever,*

**Ni luddiwyd yr awen gan erchyll law brad,**
*(Nee LITH-oo-id ur OW-en gan ER-khil low brahd)*
*The muse has not been hindered by the terrible hand of treason,*

**Na thelyn berseiniol fy ngwlad.**
*(Nah HEL-in ber-SAYN-ee-ol vuh ngwlad)*
*Nor the melodious harp of my land.*

## Quick Singing Guide

### Pronunciation Tips:
- **Gwlad** (gwlad) - Start with "g" then "oo-lad"
- **Nhadau** (NHAD-eye) - The "nh" is soft, like "n" + slight "h"
- **Hen** (hen) - Like English "hen"
- **Môr** (mohr) - Long "oh" sound, like "more"
- **Iaith** (yaith) - Sounds like "yie-th"

### Rhythm Notes:
- The anthem is sung in 3/4 time (waltz time)
- Take your time with the chorus - it's the most powerful part
- The final line of the chorus should be sung with pride and emphasis

### When It's Sung:
- Before rugby and football international matches
- At Welsh cultural events and Eisteddfodau
- National celebrations like St. David's Day (March 1st)
- Official ceremonies and formal occasions

### Performance Tips:
1. Stand straight and proud when singing
2. The chorus is typically sung louder and more forcefully
3. Usually only the first verse and chorus are sung at sporting events
4. At formal events, all three verses may be performed

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid:
- Don't rush through "pleidiol wyf i'm gwlad" - each word is important
- Remember "gwlad" appears 3 times in the chorus - practice it!
- The "ll" sound in "collasant" is tricky - tongue position for "L" then blow air
- "Dros ryddid" needs the rolled "r" sound

---

## Cultural Notes:
- Written by Evan James (lyrics) and James James (music) in 1856
- First performed in the Vestry of Capel Tabor, Maesteg
- Became recognized as the Welsh national anthem in the 1890s
- One of the oldest national anthems in the world
- The melody is also used for the Cornish anthem "Bro Goth Agan Tasow"

---

3. Practice the chorus first - it repeats so you'll sing it most
4. Sing verse 1 with the chorus
5. Build up to full speed gradually
