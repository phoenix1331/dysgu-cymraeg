## 🔗 Quick Reference

### Common Phrases
- Bore da - Good morning (BOH-reh DAH)
- Diolch - Thank you (DEE-olkh)
- Croeso - You're welcome (KROY-so)
- Sut dych chi? - How are you? (SEET deekh KHEE)
- Dw i'n dysgu Cymraeg - I'm learning Welsh (DOO een DUH-sgee cum-RIGH-g)

### Essential Patterns
- **I am doing**: Dw i'n + verb-noun (Dw i'n mynd = I am going)
- **He/She is doing**: Mae e/hi'n + verb-noun (Mae hi'n dod = She is coming)
- **We are doing**: Dyn ni'n + verb-noun (Dyn ni'n dysgu = We are learning)
- **You are doing**: Wyt ti'n + verb-noun (informal) / Dych chi'n + verb-noun (formal)
- **They are doing**: Maen nhw'n + verb-noun (Maen nhw'n chwarae = They are playing)

### Possessives Quick Guide
- **my** (fy + nasal): fy nghar (my car), fy mhen (my head), fy nhad (my father)
- **your** (dy + soft): dy gar (your car), dy ben (your head), dy dad (your father)
- **his** (ei + soft): ei gar (his car)
- **her** (ei + aspirate): ei char (her car), ei phen (her head), ei thad (her father)

### Days of the Week
- Llun (Monday), Mawrth (Tuesday), Mercher (Wednesday), Iau (Thursday), Gwener (Friday), Sadwrn (Saturday), Sul (Sunday)

### Numbers 1-10
- un, dau/dwy, tri/tair, pedwar/pedair, pump, chwech, saith, wyth, naw, deg
