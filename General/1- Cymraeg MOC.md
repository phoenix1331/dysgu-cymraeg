## 🔗 Quick Reference

### Common Phrases
- **Bore da** - Good morning (BOH-reh dah)
- **Prynhawn da** - Good afternoon (pruhn-HOWN dah)
- **Nos da** - Good night (nohs dah)
- **Diolch** - Thank you (DEE-olkh)
- **Croeso** - You're welcome (KROY-soh)
- **Sut dych chi?** - How are you? (seet deekh khee?)
- **Dw i'n dysgu Cymraeg** - I'm learning Welsh (doo een DUH-sgee kum-RAYG)
- **Pob lwc** - Good luck (pohb look)

### Essential Patterns
- **I am doing**: Dw i'n + verb-noun (Dw i'n mynd = I am going)
- **He/She is doing**: Mae e/hi'n + verb-noun (Mae hi'n dod = She is coming)
- **We are doing**: Dan ni'n + verb-noun (Dan ni'n dysgu = We are learning)
- **You are doing**: Wyt ti'n + verb-noun (informal) / Dych chi'n + verb-noun (formal)
- **They are doing**: Maen nhw'n + verb-noun (Maen nhw'n chwarae = They are playing)

### Possessives Quick Guide
- **my** (fy + nasal): fy nghar (my car), fy mhen (my head), fy nhad (my father)
- **your** (dy + soft): dy gar (your car), dy ben (your head), dy dad (your father)
- **his** (ei + soft): ei gar (his car), ei ben (his head), ei dad (his father)
- **her** (ei + aspirate): ei char (her car), ei phen (her head), ei thad (her father)
- **our** (ein + no mutation): ein car (our car)
- **your pl.** (eich + no mutation): eich car (your car)
- **their** (eu + no mutation): eu car (their car)

### Days of the Week
- **Dydd Llun** (Monday), **Dydd Mawrth** (Tuesday), **Dydd Mercher** (Wednesday), **Dydd Iau** (Thursday), **Dydd Gwener** (Friday), **Dydd Sadwrn** (Saturday), **Dydd Sul** (Sunday)

### Numbers 1-20
- un, dau/dwy, tri/tair, pedwar/pedair, pump, chwech, saith, wyth, naw, deg, un deg un, un deg dau, un deg tri, un deg pedwar, pymtheg, un deg chwech, un deg saith, un deg wyth, un deg naw, ugain

### Question Words
- **Pwy?** - Who?
- **Beth?** - What?
- **Ble?** - Where?
- **Pryd?** - When?
- **Pam?** - Why?
- **Sut?** - How?
- **Faint?** - How much/many?
- **Pa?** - Which?

## 💡 Tips for Success

### Daily Practice
- **Consistency beats intensity** - 30 minutes daily is better than 3 hours once a week
- **Use the Daily Practice generator** - Keeps content fresh and varied
- **Read aloud every day** - Builds pronunciation and confidence
- **Don't fear mistakes** - They're essential for learning!

### Using This MOC
- **Bookmark this page** - Your central hub
- **Follow the 12-week path** - Proven progression
- **Take all assessments** - Track your progress
- **Review regularly** - Spaced repetition is key

### Staying Motivated
- **Set small goals** - One dialogue per day, one assessment per week
- **Celebrate wins** - Completed a week? Celebrate!
- **Find a language partner** - Practice with others
- **Immerse yourself** - Welsh music, TV, social media
- **Remember your "why"** - Living in Wales? Cultural connection? Keep that in mind!
