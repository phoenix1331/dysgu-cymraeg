# Numbers (Rhifau)

> [!info] Two Number Systems
> Welsh has two counting systems: traditional and modern (decimal). Most learners use the modern system, but you'll hear both.

## Numbers 0-10 (Modern System)

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| dim | DIM | zero |
| un | EEN | one |
| dau (m) / dwy (f) | DIGH / DOO-ee | two |
| tri (m) / tair (f) | TREE / TY-r | three |
| pedwar (m) / pedair (f) | PED-war / PED-eye-r | four |
| pump | PIMP | five |
| chwech | KHWEKH | six |
| saith | SIGHTH | seven |
| wyth | OO-eeth | eight |
| naw | NOW | nine |
| deg | DEG | ten |

> [!tip] Gender with 2, 3, 4
> Numbers 2, 3, and 4 have different forms for masculine and feminine nouns!

### Examples

```
un car - one car (m)
dau gar - two cars (m)
dwy gath - two cats (f)
tri chi - three dogs (m)
tair cath - three cats (f)
pedwar bachgen - four boys (m)
pedair merch - four girls (f)
pump afal - five apples
```

> [!note] Alternative Form
> You may also see "un ar ddeg" (EEN ar THEG) for 11, "deuddeg" (DAY-theg) for 12, etc. These are traditional forms.

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |

| trydydd (m) / trydedd (f) | TRUH-deeth / truh-DETH | third |

| pumed | PIM-ed | fifth |

| nawfed | NOW-ved | ninth |
| degfed | DEG-ved | tenth |

### Usage

```
y cyntaf o Fai
uh CUN-tav oh VIGH
the first of May

yr ail ddydd
uh EYEL THEETH
the second day

y trydydd tro
uh TRUH-deeth TROH
the third time
```

## Using Numbers with Nouns

### Singular After Numbers

In Welsh, nouns stay **singular** after numbers (except for 1):

```
un afal - one apple
dau afal - two apples (not "afalau")
tri car - three cars (not "ceir")
pump llyfr - five books (not "llyfrau")
deg ci - ten dogs (not "cŵn")
```

### Special Cases

Some common words have special plural forms after numbers:

| Singular | After Number | Phonetic |
|----------|--------------|----------|
| blwyddyn (year) | blwydd | BLOOITH |
| diwrnod (day) | diwrnod/dydd | DEE-oor-nod/DEETH |

```
un flwyddyn - one year
pum mlwydd - five years
deng mlynedd - ten years (alternative)
```

## Age

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Faint yw dy oed di? | VIGHNT you dee OYD dee? | How old are you? |
| Dw i'n [number] oed | DOO een [number] OYD | I am [number] years old |
| Dw i'n ddeunaw oed | DOO een THAY-now OYD | I am eighteen years old |

### Examples

```
Dw i'n dri deg oed
DOO een TREE DEG OYD
I am thirty years old

Mae hi'n bump oed
MIGH heen PIMP OYD
She is five years old

Dw i'n dau ddeg pump oed
DOO een DIGH THEG PIMP OYD
I am twenty-five years old
```

## Money & Prices

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| punt | PINT | pound (£1) |
| ceiniog | KAYN-ee-og | penny |
| Faint yw e? | VIGHNT you EH? | How much is it? |
| Mae'n costio... | MINE KOST-ee-oh... | It costs... |

### Examples

```
Mae'n costio pum punt
MINE KOST-ee-oh PIM PINT
It costs five pounds

dau ddeg punt
DIGH THEG PINT
twenty pounds

tri deg pum punt
TREE DEG PIM PINT
thirty-five pounds
```
