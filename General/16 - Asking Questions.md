# Asking Questions

> [!info] Essential Communication Skill
> Learning to ask questions is crucial for living in Wales and having real conversations. Welsh has specific question patterns, particles, and word orders that differ from English.

---

## Question Words (Interrogatives)

### Basic Question Words

| Welsh | Pronunciation | English | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|---------|
| **Pwy** | POO-ee | Who | Pwy wyt ti? (Who are you?) |
| **Beth** | BEHTH | What | Beth yw hwn? (What is this?) |
| **Ble** | BLEH | Where | Ble mae'r siop? (Where is the shop?) |
| **Pryd** | PREED | When | Pryd wyt ti'n dod? (When are you coming?) |
| **Pam** | PAM | Why | Pam mae hi'n mynd? (Why is she going?) |
| **Sut** | SEET | How | Sut mae? (How are you?) |
| **Faint** | VINE-t | How much/many | Faint yw e? (How much is it?) |
| **Pa** | PAH | Which/What | Pa un? (Which one?) |
| **Sawl** | SOWL | How many | Sawl un? (How many?) |

### Extended Question Phrases

| Welsh | Pronunciation | English | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|---------|
| **Pa fath** | PAH VATH | What kind/type | Pa fath o gar? (What kind of car?) |
| **Pa bryd** | PAH BREED | When | Pa bryd wyt ti'n mynd? (When are you going?) |
| **Pa mor** | PAH MOR | How (degree) | Pa mor fawr? (How big?) |
| **Faint o** | VINE-t OH | How much (quantity) | Faint o arian? (How much money?) |
| **I ble** | EE BLEH | To where | I ble wyt ti'n mynd? (Where are you going?) |
| **O ble** | OH BLEH | From where | O ble wyt ti'n dod? (Where are you from?) |
| **Gyda pwy** | GUH-dah POO-ee | With whom | Gyda pwy wyt ti? (Who are you with?) |

## Yes/No Questions

Welsh doesn't have a single word for "yes" or "no" - instead, you answer with the appropriate form of the verb!

### Question Particles with "Bod" (To Be)

| Person | Question Form | Pronunciation | English |
|--------|---------------|---------------|---------|
| I | **Ydw i...?** | UH-doo ee | Am I...? |
| You (sing) | **Wyt ti...?** | OO-eet tee | Are you...? |
| He | **Ydy e...?** | UH-dee eh | Is he...? |
| She | **Ydy hi...?** | UH-dee hee | Is she...? |
| We | **Ydyn ni...?** | UH-din nee | Are we...? |
| You (formal/pl) | **Ydych chi...?** | UH-deekh khee | Are you...? |
| They | **Ydyn nhw...?** | UH-din noo | Are they...? |

### Answering Yes/No Questions

**Positive Answers:**
- **Ydw** (UH-doo) - Yes (I am)
- **Wyt** (OO-eet) - Yes (you are - singular)
- **Ydy** (UH-dee) - Yes (he/she/it is)
- **Ydyn** (UH-din) - Yes (we/they are)
- **Ydych** (UH-deekh) - Yes (you are - formal)

**Negative Answers:**
- **Nac ydw** (NAK UH-doo) - No (I'm not)
- **Nac wyt** (NAK OO-eet) - No (you're not - singular)
- **Nac ydy** (NAK UH-dee) - No (he/she/it isn't)
- **Nac ydyn** (NAK UH-din) - No (we/they aren't)
- **Nac ydych** (NAK UH-deekh) - No (you're not - formal)

### Examples

```
Wyt ti'n dysgu Cymraeg?
OO-eet teen DUH-sgee cum-RIGH-g
Are you learning Welsh?

Ydw! / Nac ydw
UH-doo / NAK UH-doo
Yes! / No

Ydy hi'n bwrw glaw?
UH-dee heen BOO-roo glow
Is it raining?

Ydy / Nac ydy
UH-dee / NAK UH-dee
Yes / No
```

## "Oes" Questions (There is/There are)

Use **Oes** to ask "Is there...?" or "Are there...?"

### Structure

| Question | Pronunciation | English |
|----------|---------------|---------|
| **Oes...?** | OYSS | Is there...? / Are there...? |
| **Oes arian gyda ti?** | OYSS AR-ee-an GUH-dah tee | Do you have money? (Is there money with you?) |
| **Oes ci gyda chi?** | OYSS kee GUH-dah khee | Do you have a dog? |
| **Oes siop yma?** | OYSS shop UH-mah | Is there a shop here? |

### Answers

**Positive:**
- **Oes** (OYSS) - Yes (there is/are)

**Negative:**
- **Nag oes** (NAG OYSS) - No (there isn't/aren't)

### Examples

```
Oes coffi ar ôl?
OYSS KOF-fee ar ohl
Is there any coffee left?

Oes / Nag oes
OYSS / NAG OYSS
Yes / No

Oes car gyda ti?
OYSS kar GUH-dah tee
Do you have a car?

Oes / Nag oes
OYSS / NAG OYSS
Yes / No
```

## Question Word Order

### Standard Question Word Order

**Question Word + Verb + Subject + Rest**

Examples:
- **Beth wyt ti'n wneud?** - What are you doing?
- **Ble mae'r siop?** - Where is the shop?
- **Pryd mae hi'n dod?** - When is she coming?
- **Pam wyt ti'n mynd?** - Why are you going?

### Word-for-Word Breakdown

```
Beth wyt ti'n wneud?
What are you doing? (What are you-PROG doing?)

Ble mae'r ty?
Where is the house? (Where is-the house?)

Pwy yw e?
Who is he? (Who is he?)

Faint yw e?
How much is it? (How-much is it?)
```

## Negative Questions

Negative questions use **Nag** at the start:

| Welsh | Pronunciation | English |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **Nag wyt ti'n...?** | NAG OO-eet teen | Aren't you...? / Don't you...? |
| **Nag ydy e'n...?** | NAG UH-dee en | Isn't he...? |
| **Nag oes...?** | NAG OYSS | Isn't there...? |

### Examples

```
Nag wyt ti'n dod?
NAG OO-eet teen DOD
Aren't you coming?

Nag ydy hi'n dysgu?
NAG UH-dee heen DUH-sgee
Isn't she learning?

Nag oes amser gyda ti?
NAG OYSS AM-ser GUH-dah tee
Don't you have time?
```

## Tag Questions

Tag questions in Welsh add **ydw e?**, **ydw i?**, **ydy hi?**, etc. at the end to seek confirmation.

### Common Tag Patterns

| Welsh | Pronunciation | English |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **...ydw i?** | UH-doo ee | ...am I? |
| **...wyt ti?** | OO-eet tee | ...are you? |
| **...ydy e?** | UH-dee eh | ...is he? / ...isn't it? |
| **...ydy hi?** | UH-dee hee | ...is she? / ...isn't it? |
| **...yndw?** | UN-doo | ...isn't it? (informal) |
| **...ynde?** | UN-deh | ...isn't it? / ...right? |
| **...ie?** | YEH | ...yes? / ...right? |
| **...nage?** | NAH-geh | ...no? |

### Examples

```
Mae'n braf heddiw, ydy e?
MINE BRAV HEH-thee-oo, UH-dee eh
It's nice today, isn't it?

Rwyt ti'n dod, wyt ti?
ROO-eet teen DOD, OO-eet tee
You're coming, aren't you?

Dyn ni'n mynd yfory, yndyn ni?
DIN neen MIND uh-VOR-ee, UN-din nee
We're going tomorrow, aren't we?

Mae hi'n oer, ynde?
MINE heen OY-r, UN-deh
It's cold, isn't it?
```

## Practice Dialogues

### Dialogue 1: Meeting Someone

```
A: Shwmae! Sut wyt ti?
   SHOO-my! SEET oo-it tee
   Hello! How are you?

B: Iawn, diolch. Beth yw dy enw di?
   YOWN, DEE-olkh. BEHTH you dee EN-oo dee
   Fine, thanks. What's your name?

A: Carys ydw i. Pwy wyt ti?
   KAH-ris UH-doo ee. POO-ee oo-it tee
   I'm Carys. Who are you?

B: Dafydd. O ble wyt ti'n dod?
   DAH-vith. OH BLEH oo-it teen DOD
   Dafydd. Where are you from?

A: O Gaerdydd. Wyt ti'n byw yma?
   OH GYE-rith. OO-eet teen BIOO UH-mah
   From Cardiff. Do you live here?

B: Ydw. Ers pum mlynedd.
   UH-doo. AIRS pim MLUH-neth
   Yes. For five years.
```

### Dialogue 2: In a Shop

```
A: Bore da. Ga i helpu?
   BOH-reh DAH. GAH ee HEL-pee
   Good morning. Can I help?

B: Oes bara gyda chi?
   OYSS BAH-rah GUH-dah khee
   Do you have bread?

A: Oes. Pa fath wyt ti eisiau?
   OYSS. PAH VATH oo-it tee EYE-shy
   Yes. What kind do you want?

B: Bara brown, os gwelwch yn dda. Faint yw e?
   BAH-rah BROWN, ohs GWEL-ookh un THAH. VINE-t you eh
   Brown bread, please. How much is it?

A: Dau bunt, os gwelwch yn dda.
   DYE bint, ohs GWEL-ookh un THAH
   Two pounds, please.

B: Ble mae'r llaeth?
   BLEH mine KHLY-eth
   Where is the milk?

A: Yn y cefn, ar y chwith.
   UN uh KEV-n, ar uh KHWEETH
   At the back, on the left.
```

### Dialogue 3: Asking for Directions

```
A: Esgusodwch fi. Ble mae'r orsaf?
   es-gee-SOD-ookh VEE. BLEH mine OR-sav
   Excuse me. Where is the station?

B: Yr orsaf trenau?
   UR OR-sav TREN-eye
   The train station?

A: Ie. Pa mor bell yw e?
   YEH. PAH mor BELL you eh
   Yes. How far is it?

B: Deg munud ar droed. Wyt ti'n gwybod y ffordd?
   DEG MIN-id ar DROYD. OO-eet teen GWEE-bod uh FORTH
   Ten minutes on foot. Do you know the way?

A: Nac ydw. Sut dw i'n mynd?
   NAK UH-doo. SEET doo een MIND
   No. How do I go?

B: Cer yn syth ymlaen, troi ar y chwith. Wedyn mae'r orsaf ar y dde.
   KEHR un SEETH um-LYNE, TROY ar uh KHWEETH. WED-in mine OR-sav ar uh THEH
   Go straight ahead, turn left. Then the station is on the right.

A: Diolch yn fawr!
   DEE-olkh un VOWR
   Thank you very much!
```

## Tips for Asking Questions in Welsh

1. **Remember the verb comes second** - Question word, then verb, then subject
2. **No direct "yes/no"** - Answer with the appropriate verb form (Ydw, Oes, etc.)
3. **Oes vs Ydy** - Use "Oes" for "there is/are" and possession, "Ydy" for states/conditions
4. **Practice tag questions** - They're very common in natural Welsh conversation
5. **Listen for question intonation** - Sometimes Welsh speakers use statement word order with rising intonation for informal questions
6. **Mutations apply** - Question words can trigger mutations (Pa + soft mutation)
