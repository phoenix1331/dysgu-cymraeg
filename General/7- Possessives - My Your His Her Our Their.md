# Possessives - My, Your, His, Her, Our, Their

> [!warning] Mutations Are Critical Here!
> Possessives in Welsh cause different mutations. The mutation tells you WHOSE it is! "fy nghar" (my car) vs "dy gar" (your car) vs "ei char" (her car) - all different mutations.

## All Possessive Forms

| English | Welsh | Phonetic | Mutation Type | Example (car) |
|---------|-------|----------|---------------|---------------|
| my | fy | VUH | Nasal | fy **ngh**ar (vuh NKHAR) |
| your (informal) | dy | DUH | Soft | dy **g**ar (duh GAR) |
| his | ei | AY | Soft | ei **g**ar (ay GAR) |
| her | ei | AY | Aspirate | ei **ch**ar (ay KHAR) |
| our | ein | EYEN | No mutation | ein car (EYEN KAR) |
| your (formal/plural) | eich | EYKH | No mutation | eich car (EYKH KAR) |
| their | eu | AY | No mutation | eu car (AY KAR) |

> [!important] "ei" = His OR Her
> Both "his" and "her" are "ei" - the mutation tells you which one!
> - ei **g**ar = his car (soft mutation)
> - ei **ch**ar = her car (aspirate mutation)

## My (Fy) - NASAL MUTATION

### Nasal Mutation Changes

| Original | Mutates to | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| c | ngh | car → fy **ngh**ar (my car) |
| p | mh | pen → fy **mh**en (my head) |
| t | nh | tŷ → fy **nh**ŷ (my house) |
| g | ng | gardd → fy **ng**ardd (my garden) |
| b | m | bag → fy **m**ag (my bag) |
| d | n | dillad → fy **n**illad (my clothes) |

## Your (Informal) - Dy - SOFT MUTATION

### Soft Mutation Changes

| Original | Mutates to | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| c | g | car → dy **g**ar (your car) |
| p | b | pen → dy **b**en (your head) |
| t | d | tŷ → dy **d**ŷ (your house) |
| g | (disappears) | gardd → dy ardd (your garden) |
| b | f | bag → dy **f**ag (your bag) |
| d | dd | dillad → dy **dd**illad (your clothes) |
| m | f | mam → dy **f**am (your mother) |

## Her (Ei) - ASPIRATE MUTATION

### Aspirate Mutation Changes

| Original | Mutates to | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| c | ch | car → ei **ch**ar (her car) |
| p | ph | pen → ei **ph**en (her head) |
| t | th | tŷ → ei **th**ŷ (her house) |

> [!note] Only c, p, t Get Aspirate
> Only these three letters change with aspirate mutation. Everything else stays the same.

## Telling His/Her Apart

This is one of the trickiest parts of Welsh! Context and mutation help:

### His (Soft Mutation)

```
Mae ei gar e'n goch
MIGH ay GAR en GOKH
His car is red
(ei + soft mutation = his)

Mae ei dad e'n gweithio
MIGH ay DAD en GWEYE-thee-oh
His father is working
```

### Her (Aspirate Mutation)

```
Mae ei char hi'n goch
MIGH ay KHAR heen GOKH
Her car is red
(ei + aspirate mutation = her)

Mae ei thad hi'n gweithio
MIGH ay THAD heen GWEYE-thee-oh
Her father is working
```

> [!tip] Adding "e/fe/o" or "hi" for Clarity
> Add "e/fe/o" (him) or "hi" (her) after the noun to make it crystal clear:
> - ei gar **e** = his car (definitely his)
> - ei char **hi** = her car (definitely her)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Don't Confuse These!

| Correct | Wrong | English |
|---------|-------|---------|
| fy **ngh**ar | fy car | my car |
| dy **g**ar | dy car | your car |
| ei **ch**ar (her) | ei gar (her) | her car |

### Remember the Differences

```
fy nghar = MY car (nasal: c → ngh)
dy gar = YOUR car (soft: c → g)
ei gar = HIS car (soft: c → g)
ei char = HER car (aspirate: c → ch)
```
