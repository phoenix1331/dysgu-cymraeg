# Prepositions and Prepositional Phrases

Prepositions (Arddodiaid) are essential for expressing relationships between things - location, time, direction, and more. Welsh prepositions can be simple or conjugated (inflected) with pronouns.

---

## Simple Prepositions

### Location and Position

| Welsh | Pronunciation | English | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|---------|
| **yn** | un | in, at | Dw i yn y tŷ (I'm in the house) |
| **ar** | ar | on | Mae'r llyfr ar y bwrdd (The book is on the table) |
| **dan/tan** | dan/tan | under | Dan y bwrdd (Under the table) |
| **wrth** | oorith | by, near | Wrth y drws (By the door) |
| **ger** | gehr | near | Ger y parc (Near the park) |
| **rhwng** | rhoong | between | Rhwng y tai (Between the houses) |
| **o flaen** | oh vline | in front of | O flaen y siopau (In front of the shops) |
| **y tu ôl i** | uh tee ohl ee | behind | Y tu ôl i'r car (Behind the car) |
| **uwchben** | oo-ikh-ben | above | Uwchben y mynydd (Above the mountain) |

### Direction and Movement

| Welsh | Pronunciation | English | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|---------|
| **i** | ee | to, into | Dw i'n mynd i'r dref (I'm going to town) |
| **o** | oh | from, of | O Gymru (From Wales) |
| **at** | at | to, towards | At y meddyg (To the doctor) |
| **trwy** | troo-ee | through | Trwy'r drws (Through the door) |
| **dros** | dross | over, across | Dros y bont (Over the bridge) |
| **heibio** | hay-bee-oh | past | Heibio'r eglwys (Past the church) |
| **tua/tuag at** | tee-ah/tee-ag at | towards | Tua'r gogledd (Towards the north) |

### Time

| Welsh | Pronunciation | English | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|---------|
| **cyn** | kin | before | Cyn cinio (Before lunch) |
| **ar ôl** | ar ohl | after | Ar ôl gwaith (After work) |
| **ers** | airs | since, for | Ers blwyddyn (For a year) |
| **am** | am | for, at (time) | Am ddeg o'r gloch (At ten o'clock) |
| **yn ystod** | un UH-stod | during | Yn ystod y dydd (During the day) |
| **hyd** | heed | until | Hyd yfory (Until tomorrow) |

### Other Common Prepositions

| Welsh | Pronunciation | English | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|---------|

| **heb** | heb | without | Heb arian (Without money) |

## Conjugated/Inflected Prepositions

Many Welsh prepositions combine with pronouns to create specific forms. This is a key feature of Welsh grammar!

### AR (on) - Conjugated Forms

| Person | Welsh | Pronunciation | English |
|--------|-------|---------------|---------|
| I | **arna i** | AR-nah ee | on me |
| You (singular) | **arnat ti** | AR-nat tee | on you |
| He | **arno fe/fo** | AR-noh veh/voh | on him |
| She | **arni hi** | AR-nee hee | on her |
| We | **arnon ni** | AR-non nee | on us |
| You (plural) | **arnoch chi** | AR-nokh khee | on you |
| They | **arnyn nhw** | AR-nin noo | on them |

**Usage:**
- **Mae arna i eisiau...** (I want...) - literally "There is on me wanting..."
- **Mae arna i ddyled** (I owe money) - "There is on me debt"

---

### GAN/GYDA (with) - Conjugated Forms

| Person | Welsh | Pronunciation | English |
|--------|-------|---------------|---------|
| I | **gen i/gyda fi** | gen ee/GUH-dah vee | with me, I have |
| You (singular) | **gen ti/gyda ti** | gen tee/GUH-dah tee | with you, you have |
| He | **ganddo fe/gydag ef** | GAN-thoh veh | with him, he has |
| She | **ganddi hi/gyda hi** | GAN-thee hee | with her, she has |
| We | **gynnon ni/gyda ni** | GUH-non nee | with us, we have |
| You (plural) | **gynnoch chi/gyda chi** | GUH-nokh khee | with you, you have |
| They | **ganddyn nhw/gyda nhw** | GAN-thin noo | with them, they have |

**Usage:**
- **Mae gen i gar** (I have a car) - North Wales
- **Mae car gyda fi** (I have a car) - South Wales
- **Dere gyda fi!** (Come with me!)

## Mutations with Prepositions

Many prepositions cause mutations in the following word:

### Soft Mutation Triggers:
- **am** (about, for): am **b**ris → am **b**ris (for a price)
- **ar** (on): ar **g**ês → ar **g**ês (on a case)
- **at** (to): at **f**eddyg → at **f**eddyg (to a doctor)
- **dan/tan** (under): dan **d**ŷ → dan **d**ŷ (under a house)
- **dros** (over): dros **b**ont → dros **b**ont (over a bridge)
- **drwy/trwy** (through): trwy **d**drws → trwy **d**drws (through a door)
- **heb** (without): heb **g**ar → heb **g**ar (without a car)
- **i** (to): i **G**aerdydd → i **G**aerdydd (to Cardiff)
- **o** (from): o **G**ymru → o **G**ymru (from Wales)
- **wrth** (by): wrth **f**wrdd → wrth **f**wrdd (by a table)

### Nasal Mutation Triggers:
- **yn** (in): yn **Ng**haerdydd (in Cardiff), yn **nh**ŷ (in my house)

### Aspirate Mutation Triggers:
- **â/gyda** (with): â **ch**yllell (with a knife)
- **tua** (towards): tua **th**ref (towards town)

## Tips for Learning Prepositions

1. **Learn conjugated forms in chunks** - Practice "gen i, gen ti, ganddo fe..." as a rhythm
2. **Notice patterns** - Most conjugated prepositions follow similar patterns
3. **Practice with real sentences** - Don't just memorize lists
4. **Watch for mutations** - Prepositions often cause soft mutations
5. **North vs South** - Be aware of regional variations (gen i vs gyda fi)
6. **Common phrases** - Many expressions use prepositions idiomatically
