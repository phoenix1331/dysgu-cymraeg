## Welsh Social Etiquette Tips

**For successful integration:**

1. **Greetings:**
   - Hugs and cheek kisses common among friends
   - "Hwyl" more common than "hwyl fawr" for goodbye
   - Using someone's name shows respect

2. **Pub Culture:**
   - Rounds system: take turns buying drinks for the group
   - Standing at the bar is normal
   - Don't sit at a table before ordering

3. **Punctuality:**
   - More relaxed than some cultures
   - 10-15 minutes late acceptable for social events
   - Be on time for official appointments

4. **Conversation:**
   - Rugby and football are safe topics
   - Politics can be discussed (Welsh independence is debated)
   - Weather is always a good conversation starter
   - Ask about local area - Welsh people proud of their regions

5. **Language:**
   - Showing effort to learn Welsh greatly appreciated
   - Don't worry about mistakes - people will help
   - Switching between Welsh and English (code-switching) is normal
   - In North Wales, Welsh is more dominant
