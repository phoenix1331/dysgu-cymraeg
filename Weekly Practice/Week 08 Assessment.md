## Essential Prepositional Pronouns

Master these - they're used constantly in Welsh:

### "Ar" (on) - Used for "I need":
| **Form** | **Pronunciation** | **Meaning** |
|----------|-------------------|-------------|
| arna i | AR-na ee | on me (I need) |
| arnat ti | AR-nat tee | on you |
| arno fe | AR-noh veh | on him |
| arni hi | AR-nee hee | on her |
| arnon ni | AR-non nee | on us |
| arnoch chi | AR-nokh khee | on you (plural/formal) |
| arnyn nhw | AR-nin hoo | on them |

**Example:** Mae angen arna i = I need (lit. "There is need on me")

### "Gyda/â" (with):
| **Form** | **Meaning** |
|----------|-------------|
| gyda fi | with me |
| gyda ti | with you |
| gyda fe | with him |
| gyda hi | with her |
| gyda ni | with us |
| gyda chi | with you (formal) |
| gyda nhw | with them |
