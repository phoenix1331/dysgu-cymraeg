# 12-Week Welsh Learning Curriculum

> **Goal:** Structured progression from absolute beginner to conversational competency (A1 → A2 → early B1) through systematic daily practice and weekly assessments.

---

## Overview

This 12-week curriculum is designed for learners who can commit **30-60 minutes daily** to Welsh language study. By the end of 12 weeks, you should be able to:

- ✅ Hold basic conversations on everyday topics
- ✅ Understand and use 800-1000+ Welsh words
- ✅ Apply mutation rules naturally in speech
- ✅ Ask and answer questions confidently
- ✅ Read simple Welsh texts with comprehension
- ✅ Navigate daily life situations in Welsh-speaking Wales

---

## Curriculum Structure

### **Weeks 1-3: Foundation (CEFR A1)**
**Focus:** Phonetics, basic grammar, survival phrases

### **Weeks 4-6: Core Grammar (CEFR A1-A2)**
**Focus:** Pronouns, possessives, mutations, question formation

### **Weeks 7-9: Expansion (CEFR A2)**
**Focus:** Verb conjugations, prepositions, themed vocabulary

### **Weeks 10-12: Consolidation (CEFR A2-B1)**
**Focus:** Conversational fluency, reading comprehension, cultural integration

---

## Daily Time Breakdown

### **30-Minute Days** (Minimum Commitment)
- **10 min:** Daily Practice note (vocabulary review, exercises)
- **15 min:** Study one module or vocabulary topic (reading + notes)
- **5 min:** Review flashcards or phonetic practice

### **60-Minute Days** (Recommended)
- **15 min:** Daily Practice note
- **20 min:** Grammar/vocabulary study from curriculum module
- **20 min:** Conversation dialogue practice (read aloud, role-play)
- **5 min:** Progress tracker update + review

### **Weekly Additions**
- **1x weekly assessment** (30-40 min on weekend)
- **2-3x conversation practice** with language partner or online tutor (optional)
- **Daily passive exposure** (5-10 min Welsh social media, music, or news)

## Assessment Structure

Each weekly assessment includes:

1. **Vocabulary Review** (10 points)
   - Fill-in-blank exercises
   - Matching Welsh-English
   - Gender identification

2. **Grammar Exercises** (15 points)
   - Conjugation practice
   - Mutation application
   - Sentence construction

3. **Translation Practice** (10 points)
   - Welsh → English (5 sentences)
   - English → Welsh (5 sentences)

4. **Reading Comprehension** (5 points)
   - Short text with questions

5. **Listening Practice** (5 points)
   - Prompts to find and listen to Welsh media
   - Self-assessment of understanding

6. **Conversation Scenarios** (5 points)
   - Role-play prompts
   - Self-evaluation rubric

**Total per week:** 50 points
**Passing score:** 35/50 (70%)

## Tips for Success

### **Consistency is Key**
- Study every day, even if only 15-20 minutes
- Use the Daily Practice system to maintain momentum
- Don't skip assessments - they reveal progress and gaps

### **Active Practice**
- Read dialogues aloud every day
- Record yourself speaking Welsh
- Find a language partner or tutor
- Join Welsh social media groups

### **Immersion**
- Change phone/computer language to Welsh
- Listen to Welsh music daily (use Songs folder)
- Watch S4C (Welsh TV channel)
- Follow Welsh social media accounts

### **Don't Fear Mistakes**
- Mutations will feel unnatural at first - keep practicing
- Welsh speakers appreciate learners' efforts
- Making mistakes is part of the learning process

### **Cultural Connection**
- Learn about Welsh history and customs
- Attend local Welsh events (Eisteddfod, St David's Day)
- Understand regional variations
- Connect language learning to real-life goals (living in Wales)
