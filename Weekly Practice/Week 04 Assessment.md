**Important Note About Mutations:**

Mutations feel unnatural at first - this is NORMAL! Native Welsh speakers apply them automatically, but learners need practice. Don't worry if you make mistakes. With daily practice, mutations will become more intuitive.

**Key tips:**
- Soft mutations are by far the most common (80%+)
- Listen to Welsh speakers - hearing mutations helps
- Practice daily - even 5 minutes of mutation drills helps
- Don't let mutations discourage you - they're learnable!
