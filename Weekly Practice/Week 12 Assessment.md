## Part 2: Grammar Mastery (25 points)

### A. "Bod" - All Tenses (6 points - 1 point each)

Conjugate "bod" correctly:

1. Present: Dw i ________ hapus. (I am happy)
2. Past: ________ i'n sâl ddoe. (I was ill yesterday)
3. Future: ________ i yno yfory. (I will be there tomorrow)
4. Question: ________ ti'n iawn? (Are you okay?)
5. Negative: ________ i ddim yn gwybod. (I don't know)
6. Perfect: Dw i ________ gweld y ffilm. (I have seen the film)

### B. Mutations (6 points - 1 point each)

Apply the correct mutation:

1. fy + brawd = ________________ (my brother - nasal)
2. ei + tŷ (his) = ________________ (his house - soft)
3. ei + cath (her) = ________________ (her cat - aspirate)
4. yn + Cymru = ________________ (in Wales - soft)
5. dy + mam = ________________ (your mother - soft)
6. dw i'n + gw eld = ________________ (I see - soft)

### C. Question Formation (5 points - 1 point each)

Form questions:

1. (Who) ________ yw hwn?
2. (What) ________ wyt ti'n wneud?
3. (Where) ________ mae'r banc?
4. (When) ________ wyt ti'n dod?
5. (How much) ________ mae hwn?

### D. Verb Conjugation (4 points - 1 point each)

Use correct verb forms:

1. Dw i wedi ________ bwyd. (eat - perfect tense)
2. Mae hi'n ________ i fynd. (go - going to)
3. ________ i i'r dre ddoe. (go - simple past)
4. Maen nhw'n ________ Cymraeg. (speak - present)

### E. Prepositions (4 points - 1 point each)

1. Dw i'n byw ________ Caerdydd. (in Cardiff)
2. Mae angen ________ i help. (I need - "on me")
3. Dw i'n mynd ________ y gwaith. (to work)
