# Banking and Official Business

> [!info] Professional Welsh
> Essential dialogues for handling banking, postal services, and official business in Welsh. These conversations cover common transactions and formal interactions you'll encounter in Wales.

## Opening a Bank Account

### Dialogue 1
**Cwsmer:** Bore da. Hoffwn i agor cyfrif banc os gwelwch yn dda.
*(BOH-reh dah. hoff-oon ee AG-or KUV-riv bank oss gwel-ookh un thah)*
*Good morning. I would like to open a bank account please.*

**Swyddog:** Bore da. Wrth gwrs, gallwn ni helpu gyda hynny. Pa fath o gyfrif ydych chi eisiau?
*(BOH-reh dah. oorth goorss, gaLl-oon nee hel-pee guh-dah huhn-ee. pah vath oh guv-riv uh-deekh khee ay-shah?)*
*Good morning. Of course, we can help with that. What type of account do you want?*

**Cwsmer:** Cyfrif cyfredol, os gwelwch yn dda.
*(KUV-riv kuf-RED-ol, oss gwel-ookh un thah)*
*A current account, please.*

**Swyddog:** Iawn. Oes prawf o hunaniaeth gyda chi? Pasbort neu drwydded yrru?
*(yown. oh-ess prowf oh heen-an-YETH guh-dah khee? pass-port nay TROO-with-ed uh-ree?)*
*Okay. Do you have proof of identity? Passport or driving licence?*

**Cwsmer:** Oes, dyma fy mhasbort.
*(oh-ess, duh-mah vuh PASS-port)*
*Yes, here's my passport.*

**Swyddog:** Diolch. A oes prawf o gyfeiriad gyda chi hefyd? Bil trydan neu ddŵr, efallai?
*(dee-olkh. ah oh-ess prowf oh guv-EYER-yad guh-dah khee heh-vid? beel TRUD-an nay THOOR, ev-aLl-eye?)*
*Thanks. And do you have proof of address too? Electricity or water bill, perhaps?*

**Cwsmer:** Oes, dyma fil trydan o'r mis diwethaf.
*(oh-ess, duh-mah veel TRUD-an ohr meess dee-WETH-av)*
*Yes, here's an electricity bill from last month.*

**Swyddog:** Perffaith. Nawr, beth yw eich cyfeiriad e-bost?
*(per-FAH-eeth. now-r, beth you aykh kuv-EYER-yad EE-bost?)*
*Perfect. Now, what's your email address?*

**Cwsmer:** mae'n enw.cyntaf@ebost.com
*(mah-een EN-oo KUN-tav at EB-ost dot kom)*
*It's firstname.surname@email.com*

**Swyddog:** Diolch yn fawr. A beth yw eich rhif ffôn symudol?
*(dee-olkh un vow-r. ah beth you aykh rheev ffohn sum-ID-ol?)*
*Thanks very much. And what's your mobile phone number?*

**Cwsmer:** Sero saith naw pedwar pedwar, pump chwech saith wyth tri dau.
*(SER-oh saith nah-oo PED-weyer PED-weyer, peemp khwekh saith weeth tree dah-ee)*
*Zero seven nine four four, five six seven eight three two.*

**Swyddog:** Ardderchog. Ydych chi eisiau cerdyn debyd gyda'r cyfrif?
*(ar-THER-khog. uh-deekh khee ay-shah ker-din DEB-id guh-dar kuv-riv?)*
*Excellent. Do you want a debit card with the account?*

**Cwsmer:** Ydw, os gwelwch yn dda. Pryd fydd e'n cyrraedd?
*(uh-doo, oss gwel-ookh un thah. preed veeth een kuhr-EYETH?)*
*Yes, please. When will it arrive?*

**Swyddog:** Bydd y cerdyn yn cyrraedd o fewn pum i saith diwrnod gwaith.
*(beeth uh ker-din un kuhr-EYETH oh veh-oon peem ee saith dee-OOR-nod gweyeth)*
*The card will arrive within five to seven working days.*

**Cwsmer:** Gwych. A fydd PIN yn dod ar wahân?
*(gweekh. ah veeth PIN un dod ar WAH-han?)*
*Great. And will the PIN come separately?*

**Swyddog:** Bydd, bydd eich cod PIN yn cyrraedd dridiau ar ôl y cerdyn.
*(beeth, beeth aykh kod PIN un kuhr-EYETH DRID-eye ar ohl uh ker-din)*
*Yes, your PIN code will arrive three days after the card.*

**Cwsmer:** Perffaith. Diolch yn fawr iawn am eich cymorth.
*(per-FAH-eeth. dee-olkh un vow-r yown am aykh KUM-orth)*
*Perfect. Thanks very much indeed for your help.*

**Swyddog:** Croeso. Bydd eich cyfrif yn weithredol o fory ymlaen.
*(kroy-soh. beeth aykh kuv-riv un GAYTH-red-ol oh vor-ee um-LEYEN)*
*You're welcome. Your account will be active from tomorrow onwards.*

### Dialogue 2
**Cwsmer:** Prynhawn da. Oes angen llofnodi unrhyw beth?
*(prin-HOWN dah. oh-ess ang-en lhov-NOD-ee uhn-rhew beth?)*
*Good afternoon. Do I need to sign anything?*

**Swyddog:** Oes, rhaid i chi lofnodi'r ffurflenni hyn, os gwelwch yn dda.
*(oh-ess, rhide ee khee lhov-NOD-eer ffirf-LEN-ee heen, oss gwel-ookh un thah)*
*Yes, you need to sign these forms, please.*

**Cwsmer:** Iawn. Ble mae angen llofnodi?
*(yown. bleh my ang-en lhov-NOD-ee?)*
*Okay. Where do I need to sign?*

**Swyddog:** Fan hyn, ac yma ar y dudalen olaf hefyd.
*(van heen, ak uh-mah ar uh did-AL-en OL-av heh-vid)*
*Here, and here on the last page too.*

**Cwsmer:** Felly. A oes ffi am agor y cyfrif?
*(fel-ee. ah oh-ess fee am AG-or uh kuv-riv?)*
*There. And is there a fee for opening the account?*

**Swyddog:** Nac oes, mae agor cyfrif yn gwbl am ddim.
*(nak oh-ess, my AG-or kuv-riv un goo-bl am thim)*
*No, opening an account is completely free.*

**Cwsmer:** Gwych. A beth am ffi misol?
*(gweekh. ah beth am fee MISS-ol?)*
*Great. And what about a monthly fee?*

**Swyddog:** Does dim ffi misol ar y cyfrif cyfredol sylfaenol hwn.
*(doyss dim fee MISS-ol ar uh kuv-riv kuf-RED-ol sil-VEYEN-ol hoon)*
*There's no monthly fee on this basic current account.*

**Cwsmer:** Perffaith. Pryd gallaf i ddechrau ei ddefnyddio?
*(per-FAH-eeth. preed gaLl-av ee THEKH-reye ay thev-NITH-yoh?)*
*Perfect. When can I start using it?*

**Swyddog:** Gallwch chi ddefnyddio bancio ar-lein o heddiw ymlaen.
*(gaLl-ookh khee thev-NITH-yoh bank-yoh ar-LEYEN oh heh-thyoo um-LEYEN)*
*You can use online banking from today onwards.*

**Cwsmer:** Ardderchog. Sut mae cofrestru ar gyfer bancio ar-lein?
*(ar-THER-khog. seet my kov-REST-ree ar guh-ver bank-yoh ar-LEYEN?)*
*Excellent. How do I register for online banking?*

**Swyddog:** Bydd e-bost yn cyrraedd gyda'ch manylion mewngofnodi.
*(beeth EE-bost un kuhr-EYETH guh-dakh man-IHL-yon meh-oon-gov-NOD-ee)*
*An email will arrive with your login details.*

**Cwsmer:** Grêt. Diolch yn fawr am eich help.
*(grayt. dee-olkh un vow-r am aykh help)*
*Great. Thanks very much for your help.*

## Making Deposits and Withdrawals

### Dialogue 3
**Cwsmer:** Bore da. Hoffwn i adneuo arian os gwelwch yn dda.
*(BOH-reh dah. hoff-oon ee ad-NEY-oh ar-yan oss gwel-ookh un thah)*
*Good morning. I would like to deposit money please.*

**Swyddog:** Wrth gwrs. Faint hoffech chi adneuo?
*(oorth goorss. vah-eent hoff-ekh khee ad-NEY-oh?)*
*Of course. How much would you like to deposit?*

**Cwsmer:** Tri chant punt mewn arian parod.
*(tree khant peent meh-oon ar-yan par-OD)*
*Three hundred pounds in cash.*

**Swyddog:** Iawn. Oes eich cerdyn banc gyda chi?
*(yown. oh-ess aykh ker-din bank guh-dah khee?)*
*Okay. Do you have your bank card with you?*

**Cwsmer:** Oes, dyma fe.
*(oh-ess, duh-mah veh)*
*Yes, here it is.*

**Swyddog:** Diolch. Rhowch yr arian yn y peiriant cyfri fan hyn.
*(dee-olkh. rhow-kh eer ar-yan uhn uh pay-ree-ant KUV-ree van heen)*
*Thanks. Put the money in the counting machine here.*

**Cwsmer:** Felly. Mae'r peiriant yn dweud tri chant punt.
*(vel-ee. my-r pay-ree-ant un dweyd tree khant peent)*
*There. The machine says three hundred pounds.*

**Swyddog:** Perffaith. Nawr, rhowch eich cod PIN i gadarnhau.
*(per-FAH-eeth. now-r, rhow-kh aykh kod PIN ee gad-AR-nhow)*
*Perfect. Now, enter your PIN code to confirm.*

**Cwsmer:** Wedi gwneud. Pryd fydd yr arian yn y cyfrif?
*(weh-dee oo-nay-d. preed veeth eer ar-yan uhn uh kuv-riv?)*
*Done. When will the money be in the account?*

**Swyddog:** Bydd yr arian yn eich cyfrif yn syth.
*(beeth eer ar-yan uhn aykh kuv-riv un seeth)*
*The money will be in your account immediately.*

**Cwsmer:** Gwych. Ga i dderbynneb os gwelwch yn dda?
*(gweekh. gah ee ther-buhn-eb oss gwel-ookh un thah?)*
*Great. Can I have a receipt please?*

**Swyddog:** Wrth gwrs. Dyma'ch derbynneb. Ydych chi eisiau gwneud unrhyw beth arall?
*(oorth goorss. duh-makh ther-buhn-eb. uh-deekh khee ay-shah oo-nay-d uhn-rhew beth ar-aLl?)*
*Of course. Here's your receipt. Do you want to do anything else?*

**Cwsmer:** Nac ydw, diolch. Dyna i gyd am heddiw.
*(nak uh-doo, dee-olkh. duh-nah ee geed am heh-thyoo)*
*No thanks. That's all for today.*

### Dialogue 4
**Cwsmer:** Prynhawn da. Hoffwn i dynnu arian allan.
*(prin-HOWN dah. hoff-oon ee tuhn-ee ar-yan AL-an)*
*Good afternoon. I would like to withdraw money.*

**Swyddog:** Iawn. Faint hoffech chi dynnu?
*(yown. vah-eent hoff-ekh khee tuhn-ee?)*
*Okay. How much would you like to withdraw?*

**Cwsmer:** Dau gant punt, os gwelwch yn dda.
*(dah-ee gant peent, oss gwel-ookh un thah)*
*Two hundred pounds, please.*

**Swyddog:** Dim problem. Oes eich cerdyn gyda chi?
*(dim prob-lem. oh-ess aykh ker-din guh-dah khee?)*
*No problem. Do you have your card with you?*

**Cwsmer:** Oes. Alla i ddefnyddio'r peiriant arian parod?
*(oh-ess. aLl-ah ee thev-NITH-yoh-r pay-ree-ant ar-yan par-OD?)*
*Yes. Can I use the cash machine?*

**Swyddog:** Gallwch, mae'r peiriant draw fan yna.
*(gaLl-ookh, my-r pay-ree-ant draw van un-ah)*
*You can, the machine is over there.*

**Cwsmer:** Diolch. Ond mae'r peiriant wedi gwrthod fy ngherdyn.
*(dee-olkh. ond my-r pay-ree-ant weh-dee goor-THOD vuh NKHER-din)*
*Thanks. But the machine has rejected my card.*

**Swyddog:** O na. Gadewch i fi weld. Mae'n dweud bod y PIN yn anghywir.
*(oh nah. gad-EH-ookh ee vee weld. my-n dweyd bod uh PIN uhn ang-KHUH-wir)*
*Oh no. Let me see. It says the PIN is incorrect.*

**Cwsmer:** O diar. Gallaf i gofio nawr. Ga i drio eto?
*(oh DEE-ar. gaLl-av ee gov-yoh now-r. gah ee dree-oh et-oh?)*
*Oh dear. I can remember now. Can I try again?*

**Swyddog:** Gallwch, ond byddwch yn ofalus. Ar ôl tri chynnig anghywir, bydd y cerdyn yn cael ei gloi.
*(gaLl-ookh, ond beeth-ookh un ov-AL-iss. ar ohl tree KHUHN-ig ang-KHUH-wir, beeth uh ker-din un kah-el ay gloy)*
*You can, but be careful. After three incorrect attempts, the card will be locked.*

**Cwsmer:** Iawn, diolch am y rhybudd. Dw i'n siŵr nawr.
*(yown, dee-olkh am uh RHUH-bith. doo een shoo-r now-r)*
*Okay, thanks for the warning. I'm sure now.*

**Swyddog:** Llwyddiant! Dyma'ch arian parod - dau gant punt.
*(lhoo-ITH-yant! duh-makh ar-yan par-OD - dah-ee gant peent)*
*Success! Here's your cash - two hundred pounds.*

**Cwsmer:** Diolch yn fawr iawn. Cewch ddiwrnod da.
*(dee-olkh un vow-r yown. keh-ookh thee-oor-nod dah)*
*Thanks very much indeed. Have a good day.*

## Asking About Fees and Services

### Dialogue 5
**Cwsmer:** Bore da. Allwch chi egluro'r ffioedd banc i fi?
*(BOH-reh dah. aLl-ookh khee eg-LEER-oh-r fee-OY-th bank ee vee?)*
*Good morning. Can you explain the bank fees to me?*

**Swyddog:** Wrth gwrs. Pa fath o ffioedd sydd o ddiddordeb i chi?
*(oorth goorss. pah vath oh fee-OY-th seeth oh THITH-or-deb ee khee?)*
*Of course. What kind of fees are of interest to you?*

**Cwsmer:** Yn gyntaf, beth yw'r ffi am drosglwyddo arian tramor?
*(un gun-tav, beth you-r fee am dross-GLOOD-oh ar-yan TRAM-or?)*
*Firstly, what's the fee for transferring money abroad?*

**Swyddog:** Mae'n dibynnu ar y swm. Hyd at fil punt, y ffi yw ugain punt.
*(my-n dib-UHN-ee ar uh soom. heed at veel peent, uh fee you ee-gain peent)*
*It depends on the amount. Up to a thousand pounds, the fee is twenty pounds.*

**Cwsmer:** A beth am symiau uwch na mil punt?
*(ah beth am SUM-eye eekh nah veel peent?)*
*And what about amounts higher than a thousand pounds?*

**Swyddog:** Dros fil punt, y ffi yw dau y cant o'r swm.
*(dross veel peent, uh fee you dah-ee uh kant ohr soom)*
*Over a thousand pounds, the fee is two percent of the amount.*

**Cwsmer:** Iawn. A beth am gordrafft? Oes ffi am fynd dros fy mhres?
*(yown. ah beth am gor-DRAFT? oh-ess fee am veend dross vuh PRESS?)*
*Okay. And what about overdraft? Is there a fee for going over my balance?*

**Swyddog:** Oes. Mae ffi o bum punt y dydd am gordrafft heb ei awdurdodi.
*(oh-ess. my fee oh beem peent uh deeth am gor-DRAFT heb ay ow-DOOR-dod-ee)*
*Yes. There's a fee of five pounds per day for unauthorised overdraft.*

**Cwsmer:** A beth os dw i eisiau trefnu gordrafft swyddogol?
*(ah beth oss doo ee ay-shah TREV-nee gor-DRAFT sooth-OG-ol?)*
*And what if I want to arrange an official overdraft?*

**Swyddog:** Gallwn ni drefnu gordrafft awdurdodedig. Y gyfradd llog yw naw y cant.
*(gaLl-oon nee TREV-nee gor-DRAFT ow-door-DOD-ed-ig. uh guv-RATH lhog you nah-oo uh kant)*
*We can arrange an authorised overdraft. The interest rate is nine percent.*

**Cwsmer:** Diddorol. A oes ffi am ddefnyddio peiriant arian parod tramor?
*(dith-OR-ol. ah oh-ess fee am thev-NITH-yoh pay-ree-ant ar-yan par-OD TRAM-or?)*
*Interesting. And is there a fee for using a cash machine abroad?*

**Swyddog:** Oes, mae ffi o dair punt am bob tynnu arian tramor.
*(oh-ess, my fee oh deyer peent am bob tuhn-ee ar-yan TRAM-or)*
*Yes, there's a fee of three pounds for each withdrawal abroad.*

**Cwsmer:** A beth yw'r gyfradd cyfnewid?
*(ah beth you-r guv-RATH kuv-NEH-wid?)*
*And what's the exchange rate?*

**Swyddog:** Mae'r gyfradd yn newid bob dydd. Gallwch chi weld y gyfradd heddiw ar ein gwefan.
*(my-r guv-RATH un NEH-wid bob deeth. gaLl-ookh khee weld uh guv-RATH heh-thyoo ar eyen GWEV-an)*
*The rate changes every day. You can see today's rate on our website.*

**Cwsmer:** Gwych. Diolch yn fawr am eich egluriad.
*(gweekh. dee-olkh un vow-r am aykh eg-LEER-yad)*
*Great. Thanks very much for your explanation.*

## Post Office - Sending Parcels

### Dialogue 6
**Cwsmer:** Prynhawn da. Hoffwn i anfon parsel i Awstralia os gwelwch yn dda.
*(prin-HOWN dah. hoff-oon ee AN-von PAR-sel ee ow-STRAL-yah oss gwel-ookh un thah)*
*Good afternoon. I would like to send a parcel to Australia please.*

**Swyddog:** Wrth gwrs. Gadewch i fi bwyso'r parsel yn gyntaf.
*(oorth goorss. gad-EH-ookh ee vee BOO-ee-soh-r PAR-sel un gun-tav)*
*Of course. Let me weigh the parcel first.*

**Cwsmer:** Iawn. Faint mae'n pwyso?
*(yown. vah-eent my-n POO-ee-soh?)*
*Okay. How much does it weigh?*

**Swyddog:** Mae'n pwyso un pwynt pump cilogra m. Beth sydd y tu mewn?
*(my-n POO-ee-soh een poo-int peemp kil-OG-ram. beth seeth uh tee meh-oon?)*
*It weighs one point five kilograms. What's inside?*

**Cwsmer:** Llyfrau a dillad yn unig. Dim byd peryglus.
*(LHUV-reye ah DIL-ad un een-ig. dim beed per-UG-liss)*
*Books and clothes only. Nothing dangerous.*

**Swyddog:** Perffaith. Pa ddosbarth hoffech chi? Dosbarth cyntaf neu ail ddosbarth?
*(per-FAH-eeth. pah DOSS-barth hoff-ekh khee? DOSS-barth kun-tav nay ile THOSS-barth?)*
*Perfect. Which class would you like? First class or second class?*

**Cwsmer:** Beth yw'r gwahaniaeth?
*(beth you-r gwah-AN-yeth?)*
*What's the difference?*

**Swyddog:** Dosbarth cyntaf - pum i saith diwrnod. Ail ddosbarth - dau i dri wythnos.
*(DOSS-barth kun-tav - peem ee saith dee-OOR-nod. ile THOSS-barth - dah-ee ee dree WEETH-noss)*
*First class - five to seven days. Second class - two to three weeks.*

**Cwsmer:** Faint yw'r gost?
*(vah-eent you-r gost?)*
*How much is the cost?*

**Swyddog:** Dosbarth cyntaf yw tri deg pump punt. Ail ddosbarth yw ugain punt.
*(DOSS-barth kun-tav you tree deg peemp peent. ile THOSS-barth you ee-gain peent)*
*First class is thirty-five pounds. Second class is twenty pounds.*

**Cwsmer:** Ga i ddosbarth cyntaf os gwelwch yn dda. Hoffwn i dracio'r parsel hefyd.
*(gah ee THOSS-barth kun-tav oss gwel-ookh un thah. hoff-oon ee DRAK-yoh-r PAR-sel heh-vid)*
*Can I have first class please. I'd like to track the parcel too.*

**Swyddog:** Wrth gwrs. Mae tracio wedi'i gynnwys yn y pris.
*(oorth goorss. my DRAK-yoh weh-dee guhn-OOSS uhn uh preess)*
*Of course. Tracking is included in the price.*

**Cwsmer:** Gwych. Oes angen i fi lenwi ffurflen?
*(gweekh. oh-ess ang-en ee vee len-WEE ffirf-len?)*
*Great. Do I need to fill in a form?*

**Swyddog:** Oes, llenwch y ffurflen dollau hon gyda manylion y cynnwys.
*(oh-ess, len-ookh uh ffirf-len DOL-eye hon guh-dah man-IHL-yon uh KUHN-ooss)*
*Yes, fill in this customs form with details of the contents.*

**Cwsmer:** Felly. Ga i yswirio'r parsel hefyd?
*(vel-ee. gah ee uss-WEER-yoh-r PAR-sel heh-vid?)*
*There. Can I insure the parcel too?*

**Swyddog:** Gallwch. Hyd at ba werth hoffech chi yswirio?
*(gaLl-ookh. heed at bah werth hoff-ekh khee uss-WEER-yoh?)*
*You can. Up to what value would you like to insure?*

**Cwsmer:** Can punt os gwelwch yn dda.
*(kant peent oss gwel-ookh un thah)*
*A hundred pounds please.*

**Swyddog:** Iawn. Mae'r yswiriant yn costio pum punt ychwanegol.
*(yown. my-r uss-WEER-yant un koss-tyoh peem peent ukh-wan-EG-ol)*
*Okay. The insurance costs five pounds extra.*

**Cwsmer:** Dim problem. Faint yw'r cyfanswm?
*(dim prob-lem. vah-eent you-r kuh-van-SOOM?)*
*No problem. What's the total?*

**Swyddog:** Y cyfanswm yw deugain punt. Sut hoffech chi dalu?
*(uh kuh-van-SOOM you day-GAIN peent. seet hoff-ekh khee dal-ee?)*
*The total is forty pounds. How would you like to pay?*

**Cwsmer:** Gyda cherdyn os gwelwch yn dda.
*(guh-dah kher-din oss gwel-ookh un thah)*
*With card please.*

**Swyddog:** Perffaith. Dyma'ch derbynneb gyda'r rhif tracio. Bydd y parsel yn gadael heddiw.
*(per-FAH-eeth. duh-makh ther-buhn-eb guh-dar rheev DRAK-yoh. beeth uh PAR-sel un gad-EYE-l heh-thyoo)*
*Perfect. Here's your receipt with the tracking number. The parcel will leave today.*

## Post Office - Buying Stamps

### Dialogue 7
**Cwsmer:** Bore da. Ga i stampiau os gwelwch yn dda?
*(BOH-reh dah. gah ee STAMP-eye oss gwel-ookh un thah?)*
*Good morning. Can I have stamps please?*

**Swyddog:** Wrth gwrs. Pa fath o stampiau?
*(oorth goorss. pah vath oh STAMP-eye?)*
*Of course. What kind of stamps?*

**Cwsmer:** Faint yw stamp dosbarth cyntaf i'r Almaen?
*(vah-eent you stamp DOSS-barth kun-tav eer al-MEYEN?)*
*How much is a first class stamp to Germany?*

**Swyddog:** Un punt tri deg pum ceiniog i Ewrop.
*(een peent tree deg peem kayn-yog ee EH-oo-rop)*
*One pound thirty-five pence to Europe.*

**Cwsmer:** Ga i chwe stamp fel yna os gwelwch yn dda.
*(gah ee khweh stamp vel un-ah oss gwel-ookh un thah)*
*Can I have six stamps like that please.*

**Swyddog:** Wrth gwrs. Hoffech chi unrhyw beth arall?
*(oorth goorss. hoff-ekh khee uhn-rhew beth ar-aLl?)*
*Of course. Would you like anything else?*

**Cwsmer:** Oes, ga i bum stamp dosbarth cyntaf y DU hefyd?
*(oh-ess, gah ee beem stamp DOSS-barth kun-tav uh dee-EE heh-vid?)*
*Yes, can I have five first class UK stamps too?*

**Swyddog:** Wrth gwrs. Ydych chi eisiau stampiau unigol neu fwndel?
*(oorth goorss. uh-deekh khee ay-shah STAMP-eye een-IG-ol nay VOON-del?)*
*Of course. Do you want individual stamps or a book?*

**Cwsmer:** Bwndel os gwelwch yn dda. Mae'n haws.
*(VOON-del oss gwel-ookh un thah. my-n how-ss)*
*A book please. It's easier.*

**Swyddog:** Iawn. Mae bwndel o ddeuddeg yn costio wyth punt tri deg.
*(yown. my VOON-del oh THAY-theg un koss-tyoh weeth peent tree deg)*
*Okay. A book of twelve costs eight pounds thirty.*

**Cwsmer:** Perffaith. Ga i fwndel o'r rheina.
*(per-FAH-eeth. gah ee VOON-del ohr RHAY-nah)*
*Perfect. Can I have a book of those.*

**Swyddog:** Dim problem. Ydych chi eisiau amlenni hefyd?
*(dim prob-lem. uh-deekh khee ay-shah am-LEN-ee heh-vid?)*
*No problem. Do you want envelopes too?*

**Cwsmer:** Oes, ga i ddeg amlen DL os gwelwch yn dda.
*(oh-ess, gah ee theg AM-len dee-EL oss gwel-ookh un thah)*
*Yes, can I have ten DL envelopes please.*

**Swyddog:** Wrth gwrs. Mae rheina'n ddau bunt.
*(oorth goorss. my RHAY-nahn THAH-ee peent)*
*Of course. Those are two pounds.*

**Cwsmer:** Grêt. Faint yw'r cyfanswm i gyd?
*(grayt. vah-eent you-r kuh-van-SOOM ee geed?)*
*Great. What's the total altogether?*

**Swyddog:** Gadewch i fi weld... wyth punt tri deg am y bwndel, wyth punt deg ceiniog am y stampiau Ewrop, a dau bunt am amlenni. Y cyfanswm yw deunaw punt pedwar deg ceiniog.
*(gad-EH-ookh ee vee weld... weeth peent tree deg am uh VOON-del, weeth peent deg kayn-yog am uh STAMP-eye EH-oo-rop, ah dah-ee peent am am-LEN-ee. uh kuh-van-SOOM you day-NAW-oo peent PED-weyer deg kayn-yog)*
*Let me see... eight pounds thirty for the book, eight pounds ten for the Europe stamps, and two pounds for envelopes. The total is eighteen pounds forty pence.*

**Cwsmer:** Iawn. Dyma ugain punt.
*(yown. duh-mah ee-GAIN peent)*
*Okay. Here's twenty pounds.*

**Swyddog:** Diolch. A dyma'ch newid - un punt chwe deg ceiniog.
*(dee-olkh. ah duh-makh new-id - een peent khweh deg kayn-yog)*
*Thanks. And here's your change - one pound sixty pence.*

## Government Office Interactions

### Dialogue 8
**Cwsmer:** Prynhawn da. Dw i yma i gofrestru i bleidleisio.
*(prin-HOWN dah. doo ee uh-mah ee gov-REST-ree ee bleyd-LAYSS-yoh)*
*Good afternoon. I'm here to register to vote.*

**Swyddog:** Prynhawn da. Wrth gwrs, gallwn ni helpu gyda hynny. Ydych chi'n byw yn yr ardal hon?
*(prin-HOWN dah. oorth goorss, gaLl-oon nee hel-pee guh-dah huhn-ee. uh-deekh kheen beew uhn eer AR-dal hon?)*
*Good afternoon. Of course, we can help with that. Do you live in this area?*

**Cwsmer:** Ydw, dw i newydd symud yma fis diwethaf.
*(uh-doo, doo ee NEH-with sum-ID uh-mah veess dee-WETH-av)*
*Yes, I've just moved here last month.*

**Swyddog:** Ardderchog. Oes prawf o gyfeiriad gyda chi?
*(ar-THER-khog. oh-ess prowf oh guv-EYER-yad guh-dah khee?)*
*Excellent. Do you have proof of address?*

**Cwsmer:** Oes, dyma fy mil cyngor diweddaraf.
*(oh-ess, duh-mah vuh veel KUN-gor dee-WETH-ar-av)*
*Yes, here's my latest council tax bill.*

**Swyddog:** Perffaith. Nawr, mae angen i chi lenwi'r ffurflen gofrestru hon.
*(per-FAH-eeth. now-r, my ang-en ee khee len-WEE-r ffirf-len gov-REST-ree hon)*
*Perfect. Now, you need to fill in this registration form.*

**Cwsmer:** Iawn. Oes rhaid i fi ei gwblhau fan hyn?
*(yown. oh-ess rhide ee vee ay goo-bl-HEYE van heen?)*
*Okay. Do I have to complete it here?*

**Swyddog:** Na, gallwch chi fynd â hi adre a'i hanfon yn ôl.
*(nah, gaLl-ookh khee veend ah hee AD-reh ah han-VON uhn ohl)*
*No, you can take it home and send it back.*

**Cwsmer:** Faint o amser sydd gen i i'w hanfon?
*(vah-eent oh AM-ser seeth gen ee eew han-VON?)*
*How much time do I have to send it?*

**Swyddog:** Mae angen ei hanfon o fewn pythefnos.
*(my ang-en ay han-VON oh veh-oon puth-EV-noss)*
*You need to send it within two weeks.*

**Cwsmer:** Perffaith. A pryd fydd fy enw ar y gofrestr?
*(per-FAH-eeth. ah preed veeth vuh EN-oo ar uh gov-REST-r?)*
*Perfect. And when will my name be on the register?*

**Swyddog:** Bydd eich enw'n cael ei ychwanegu o fewn mis ar ôl i ni dderbyn y ffurflen.
*(beeth aykh EN-oo-n kah-el ay ukh-wan-EG-ee oh veh-oon meess ar ohl ee nee ther-BEEN uh ffirf-len)*
*Your name will be added within a month after we receive the form.*

**Cwsmer:** Gwych. Oes unrhyw beth arall mae angen i fi wybod?
*(gweekh. oh-ess uhn-rhew beth ar-aLl my ang-en ee vee GWUH-bod?)*
*Great. Is there anything else I need to know?*

**Swyddog:** Byddwch chi'n derbyn cerdyn pleidleisiwr trwy'r post.
*(beeth-ookh kheen der-BEEN ker-din bleyd-LAYSS-yoor troo-r post)*
*You'll receive a polling card through the post.*

**Cwsmer:** Diolch yn fawr iawn am eich help.
*(dee-olkh un vow-r yown am aykh help)*
*Thanks very much indeed for your help.*

## Practice Tips

- Practice formal language - official contexts require polite forms
- Learn numbers thoroughly - essential for banking and postal services
- Familiarize yourself with common forms and documents
- Practice asking for clarification if you don't understand
- Remember mutations after "eich" (your), "fy" (my), etc.
- Learn banking terminology in both Welsh and English
- Practice writing your address in Welsh
- Get comfortable with Welsh currency amounts
- Role-play scenarios with a partner
- Visit Welsh-speaking banks and post offices for real practice
