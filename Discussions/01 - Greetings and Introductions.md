# Greetings and Introductions

## Basic Greetings

### Dialogue 1
**Person A:** Bore da! Sut wyt ti?
*(BOH-reh dah! Seet oo-it tee?)*
*Good morning! How are you?*

**Person B:** Bore da! Dw i'n iawn, diolch. A ti?
*(BOH-reh dah! Doo een YOW-n, DEE-olkh. Ah tee?)*
*Good morning! I'm fine, thanks. And you?*

**Person A:** Da iawn, diolch!
*(Dah YOW-n, DEE-olkh!)*
*Very good, thanks!*

---

### Dialogue 2
**Person A:** Prynhawn da! Sut mae?
*(PRIN-how-n dah! Seet MY?)*
*Good afternoon! How are things?*

**Person B:** Prynhawn da! Dw i'n dda, diolch yn fawr.
*(PRIN-how-n dah! Doo een thah, DEE-olkh un VOW-r)*
*Good afternoon! I'm good, thanks very much.*

---

### Dialogue 3
**Person A:** Noswaith dda!
*(NOS-waith THAH!)*
*Good evening!*

**Person B:** Noswaith dda! Sut wyt ti heddiw?
*(NOS-waith THAH! Seet oo-it tee HEH-thee-oo?)*
*Good evening! How are you today?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n wych, diolch!
*(Doo een OO-eekh, DEE-olkh!)*
*I'm great, thanks!*

---

## Meeting Someone New

### Dialogue 4
**Person A:** Helo! Beth ydy dy enw di?
*(HEL-oh! Behth UH-dee duh EN-oo dee?)*
*Hello! What is your name?*

**Person B:** Helo! [Your name] dw i. Beth ydy dy enw di?
*(HEL-oh! [name] doo ee. Behth UH-dee duh EN-oo dee?)*
*Hello! I'm [Your name]. What is your name?*

**Person A:** [Partner's name] dw i. Neis cwrdd â ti!
*([name] doo ee. NICE KOO-rdd ah tee!)*
*I'm [Partner's name]. Nice to meet you!*

**Person B:** Neis cwrdd â ti hefyd!
*(NICE KOO-rdd ah tee HEV-id!)*
*Nice to meet you too!*

---

### Dialogue 5
**Person A:** O ble wyt ti'n dod?
*(Oh bleh oo-it teen dohd?)*
*Where do you come from?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n dod o [place]. A ti?
*(Doo een dohd oh [place]. Ah tee?)*
*I come from [place]. And you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n dod o [place].
*(Doo een dohd oh [place])*
*I come from [place].*

---

### Dialogue 6
**Person A:** Ble wyt ti'n byw nawr?
*(Bleh oo-it teen bew NOW-r?)*
*Where do you live now?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n byw yn [place]. Wyt ti'n byw yn lleol?
*(Doo een bew un [place]. Oo-it teen bew un KHLEH-ol?)*
*I live in [place]. Do you live locally?*

**Person A:** Ydw / Nac ydw, dw i'n byw yn [place].
*(UH-doo / Nak UH-doo, doo een bew un [place])*
*Yes / No, I live in [place].*

---

## Farewells

### Dialogue 7
**Person A:** Rhaid i fi fynd nawr.
*(RHYD ee vee vind NOW-r)*
*I have to go now.*

**Person B:** Iawn, hwyl fawr!
*(YOW-n, HOO-il VOW-r!)*
*Okay, goodbye!*

**Person A:** Hwyl! Wela i di eto!
*(HOO-il! WEL-ah ee dee ET-oh!)*
*Bye! I'll see you again!*

---

### Dialogue 8
**Person A:** Diolch am y sgwrs!
*(DEE-olkh am uh SGOO-ars!)*
*Thanks for the chat!*

**Person B:** Croeso! Mwynha dy ddiwrnod!
*(KROY-soh! MWIN-hah duh THEE-oor-nod!)*
*Welcome! Enjoy your day!*

**Person A:** A ti hefyd!
*(Ah tee HEV-id!)*
*And you too!*

---

## Practice Tips
- Take turns being Person A and Person B
- Fill in your own information for places and names
- Try varying the speed and tone
- Practice both formal (chi) and informal (ti) versions
- The "ti" (you) sound is like "tee"
- "Dw i" (I am) sounds like "doo ee"
- "ch" in Welsh is pronounced like the Scottish "loch" or German "Bach"
- "dd" is pronounced like "th" in "the"
- "ll" is a unique Welsh sound - put tongue in "L" position and blow
