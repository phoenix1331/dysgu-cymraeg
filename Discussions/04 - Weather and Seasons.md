# Weather and Seasons

## Daily Weather Chat

**Person A:** Sut mae'r tywydd heddiw?
*(seet my-r TUH-with HEH-thee-oo?)*
*How is the weather today?*

**Person B:** Mae'n oer iawn! Mae'n bwrw glaw hefyd. Sut mae'r tywydd lle wyt ti?
*(mine OY-er YOWN! mine BOO-roo GLOW HEH-vid. seet my-r TUH-with LLeh weet tee?)*
*It's very cold! It's raining too. How is the weather where you are?*

**Person A:** Mae'n wyntog iawn yma.
*(mine WIN-tog YOWN UH-mah)*
*It's very windy here.*

---

**Person A:** Ydy hi'n braf heddiw?
*(UH-dee heen BRAV HEH-thee-oo?)*
*Is it nice today?*

**Person B:** Nac ydy, mae'n gymylog. Beth am ddoe?
*(nak UH-dee, mine gum-UH-log. beth am THOH-eh?)*
*No, it's cloudy. What about yesterday?*

**Person A:** Roedd hi'n heulog iawn ddoe!
*(ROY-th heen HAY-log YOWN THOH-eh!)*
*It was very sunny yesterday!*

---

**Person A:** Beth ydy'r rhagolygon tywydd?
*(beth UH-deer hrag-ol-UH-gon TUH-with?)*
*What is the weather forecast?*

**Person B:** Mae'n mynd i fwrw glaw trwy'r wythnos.
*(mine meend ee VOO-roo GLOW TROO-eer WEETH-noss)*
*It's going to rain through the week.*

**Person A:** O na! Roeddwn i eisiau mynd am dro.
*(oh NAH! roy-THOON ee AY-shah meend am DROH)*
*Oh no! I wanted to go for a walk.*

## Talking About Seasons

**Person A:** Pa dymor wyt ti'n hoffi orau?
*(pah DUH-mor weet teen HOFF-ee OR-eye?)*
*Which season do you like best?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n caru'r haf! Mae'n gynnes ac mae'r dyddiau'n hir. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een KAR-eer HAV! mine GUN-ess ak my-r DUTH-yeye-een HEER. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I love summer! It's warm and the days are long. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n hoffi'r hydref. Dw i'n caru'r lliwiau.
*(doo een HOFF-eer HUH-drev. doo een KAR-eer LLee-WEE-eye)*
*I like autumn. I love the colours.*

---

**Person A:** Sut mae'r gaeaf yma fel arfer?
*(seet my-r GY-av UH-mah vel AR-ver?)*
*What is winter like here usually?*

**Person B:** Mae'n oer iawn ac weithiau mae'n bwrw eira. Wyt ti'n hoffi eira?
*(mine OY-er YOWN ak way-THEE-eye mine BOO-roo AY-rah. weet teen HOFF-ee AY-rah?)*
*It's very cold and sometimes it snows. Do you like snow?*

**Person A:** Ydw, mae'n hardd, ond mae'n oer iawn!
*(UH-doo, mine HARTH, ond mine OY-er YOWN!)*
*Yes, it's beautiful, but it's very cold!*

---

**Person A:** Pryd mae'r gwanwyn yn dechrau yma?
*(preed my-r GWAN-win un dekh-RY-ee UH-mah?)*
*When does spring start here?*

**Person B:** Fel arfer, tua mis Mawrth. Mae'r blodau'n dechrau tyfu.
*(vel AR-ver, tee-ah meess MOWRTH. my-r BLOD-eye-een dekh-RY-ee TUH-vee)*
*Usually, around March. The flowers start to grow.*

**Person A:** Mae hynny'n hyfryd!
*(my HUN-een HUV-rid!)*
*That's lovely!*

## Weather Activities

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n hoffi gwneud pan mae'n braf?
*(beth weet teen HOFF-ee GOO-nayd pan mine BRAV?)*
*What do you like to do when it's nice?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n hoffi mynd i'r traeth neu fynd am dro. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een HOFF-ee meend eer TRYTH nay veend am DROH. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I like to go to the beach or go for a walk. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n hoffi cerdded yn y mynyddoedd.
*(doo een HOFF-ee KER-thed un uh mun-UH-thoy-th)*
*I like to walk in the mountains.*

---

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n gwneud pan mae'n bwrw glaw?
*(beth weet teen GOO-nayd pan mine BOO-roo GLOW?)*
*What do you do when it's raining?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n aros yn y tŷ ac yn darllen llyfr. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een AR-oss un uh TEE ak un DAR-LLen LLU-veer. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I stay in the house and read a book. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n gwylio ffilmiau neu'n coginio.
*(doo een GWIL-yoh FFIL-mee-eye nay-oon kog-IN-yoh)*
*I watch films or cook.*

---

**Person A:** Ydy hi'n rhy oer i fynd allan?
*(UH-dee heen rhee OY-er ee veend aLl-AN?)*
*Is it too cold to go out?*

**Person B:** Ydy, mae'n rhewi! Gawn ni aros yn y tŷ?
*(UH-dee, mine RHEH-wee! GOWN nee AR-oss un uh TEE?)*
*Yes, it's freezing! Can we stay in the house?*

**Person A:** Syniad da. Wyt ti eisiau paned?
*(SUN-yad dah. weet tee AY-shah PAN-ed?)*
*Good idea. Do you want a cuppa?*

## Extreme Weather

**Person A:** Glywes di am y storm neithiwr?
*(GLOO-wess dee am uh STORM nay-THEE-oor?)*
*Did you hear about the storm last night?*

**Person B:** Do! Roedd hi'n ofnadwy! Oedd popeth yn iawn gyda ti?
*(DOH! ROY-th heen ov-NAD-wee! OY-th POP-eth un YOWN GUH-dah tee?)*
*Yes! It was terrible! Was everything okay with you?*

**Person A:** Oedd, ond roedd y gwynt yn uchel iawn.
*(OY-th, ond ROY-th uh GWEENT un EE-khel YOWN)*
*Yes, but the wind was very high.*

---

**Person A:** Mae hi'n boeth iawn heddiw!
*(my heen BOY-eth YOWN HEH-thee-oo!)*
*It's very hot today!*

**Person B:** Ydy, mae'n tanbaid! Rhaid i ni yfed llawer o ddŵr.
*(UH-dee, mine tan-BEYED! RHEID ee nee UH-ved LLow-ER oh THOOR)*
*Yes, it's scorching! We must drink lots of water.*

**Person A:** Wyt ti eisiau mynd i'r pwll nofio?
*(weet tee AY-shah meend eer POOLl nov-YOH?)*
*Do you want to go to the swimming pool?*

**Person B:** Syniad gwych!
*(SUN-yad GWEEKH!)*
*Excellent idea!*

## Practice Tips
- Look at actual weather and describe it
- Practice all weather vocabulary
- Discuss what you like to do in different weather
- Use mutations with "yn" (yn oer, yn gynnes, yn braf)
