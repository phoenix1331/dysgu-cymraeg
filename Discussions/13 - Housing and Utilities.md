# Housing and Utilities

> [!info] Home Life in Welsh
> Essential dialogues for dealing with housing, utilities, landlords, and maintenance issues. These conversations will help you navigate renting, setting up services, and handling household matters in Welsh.

## Viewing a Property to Rent

### Dialogue 1
**Ymwelydd:** Prynhawn da. Dw i yma i weld yr eiddo i'w rentu.
*(prin-HOWN dah. doo ee uh-mah ee weld eer AYD-oh eew REN-tee)*
*Good afternoon. I'm here to view the property to rent.*

**Asiant:** Prynhawn da! Wrth gwrs. Croeso. Dewch i mewn.
*(prin-HOWN dah! oorth goorss. KROY-soh. deh-ookh ee meh-oon)*
*Good afternoon! Of course. Welcome. Come in.*

**Ymwelydd:** Diolch. Tŷ teras hyfryd! Faint o ystafelloedd gwely sydd?
*(dee-olkh. tee TER-ass huhv-RID! vah-eent oh uss-tav-EL-oy-th WEL-ee seeth?)*
*Thanks. Lovely terraced house! How many bedrooms are there?*

**Asiant:** Mae tair ystafell wely. Dyma'r ystafell fyw a'r gegin ar y llawr gwaelod.
*(my teyer uss-TAV-eLl WEL-ee. duh-mar uss-TAV-eLl VEW ahr GEG-in ar uh lhow-r GWEY-lod)*
*There are three bedrooms. Here's the living room and kitchen on the ground floor.*

**Ymwelydd:** Mae'r gegin yn eang iawn. Ydy hi wedi'i gosod yn llawn?
*(my-r GEG-in uhn eh-ANG yown. uh-dee hee weh-dee GOSS-od uhn lhown?)*
*The kitchen is very spacious. Is it fully fitted?*

**Asiant:** Ydy. Mae popeth yma - oergell, rhewgell, popty, a peiriant golchi llestri.
*(uh-dee. my POP-eth uh-mah - OY-er-geLl, RHEH-oo-geLl, POP-tee, ah pay-ree-ant GOL-khee LHESS-tree)*
*Yes. Everything is here - fridge, freezer, oven, and dishwasher.*

**Ymwelydd:** Gwych. A beth am beiriant golchi dillad?
*(gweekh. ah beth am pay-ree-ant GOL-khee DIL-ad?)*
*Great. And what about a washing machine?*

**Asiant:** Mae'r peiriant golchi yn yr ystafell ymolchi i fyny'r grisiau.
*(my-r pay-ree-ant GOL-khee uhn eer uss-TAV-eLl um-OL-khee ee VUN-eer GRISH-eye)*
*The washing machine is in the bathroom upstairs.*

**Ymwelydd:** Iawn. Ga i weld yr ystafelloedd gwely?
*(yown. gah ee weld eer uss-tav-EL-oy-th WEL-ee?)*
*Okay. Can I see the bedrooms?*

**Asiant:** Wrth gwrs. Dilynwch fi i fyny. Dyma'r brif ystafell wely.
*(oorth goorss. dil-UN-ookh vee ee VUN-ee. duh-mar BREEV uss-TAV-eLl WEL-ee)*
*Of course. Follow me upstairs. Here's the master bedroom.*

**Ymwelydd:** Mae hi'n fawr. Oes cwpwrdd dillad?
*(my heen vow-r. oh-ess KOO-poorth DIL-ad?)*
*It's big. Is there a wardrobe?*

**Asiant:** Oes, mae cwpwrdd dillad adeiledig ar hyd un wal.
*(oh-ess, my KOO-poorth DIL-ad ad-AYL-ed-ig ar heed een wal)*
*Yes, there's a built-in wardrobe along one wall.*

**Ymwelydd:** Perffaith. A'r ystafelloedd eraill?
*(per-FAH-eeth. ahr uss-tav-EL-oy-th er-ILE?)*
*Perfect. And the other bedrooms?*

**Asiant:** Mae dwy ystafell wely ddwbl arall, a'r ystafell ymolchi draw fan hyn.
*(my doo-ee uss-TAV-eLl WEL-ee THOO-bl ar-aLl, ahr uss-TAV-eLl um-OL-khee draw van heen)*
*There are two other double bedrooms, and the bathroom is over here.*

**Ymwelydd:** Ydy'r gwresogi canolog yn gweithio'n dda?
*(uh-deer gwress-OG-ee kan-OL-og uhn GAYTH-yohn THAH?)*
*Does the central heating work well?*

**Asiant:** Ydy, mae boeler newydd sbon wedi'i osod llynedd.
*(uh-dee, my BOY-ler NEH-with SBON weh-dee OSS-od lhuhn-eth)*
*Yes, a brand new boiler was installed last year.*

**Ymwelydd:** Ardderchog. Oes gardd?
*(ar-THER-khog. oh-ess GARTH?)*
*Excellent. Is there a garden?*

**Asiant:** Oes, mae gardd gefn fach gyda patio. Gadewch i ni fynd i weld.
*(oh-ess, my GARTH GEV-n vakh guh-dah PAT-yoh. gad-EH-ookh ee nee veend ee weld)*
*Yes, there's a small back garden with a patio. Let's go and see.*

**Ymwelydd:** Hyfryd! Mae'n berffaith ar gyfer yr haf.
*(huhv-RID! my-n per-FAH-eeth ar guh-ver eer hav)*
*Lovely! It's perfect for the summer.*

**Asiant:** Ydy wir. Oes diddordeb gyda chi?
*(uh-dee weer. oh-ess dith-OR-deb guh-dah khee?)*
*Indeed. Are you interested?*

**Ymwelydd:** Oes, diddordeb mawr. Beth yw'r cam nesaf?
*(oh-ess, dith-OR-deb mow-r. beth you-r kam NESS-av?)*
*Yes, very interested. What's the next step?*

### Dialogue 2
**Ymwelydd:** Oes unrhyw broblemau gyda'r tŷ y dylwn i wybod amdanyn nhw?
*(oh-ess uhn-rhew PROB-lem-eye guh-dar tee uh DULL-oon ee GWUH-bod am-DAN-in hoo?)*
*Are there any problems with the house I should know about?*

**Asiant:** Dim problemau mawr. Weithiau mae'r draen yn y gegin yn arafu ychydig.
*(dim prob-LEM-eye mow-r. WAYTH-eye my-r dreyen uhn uh GEG-in uhn ar-AV-ee ukh-ID-ig)*
*No major problems. Sometimes the drain in the kitchen slows down a little.*

**Ymwelydd:** Iawn. A phwy sy'n gyfrifol am drwsio pethau fel yna?
*(yown. ah phew seen guv-RIV-ol am DROO-see-oh PETH-eye vel un-ah?)*
*Okay. And who's responsible for fixing things like that?*

**Asiant:** Y landlord sy'n gyfrifol am bob atgyweiriad a chynnal a chadw.
*(uh LAND-lord seen guv-RIV-ol am bob at-guh-WEYER-yad ah KHUHN-aLl ah KAD-oo)*
*The landlord is responsible for all repairs and maintenance.*

**Ymwelydd:** Da iawn. Ydy anifeiliaid anwes yn cael eu caniatáu?
*(dah yown. uh-dee an-iv-AYL-yed AN-wess uhn kah-el ay kan-yat-EYE-oh?)*
*Very good. Are pets allowed?*

**Asiant:** Mae'n dibynnu. Pa fath o anifail sydd gyda chi?
*(my-n dib-UHN-ee. pah vath oh an-IV-eyel seeth guh-dah khee?)*
*It depends. What kind of animal do you have?*

**Ymwelydd:** Cath fach. Mae hi'n dawel iawn.
*(kath vakh. my heen DOW-el yown)*
*A small cat. She's very quiet.*

**Asiant:** Dylai hynny fod yn iawn. Bydd angen i chi ofyn i'r landlord yn swyddogol.
*(dull-eye huhn-ee vod uhn yown. beeth ang-en ee khee OV-in eer LAND-lord uhn sooth-OG-ol)*
*That should be okay. You'll need to ask the landlord officially.*

**Ymwelydd:** Perffaith. Oes lle parcio?
*(per-FAH-eeth. oh-ess lheh PAR-kyoh?)*
*Perfect. Is there parking space?*

**Asiant:** Oes, mae lle parcio ar y stryd y tu allan. Mae'n rhad ac am ddim.
*(oh-ess, my lheh PAR-kyoh ar uh streed uh tee AL-an. my-n rhad ak am thim)*
*Yes, there's parking on the street outside. It's free.*

**Ymwelydd:** Gwych. Pryd fyddai'n bosib symud i mewn?
*(gweekh. preed VUH-theye-n BOSS-ib sum-ID ee meh-oon?)*
*Great. When would it be possible to move in?*

**Asiant:** Mae'r tenant presennol yn gadael ddiwedd y mis hwn. Gallech chi symud i mewn o'r cyntaf o'r mis nesaf.
*(my-r TEN-ant press-EN-ol uhn gad-EYE-l thee-WEH-th uh meess hoon. gaLl-ekh khee sum-ID ee meh-oon ohr KUN-tav ohr meess NESS-av)*
*The current tenant is leaving at the end of this month. You could move in from the first of next month.*

## Discussing Rent and Deposit

### Dialogue 3
**Ymwelydd:** Faint yw'r rhent bob mis?
*(vah-eent you-r rhent bob meess?)*
*How much is the rent per month?*

**Asiant:** Wyth gant pum deg pump o bunnoedd y mis.
*(weeth gant peem deg peemp oh PIHN-oy-th uh meess)*
*Eight hundred and fifty-five pounds per month.*

**Ymwelydd:** Ydy'r biliau wedi'u cynnwys?
*(uh-deer BIL-eye weh-deew KUHN-ooss?)*
*Are the bills included?*

**Asiant:** Nac ydyn, mae'r biliau ar wahân. Rhaid i chi dalu am drydan, nwy, dŵr, a'r dreth gyngor.
*(nak uh-deen, my-r BIL-eye ar WAH-han. rhide ee khee dal-ee am TRUD-an, noo-ee, DOOR, ahr dreth GUN-gor)*
*No, the bills are separate. You have to pay for electricity, gas, water, and council tax.*

**Ymwelydd:** Iawn. Tua faint fydd y biliau bob mis?
*(yown. tee-ah vah-eent veeth uh BIL-eye bob meess?)*
*Okay. About how much will the bills be per month?*

**Asiant:** Tua can i gant pum deg punt y mis, yn dibynnu ar eich defnydd.
*(tee-ah kant ee gant peem deg peent uh meess, uhn dib-UHN-ee ar aykh DEV-nith)*
*About one hundred to one hundred and fifty pounds per month, depending on your usage.*

**Ymwelydd:** A faint yw'r blaendal?
*(ah vah-eent you-r BLEYEN-dal?)*
*And how much is the deposit?*

**Asiant:** Mae'r blaendal yn hafal i bum wythnos o rent. Felly, mil ac wyth gant o bunnoedd.
*(my-r BLEYEN-dal uhn HAV-al ee beem WEETH-noss oh rent. vel-ee, meel ak weeth gant oh PIHN-oy-th)*
*The deposit is equal to five weeks' rent. So, one thousand eight hundred pounds.*

**Ymwelydd:** Iawn. Pryd mae angen talu'r blaendal?
*(yown. preed my ang-en TAL-eer BLEYEN-dal?)*
*Okay. When does the deposit need to be paid?*

**Asiant:** Mae angen talu'r blaendal cyn symud i mewn. Bydd yn cael ei gadw mewn cynllun diogelu blaendal.
*(my ang-en TAL-eer BLEYEN-dal keen sum-ID ee meh-oon. beeth uhn kah-el ay GAD-oo meh-oon KUHN-lheen dee-OG-el-ee BLEYEN-dal)*
*The deposit needs to be paid before moving in. It will be kept in a deposit protection scheme.*

**Ymwelydd:** Da iawn. Pa mor hir yw'r cytundeb?
*(dah yown. pah mor heer you-r kuh-TIHN-deb?)*
*Very good. How long is the contract?*

**Asiant:** Mae'r cytundeb yn gychwynnol am chwe mis, ac yna'n mynd yn fisol.
*(my-r kuh-TIHN-deb uhn gukh-WIN-ol am khweh meess, ak UN-ahn mind uhn VISS-ol)*
*The contract is initially for six months, and then goes monthly.*

**Ymwelydd:** Beth sy'n digwydd os dw i eisiau gadael cyn diwedd y cytundeb?
*(beth seen DIG-with oss doo ee ay-shah gad-EYE-l keen dee-WEH-th uh kuh-TIHN-deb?)*
*What happens if I want to leave before the end of the contract?*

**Asiant:** Mae angen rhoi mis o rybudd ysgrifenedig i'r landlord.
*(my ang-en rhoy meess oh RHUH-bith uss-griv-en-ED-ig eer LAND-lord)*
*You need to give one month's written notice to the landlord.*

**Ymwelydd:** Perffaith. Oes angen gwneud archwiliad cyn symud i mewn?
*(per-FAH-eeth. oh-ess ang-en oo-nay-d arkh-WIL-yad keen sum-ID ee meh-oon?)*
*Perfect. Do we need to do an inspection before moving in?*

**Asiant:** Oes, byddwn ni'n gwneud archwiliad manwl a chymryd lluniau. Byddwch chi'n cael copi o'r adroddiad.
*(oh-ess, beeth-oon neen oo-nay-d arkh-WIL-yad MAN-ool ah KHUM-rid LHIN-eye. beeth-ookh kheen kah-el KOP-ee ohr ad-ROTH-yad)*
*Yes, we'll do a detailed inspection and take photos. You'll get a copy of the report.*

**Ymwelydd:** Ardderchog. Pryd gallwn ni arwyddo'r cytundeb?
*(ar-THER-khog. preed gaLl-oon nee ar-WEETH-oh-r kuh-TIHN-deb?)*
*Excellent. When can we sign the contract?*

**Asiant:** Os ydych chi'n hapus, gallwn ni wneud y gwaith papur yfory.
*(oss uh-deekh kheen HAP-iss, gaLl-oon nee oo-nay-d uh gweyeth PAP-eer uh-VOR-ee)*
*If you're happy, we can do the paperwork tomorrow.*

## Setting Up Electricity and Gas

### Dialogue 4
**Cwsmer:** Bore da. Dw i wedi symud i dŷ newydd ac mae angen i fi gychwyn cyflenwad trydan a nwy.
*(BOH-reh dah. doo ee weh-dee sum-ID ee dee NEH-with ak my ang-en ee vee GUKH-win kuv-LEN-wad TRUD-an ah noo-ee)*
*Good morning. I've moved to a new house and I need to start electricity and gas supply.*

**Swyddog:** Bore da! Wrth gwrs. Beth yw'r cyfeiriad?
*(BOH-reh dah! oorth goorss. beth you-r kuv-EYER-yad?)*
*Good morning! Of course. What's the address?*

**Cwsmer:** Rhif pump deg tri, Stryd y Parc, Caerdydd, CF10 2AB.
*(rheev peem deg tree, STREED uh PARK, KEYER-deeth, kee-ef-deg-dah-ee-bee)*
*Number fifty-three, Park Street, Cardiff, CF10 2AB.*

**Swyddog:** Diolch. Pryd symudoch chi i mewn?
*(dee-olkh. preed sum-ID-okh khee ee meh-oon?)*
*Thanks. When did you move in?*

**Cwsmer:** Ddoe. Dw i eisiau cychwyn y cyflenwad o heddiw ymlaen.
*(THOY. doo ee ay-shah GUKH-win uh kuv-LEN-wad oh heh-thyoo um-LEYEN)*
*Yesterday. I want to start the supply from today onwards.*

**Swyddog:** Dim problem. Ydych chi'n gwybod rhifau'r mesuryddion?
*(dim prob-lem. uh-deekh kheen GWUH-bod RHIV-eye-r mess-eer-EETH-yon?)*
*No problem. Do you know the meter numbers?*

**Cwsmer:** Ydw, gadewch i fi weld. Rhif y mesurydd trydan yw K6789234, a'r nwy yw G4567890.
*(uh-doo, gad-EH-ookh ee vee weld. rheev uh mess-EER-ith TRUD-an you kay-chweh-saith-weeth-naw-dah-ee-tri-ped-weyer, ahr noo-ee you jee-ped-weyer-pump-chweh-saith-weeth-naw-sero)*
*Yes, let me see. The electricity meter number is K6789234, and the gas is G4567890.*

**Swyddog:** Perffaith. Allwch chi roi darlleniad y mesuryddion i fi?
*(per-FAH-eeth. aLl-ookh khee rhoy dar-LHEN-yad uh mess-eer-EETH-yon ee vee?)*
*Perfect. Can you give me the meter readings?*

**Cwsmer:** Gallaf. Y trydan yw tri dim pedwar pump chwech, a'r nwy yw un wyth saith dau tri.
*(gaLl-av. uh TRUD-an you tree dim PED-weyer peemp khwekh, ahr noo-ee you een weeth saith dah-ee tree)*
*I can. The electricity is three zero four five six, and the gas is one eight seven two three.*

**Swyddog:** Diolch yn fawr. Pa gynllun tariff hoffech chi?
*(dee-olkh un vow-r. pah GUHN-lheen TAR-if hoff-ekh khee?)*
*Thanks very much. Which tariff plan would you like?*

**Cwsmer:** Pa opsiynau sydd ar gael?
*(pah op-SEE-un-eye seeth ar gah-el?)*
*What options are available?*

**Swyddog:** Mae gennym ni gyfradd sefydlog neu gyfradd newidiol. Mae'r gyfradd sefydlog yn dri deg ceiniog yr uned, wedi'i gloi am ddwy flynedd.
*(my gen-im nee guv-RATH sev-UD-log nay guv-RATH new-ID-yol. my-r guv-RATH sev-UD-log uhn tree deg kayn-yog eer EEN-ed, weh-dee gloy am thoo-ee VLUHN-eth)*
*We have a fixed rate or variable rate. The fixed rate is thirty pence per unit, locked in for two years.*

**Cwsmer:** A beth am y gyfradd newidiol?
*(ah beth am uh guv-RATH new-ID-yol?)*
*And what about the variable rate?*

**Swyddog:** Mae'r gyfradd newidiol yn wyth punt ar hugain yr uned ar hyn o bryd, ond gall newid bob chwarter.
*(my-r guv-RATH new-ID-yol uhn weeth peent ar HEE-gain eer EEN-ed ar heen oh breed, ond gaLl new-id bob KHWAR-ter)*
*The variable rate is twenty-eight pence per unit at the moment, but can change every quarter.*

**Cwsmer:** Dw i'n meddwl bod y gyfradd sefydlog yn well. Pa mor hir mae'n cymryd i gychwyn?
*(doo een METH-il bod uh guv-RATH sev-UD-log uhn welh. pah mor heer my-n KUM-rid ee GUKH-win?)*
*I think the fixed rate is better. How long does it take to start?*

**Swyddog:** Bydd y cyflenwad yn cael ei gychwyn o fewn dau ddiwrnod gwaith.
*(beeth uh kuv-LEN-wad uhn kah-el ay GUKH-win oh veh-oon dah-ee thee-OOR-nod gweyeth)*
*The supply will be started within two working days.*

**Cwsmer:** Gwych. Sut ydw i'n talu'r biliau?
*(gweekh. seet uh-doo een TAL-eer BIL-eye?)*
*Great. How do I pay the bills?*

**Swyddog:** Gallwch chi dalu trwy ddebyd uniongyrchol, ar-lein, neu drwy'r ffôn.
*(gaLl-ookh khee dal-ee troo-ee THEB-id een-yon-GUHR-khol, ar-LEYEN, nay troo-eer ffohn)*
*You can pay by direct debit, online, or by phone.*

**Cwsmer:** Perffaith. Hoffwn i drefnu debyd uniongyrchol.
*(per-FAH-eeth. hoff-oon ee TREV-nee DEB-id een-yon-GUHR-khol)*
*Perfect. I'd like to set up direct debit.*

**Swyddog:** Ardderchog. Bydd angen eich manylion banc arnaf i.
*(ar-THER-khog. beeth ang-en aykh man-IHL-yon bank AR-nav ee)*
*Excellent. I'll need your bank details.*

## Setting Up Internet and Phone

### Dialogue 5
**Cwsmer:** Prynhawn da. Hoffwn i gysylltu band eang a llinell ffôn yn fy nghartref newydd.
*(prin-HOWN dah. hoff-oon ee guh-SUHL-tee band eh-ANG ah LHIN-eLl ffohn uhn vuh NKHAR-trev NEH-with)*
*Good afternoon. I would like to connect broadband and a phone line in my new home.*

**Swyddog:** Prynhawn da! Wrth gwrs. Beth yw'ch cyfeiriad newydd?
*(prin-HOWN dah! oorth goorss. beth you-kh kuv-EYER-yad NEH-with?)*
*Good afternoon! Of course. What's your new address?*

**Cwsmer:** Fflat dau, pedwar deg wyth, Heol y Gadeirlan, Abertawe, SA1 3XY.
*(fflat dah-ee, PED-weyer deg weeth, HEH-ol uh gad-EYER-lan, ab-er-TOW-eh, ess-ay-een-tri-eks-wey)*
*Flat two, forty-eight, Cathedral Road, Swansea, SA1 3XY.*

**Swyddog:** Diolch. Oes llinell ffôn eisoes yn yr eiddo?
*(dee-olkh. oh-ess LHIN-eLl ffohn ay-SOY-ss uhn eer AYD-oh?)*
*Thanks. Is there already a phone line in the property?*

**Cwsmer:** Oes, dw i'n meddwl. Sut allaf i wirio?
*(oh-ess, doo een METH-il. seet aLl-av ee WEER-yoh?)*
*Yes, I think so. How can I check?*

**Swyddog:** Edrychwch am soced ffôn ar y wal. Fel arfer, mae'n wyn neu'n hufen.
*(ed-RUKH-ookh am SOK-ed ffohn ar uh wal. vel AR-ver, my-n oo-een nay-n HEE-ven)*
*Look for a phone socket on the wall. Usually, it's white or cream.*

**Cwsmer:** O ie, dw i'n ei weld. Mae un yn yr ystafell fyw.
*(oh yeh, doo een ay weld. my een uhn eer uss-TAV-eLl VEW)*
*Oh yes, I see it. There's one in the living room.*

**Swyddog:** Perffaith. Felly, dim angen gosod llinell newydd. Pa gyflymder band eang hoffech chi?
*(per-FAH-eeth. vel-ee, dim ang-en GOSS-od LHIN-eLl NEH-with. pah guv-LUHM-der band eh-ANG hoff-ekh khee?)*
*Perfect. So, no need to install a new line. What broadband speed would you like?*

**Cwsmer:** Beth sydd ar gael?
*(beth seeth ar gah-el?)*
*What's available?*

**Swyddog:** Mae gennym ni becynnau ar gael o hanner cant megabeit yr eiliad i naw cant megabeit yr eiliad.
*(my gen-im nee bek-UHN-eye ar gah-el oh HAN-er kant MEG-a-byte eer AYL-yad ee nah-oo gant MEG-a-byte eer AYL-yad)*
*We have packages available from fifty megabits per second to nine hundred megabits per second.*

**Cwsmer:** Beth fyddech chi'n argymell ar gyfer dau berson sy'n gweithio o gartref?
*(beth VUH-thekh kheen ar-GUM-eLl ar guh-ver dah-ee PER-son seen GAYTH-yoh oh GAR-trev?)*
*What would you recommend for two people working from home?*

**Swyddog:** Byddwn i'n argymell o leiaf dau gant megabeit yr eiliad. Mae hynny'n ddigon ar gyfer fideo-gynadleddau ac ati.
*(beeth-oon een ar-GUM-eLl oh LAY-av dah-ee gant MEG-a-byte eer AYL-yad. my huhn-een THIG-on ar guh-ver VID-eh-oh-gun-ad-LETH-eye ak AT-ee)*
*I'd recommend at least two hundred megabits per second. That's enough for video conferences and so on.*

**Cwsmer:** Iawn, syniad da. Faint yw'r gost?
*(yown, SUN-yad dah. vah-eent you-r gost?)*
*Okay, good idea. How much is the cost?*

**Swyddog:** Mae'r pecyn dau gant megabeit gyda galwadau ffôn diderfyn yn tri deg pump o bunnoedd y mis.
*(my-r PEK-in dah-ee gant MEG-a-byte guh-dah gal-WAD-eye ffohn did-ER-vin uhn tree deg peemp oh PIHN-oy-th uh meess)*
*The two hundred megabit package with unlimited phone calls is thirty-five pounds per month.*

**Cwsmer:** Ydy hynny ar gytundeb?
*(uh-dee huhn-ee ar guh-TIHN-deb?)*
*Is that on a contract?*

**Swyddog:** Ydy, cytundeb deunaw mis. Ar ôl hynny, mae'n mynd yn fisol.
*(uh-dee, kuh-TIHN-deb day-NAW-oo meess. ar ohl huhn-ee, my-n mind uhn VISS-ol)*
*Yes, an eighteen-month contract. After that, it goes monthly.*

**Cwsmer:** A beth os dw i eisiau canslo cyn diwedd y cytundeb?
*(ah beth oss doo ee ay-shah KAN-sloh keen dee-WEH-th uh kuh-TIHN-deb?)*
*And what if I want to cancel before the end of the contract?*

**Swyddog:** Bydd angen i chi dalu ffi canslo - cyfanswm y misoedd sy'n weddill.
*(beeth ang-en ee khee dal-ee fee KAN-sloh - kuh-van-SOOM uh MISS-oy-th seen WETH-iLl)*
*You'll need to pay a cancellation fee - the total of the remaining months.*

**Cwsmer:** Iawn, dw i'n deall. Pryd gallwch chi gysylltu'r gwasanaeth?
*(yown, doo een dah-eLl. preed gaLl-ookh khee guh-SUHL-tee-r gwass-AN-eyeth?)*
*Okay, I understand. When can you connect the service?*

**Swyddog:** Gallwn ni anfon y modem a'r llawlyfr o fewn tri diwrnod. Yna, bydd angen i chi ei gysylltu eich hun.
*(gaLl-oon nee AN-von uh MOD-em ahr LHOW-luhv-r oh veh-oon tree dee-OOR-nod. UN-ah, beeth ang-en ee khee ay guh-SUHL-tee aykh heen)*
*We can send the modem and manual within three days. Then, you'll need to connect it yourself.*

**Cwsmer:** Ydy hynny'n hawdd?
*(uh-dee huhn-een HOW-th?)*
*Is that easy?*

**Swyddog:** Ydy, mae'n syml iawn. Os oes problem, gallwch chi ffonio'r llinell gymorth.
*(uh-dee, my-n SIM-l yown. oss oh-ess PROB-lem, gaLl-ookh khee FON-yoh-r LHIN-eLl GUM-orth)*
*Yes, it's very simple. If there's a problem, you can phone the support line.*

## Reporting Maintenance Issues

### Dialogue 6
**Tenant:** Bore da. Mae gen i broblem gyda'r boeler. Does dim dŵr poeth.
*(BOH-reh dah. my gen ee PROB-lem guh-dar BOY-ler. doyss dim door poyth)*
*Good morning. I have a problem with the boiler. There's no hot water.*

**Landlord:** Bore da. O na, mae'n ddrwg gen i glywed hynny. Pryd ddigwyddodd hyn?
*(BOH-reh dah. oh nah, my-n throong gen ee GLOO-wed huhn-ee. preed dig-WITH-oth heen?)*
*Good morning. Oh no, I'm sorry to hear that. When did this happen?*

**Tenant:** Ddoe. Roedd popeth yn iawn ddydd Llun, ond dim dŵr poeth o gwbl ddoe.
*(THOY. royd POP-eth uhn yown theeth lheen, ond dim door poyth oh goo-bl thoy)*
*Yesterday. Everything was fine on Monday, but no hot water at all yesterday.*

**Landlord:** Ydych chi wedi edrych ar y boeler? Oes negeseuon gwall arno?
*(uh-deekh khee weh-dee ED-rukh ar uh BOY-ler? oh-ess neg-ESS-eh-yon gwaLl AR-noh?)*
*Have you looked at the boiler? Are there any error messages on it?*

**Tenant:** Ydw. Mae golau coch yn fflachio ac mae'n dweud 'E110'.
*(uh-doo. my GOL-eye kokh uhn FLAKH-yoh ak my-n dweyd ee-cant-deg)*
*Yes. A red light is flashing and it says 'E110'.*

**Landlord:** Iawn, mae hynny'n swnio fel problem gyda'r pwysedd. Gallaf i anfon plymwr heddiw.
*(yown, my huhn-een SOON-yoh vel PROB-lem guh-dar POO-ee-seth. gaLl-av ee AN-von PLUHM-oor heh-thyoo)*
*Okay, that sounds like a pressure problem. I can send a plumber today.*

**Tenant:** Diolch yn fawr. Pa mor gyflym allwch chi drefnu hyn?
*(dee-olkh un vow-r. pah mor GUHV-lim aLl-ookh khee TREV-nee heen?)*
*Thanks very much. How quickly can you arrange this?*

**Landlord:** Gadewch i fi ffonio'r plymwr nawr. Fyddwch chi gartref y prynhawn yma?
*(gad-EH-ookh ee vee FON-yoh-r PLUHM-oor now-r. VUH-thookh khee GAR-trev uh prin-HOWN uh-mah?)*
*Let me phone the plumber now. Will you be home this afternoon?*

**Tenant:** Byddaf, rhwng dau a chwech o'r gloch.
*(buh-DAV, rhoong dah-ee ah khwekh ohr glokh)*
*Yes, between two and six o'clock.*

**Landlord:** Perffaith. Byddaf yn anfon neges atoch chi gyda'r amser penodol.
*(per-FAH-eeth. buh-DAV uhn AN-von NEG-ess AT-okh khee guh-dar AM-ser pen-OD-ol)*
*Perfect. I'll send you a message with the specific time.*

**Tenant:** Diolch yn fawr iawn. Oes unrhyw beth arall y dylwn i wybod?
*(dee-olkh un vow-r yown. oh-ess uhn-rhew beth ar-aLl uh DULL-oon ee GWUH-bod?)*
*Thanks very much indeed. Is there anything else I should know?*

**Landlord:** Os nad yw'r plymwr yn gallu trwsio heddiw, byddaf yn trefnu llety dros dro i chi.
*(oss nad you-r PLUHM-oor uhn gaLl-ee TROO-see-oh heh-thyoo, buh-DAV uhn TREV-nee LHET-ee dross droh ee khee)*
*If the plumber can't fix it today, I'll arrange temporary accommodation for you.*

**Tenant:** Diolch, ond gobeithio bydd popeth yn iawn.
*(dee-olkh, ond gob-AYTH-yoh beeth POP-eth uhn yown)*
*Thanks, but hopefully everything will be okay.*

### Dialogue 7
**Tenant:** Prynhawn da. Mae'r toiled yn gollwng ac mae dŵr ar y llawr.
*(prin-HOWN dah. my-r TOY-led uhn GOL-hoong ak my door ar uh lhow-r)*
*Good afternoon. The toilet is leaking and there's water on the floor.*

**Landlord:** O diar! Ydy hi'n argyfwng? Oes llawer o ddŵr?
*(oh DEE-ar! uh-dee heen ar-GUV-oong? oh-ess LHOW-er oh THOOR?)*
*Oh dear! Is it an emergency? Is there a lot of water?*

**Tenant:** Na, dim llawer. Ond mae'n gollwng yn gyson.
*(nah, dim LHOW-er. ond my-n GOL-hoong uhn GUSS-on)*
*No, not much. But it's leaking constantly.*

**Landlord:** Ydych chi wedi troi'r dŵr i ffwrdd?
*(uh-deekh khee weh-dee troy-r door ee FFOOR-th?)*
*Have you turned the water off?*

**Tenant:** Na, dw i ddim yn gwybod ble mae'r stopfalf.
*(nah, doo ee theem uhn GWUH-bod bleh my-r STOP-valv)*
*No, I don't know where the stopcock is.*

**Landlord:** Mae'r stopfalf o dan y sinc yn y gegin. Trowch hi i'r chwith.
*(my-r STOP-valv oh dan uh sink uhn uh GEG-in. trow-kh hee eer khweeth)*
*The stopcock is under the sink in the kitchen. Turn it to the left.*

**Tenant:** Iawn, dw i wedi gwneud hynny. Mae'r dŵr wedi stopio.
*(yown, doo ee weh-dee oo-nay-d huhn-ee. my-r door weh-dee STOP-yoh)*
*Okay, I've done that. The water has stopped.*

**Landlord:** Da iawn. Nawr, sychwch y dŵr ar y llawr. Byddaf yn anfon plymwr yfory.
*(dah yown. now-r, SUKH-ookh uh door ar uh lhow-r. buh-DAV uhn AN-von PLUHM-oor uh-VOR-ee)*
*Very good. Now, dry the water on the floor. I'll send a plumber tomorrow.*

**Tenant:** Diolch. Ond heb ddŵr, sut allaf i defnyddio'r toiled neu ymolchi?
*(dee-olkh. ond heb THOOR, seet aLl-av ee dev-NITH-yoh-r TOY-led nay um-OL-khee?)*
*Thanks. But without water, how can I use the toilet or wash?*

**Landlord:** Gallwch chi droi'r dŵr yn ôl ymlaen am ychydig, ond gwyliwch yn ofalus.
*(gaLl-ookh khee troy-r door uhn ohl um-LEYEN am ukh-ID-ig, ond GWEEL-yookh uhn ov-AL-iss)*
*You can turn the water back on for a little while, but watch carefully.*

**Tenant:** Iawn, diolch am eich help.
*(yown, dee-olkh am aykh help)*
*Okay, thanks for your help.*

## Talking to the Landlord

### Dialogue 8
**Tenant:** Bore da. Hoffwn i drafod y cytundeb am y flwyddyn nesaf.
*(BOH-reh dah. hoff-oon ee TRAV-od uh kuh-TIHN-deb am uh VLOO-eeth-een NESS-av)*
*Good morning. I'd like to discuss the contract for next year.*

**Landlord:** Bore da! Wrth gwrs. Ydych chi eisiau aros yn y tŷ?
*(BOH-reh dah! oorth goorss. uh-deekh khee ay-shah AR-oss uhn uh tee?)*
*Good morning! Of course. Do you want to stay in the house?*

**Tenant:** Ydw, rydyn ni'n hapus iawn yma. Ond mae gen i ychydig o gwestiynau.
*(uh-doo, RUD-in neen HAP-iss yown uh-mah. ond my gen ee ukh-ID-ig oh gwes-TIHN-eye)*
*Yes, we're very happy here. But I have a few questions.*

**Landlord:** Wrth gwrs. Beth hoffech chi wybod?
*(oorth goorss. beth hoff-ekh khee GWUH-bod?)*
*Of course. What would you like to know?*

**Tenant:** Yn gyntaf, ydych chi'n mynd i godi'r rhent?
*(uhn GUN-tav, uh-deekh kheen mind ee GOD-ee-r rhent?)*
*Firstly, are you going to increase the rent?*

**Landlord:** Yn anffodus, mae angen i fi godi'r rhent ychydig. Mae costau cynnal a chadw wedi codi.
*(uhn an-FFOD-iss, my ang-en ee vee GOD-ee-r rhent ukh-ID-ig. my KOSS-teye KUHN-aLl ah KAD-oo weh-dee KOD-ee)*
*Unfortunately, I need to increase the rent a little. Maintenance costs have risen.*

**Tenant:** Faint o gynnydd?
*(vah-eent oh GUHN-ith?)*
*How much of an increase?*

**Landlord:** Pum deg punt y mis. O wyth gant pum deg pump i naw cant pum punt.
*(peem deg peent uh meess. oh weeth gant peem deg peemp ee nah-oo gant peemp peent)*
*Fifty pounds per month. From eight hundred and fifty-five to nine hundred and five pounds.*

**Tenant:** Hmm, mae hynny'n gynnydd go fawr. Oes unrhyw le i drafod?
*(hmm, my huhn-een GUHN-ith goh vow-r. oh-ess uhn-rhew leh ee TRAV-od?)*
*Hmm, that's quite a big increase. Is there any room to negotiate?*

**Landlord:** Beth am tri deg pump punt? Wyth gant naw deg punt?
*(beth am tree deg peemp peent? weeth gant nah-oo deg peent?)*
*What about thirty-five pounds? Eight hundred and ninety pounds?*

**Tenant:** Mae hynny'n fwy rhesymol. Ond hoffwn i ofyn am ychydig o waith.
*(my huhn-een voy rhess-UM-ol. ond hoff-oon ee OV-in am ukh-ID-ig oh weyeth)*
*That's more reasonable. But I'd like to ask for a little work.*

**Landlord:** Pa fath o waith?
*(pah vath oh weyeth?)*
*What kind of work?*

**Tenant:** Mae'r gegin angen ei pheintio, ac mae'r carped yn yr ystafell fyw yn hen iawn.
*(my-r GEG-in ang-en ay PHAYN-tyoh, ak my-r KAR-ped uhn eer uss-TAV-eLl VEW uhn hen yown)*
*The kitchen needs painting, and the carpet in the living room is very old.*

**Landlord:** Iawn. Os ydych chi'n cytuno i'r rhent newydd, byddaf yn peintio'r gegin cyn i chi adnewyddu'r cytundeb.
*(yown. oss uh-deekh kheen kuh-TIN-oh eer rhent NEH-with, buh-DAV uhn PAYN-tyoh-r GEG-in keen ee khee ad-new-EETH-ee-r kuh-TIHN-deb)*
*Okay. If you agree to the new rent, I'll paint the kitchen before you renew the contract.*

**Tenant:** A beth am y carped?
*(ah beth am uh KAR-ped?)*
*And what about the carpet?*

**Landlord:** Bydd rhaid i chi aros tan y flwyddyn nesaf am hynny, mae'n ddrwg gen i.
*(beeth rhide ee khee AR-oss tan uh VLOO-eeth-een NESS-av am huhn-ee, my-n throong gen ee)*
*You'll have to wait until next year for that, I'm sorry.*

**Tenant:** Iawn, dw i'n deall. Cytunaf i'r telerau newydd.
*(yown, doo een dah-eLl. kuh-TIN-av eer tel-ER-eye NEH-with)*
*Okay, I understand. I agree to the new terms.*

## Moving In/Moving Out

### Dialogue 9
**Tenant:** Prynhawn da. Dw i yma i gasglu'r allweddi ar gyfer symud i mewn.
*(prin-HOWN dah. doo ee uh-mah ee KASS-glee-r aLl-WEH-thee ar guh-ver sum-ID ee meh-oon)*
*Good afternoon. I'm here to collect the keys for moving in.*

**Landlord:** Prynhawn da! Wrth gwrs. Ydych chi wedi talu'r blaendal a'r rhent cyntaf?
*(prin-HOWN dah! oorth goorss. uh-deekh khee weh-dee TAL-eer BLEYEN-dal ahr rhent KUN-tav?)*
*Good afternoon! Of course. Have you paid the deposit and first rent?*

**Tenant:** Ydw, trosglwyddesi i'r arian ddoe. Dyma'r derbynneb.
*(uh-doo, tross-GLOOD-ess-ee eer ar-yan THOY. duh-mar ther-buhn-eb)*
*Yes, I transferred the money yesterday. Here's the receipt.*

**Landlord:** Perffaith. Nawr, gadewch i ni fynd trwy'r archwiliad.
*(per-FAH-eeth. now-r, gad-EH-ookh ee nee veend troo-eer arkh-WIL-yad)*
*Perfect. Now, let's go through the inspection.*

**Tenant:** Iawn. Beth mae angen i fi wneud?
*(yown. beth my ang-en ee vee oo-nay-d?)*
*Okay. What do I need to do?*

**Landlord:** Byddwn ni'n mynd o gwmpas y tŷ ac yn nodi cyflwr popeth. Yna, byddwch chi'n llofnodi'r adroddiad.
*(beeth-oon neen mind oh GOOM-pass uh tee ak uhn NOD-ee KUV-loor POP-eth. UN-ah, beeth-ookh kheen lhov-NOD-eer ad-ROTH-yad)*
*We'll go around the house and note the condition of everything. Then, you'll sign the report.*

**Tenant:** Iawn. Ga i dynnu lluniau hefyd?
*(yown. gah ee tuhn-ee LHIN-eye heh-vid?)*
*Okay. Can I take photos too?*

**Landlord:** Gallwch, mae hynny'n syniad da. Mae'n eich diogelu chi a fi.
*(gaLl-ookh, my huhn-een SUN-yad dah. my-n aykh dee-OG-el-ee khee ah vee)*
*You can, that's a good idea. It protects both you and me.*

**Tenant:** Perffaith. Beth am yr allweddi? Sawl copi sydd?
*(per-FAH-eeth. beth am eer aLl-WEH-thee? sow-l KOP-ee seeth?)*
*Perfect. What about the keys? How many copies are there?*

**Landlord:** Mae dau gopi o'r allwedd ffrynt a dwy allwedd gefn. Hefyd, un allwedd garej.
*(my dah-ee GOP-ee ohr aLl-WEH-th frint ah doo-ee aLl-WEH-th gev-n. heh-vid, een aLl-WEH-th GAR-ej)*
*There are two copies of the front key and two back keys. Also, one garage key.*

**Tenant:** Gwych. Beth os dw i eisiau copïau ychwanegol?
*(gweekh. beth oss doo ee ay-shah KOP-ee-eye ukh-wan-EG-ol?)*
*Great. What if I want extra copies?*

**Landlord:** Rhaid i chi ofyn i fi'n gyntaf. Yna, gallwch chi gael copïau ar eich cost eich hun.
*(rhide ee khee OV-in ee veen GUN-tav. UN-ah, gaLl-ookh khee gah-el KOP-ee-eye ar aykh kost aykh heen)*
*You have to ask me first. Then, you can get copies at your own cost.*

**Tenant:** Iawn, dw i'n deall. Beth am y biniau ailgylchu?
*(yown, doo een dah-eLl. beth am uh BIN-eye ile-GULL-khee?)*
*Okay, I understand. What about the recycling bins?*

**Landlord:** Mae'r biniau yn yr ardd gefn. Casgliad dydd Llun bob wythnos.
*(my-r BIN-eye uhn eer arth GEV-n. KASS-lyad theeth lheen bob WEETH-noss)*
*The bins are in the back garden. Collection Monday every week.*

**Tenant:** Diolch. Pryd mae angen i fi roi rhybudd os dw i eisiau gadael?
*(dee-olkh. preed my ang-en ee vee rhoy RHUH-bith oss doo ee ay-shah gad-EYE-l?)*
*Thanks. When do I need to give notice if I want to leave?*

**Landlord:** Mis o rybudd ysgrifenedig. A bydd angen glanhau'r tŷ'n drylwyr cyn gadael.
*(meess oh RHUH-bith uss-griv-en-ED-ig. ah beeth ang-en glan-HEYE-r teen drul-OOR keen gad-EYE-l)*
*One month's written notice. And you'll need to clean the house thoroughly before leaving.*

**Tenant:** Wrth gwrs. Diolch am bopeth.
*(oorth goorss. dee-olkh am BOP-eth)*
*Of course. Thanks for everything.*
