# Family and Friends

## Talking About Family

**Person A:** Oes teulu mawr gyda ti?
*(oyss TAY-lee MOW-r GUH-dah tee?)*
*Do you have a big family?*

**Person B:** Oes, mae brodyr a chwiorydd gyda fi. Beth amdanat ti?
*(oyss, my BROD-eer ah KHWYOR-ith GUH-dah vee. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*Yes, I have brothers and sisters. What about you?*

**Person A:** Nac oes, plentyn unig ydw i.
*(nak oyss, PLEN-tin EEN-ig UH-doo ee)*
*No, I'm an only child.*

---

**Person A:** Oes plant gyda ti?
*(oyss PLANT GUH-dah tee?)*
*Do you have children?*

**Person B:** Oes, mae dau blentyn gyda fi - bachgen a merch. Oes plant gyda ti?
*(oyss, my DIE PLEN-tin GUH-dah vee - BAKH-gen ah MERKH. oyss PLANT GUH-dah tee?)*
*Yes, I have two children - a boy and a girl. Do you have children?*

**Person A:** Nac oes, ddim eto.
*(nak oyss, thim ET-oh)*
*No, not yet.*

---

**Person A:** Ble mae dy deulu di'n byw?
*(bleh my duh DAY-lee deen BEEW?)*
*Where does your family live?*

**Person B:** Mae fy rhieni'n byw yng Nghaerdydd. Mae fy chwaer yn byw yn Llundain. Beth am dy deulu di?
*(my vuh RHEE-en-een BEEW ung NGHY-r-deeth. my vuh KHWAH-er un BEEW un LLin-DEYEN. beth am duh DAY-lee dee?)*
*My parents live in Cardiff. My sister lives in London. What about your family?*

**Person A:** Mae fy nheulu i gyd yn byw yn lleol.
*(my vuh NHAY-lee ee GEED un BEEW un LLeh-OL)*
*All my family live locally.*

## Family Activities

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n gwneud gyda dy deulu ar y penwythnos?
*(beth weet teen GOO-nayd GUH-dah duh DAY-lee ar uh pen-WEETH-noss?)*
*What do you do with your family on the weekend?*

**Person B:** Fel arfer, dyn ni'n mynd am ginio neu'n mynd am dro. Beth amdanat ti?
*(vel AR-ver, deen neen meend am GIN-yoh nay-oon meend am DROH. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*Usually, we go for lunch or go for a walk. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dyn ni'n hoffi mynd i'r sinema neu chwarae gemau.
*(deen neen HOFF-ee meend eer sin-EM-ah nay KHWAH-rye GEM-eye)*
*We like to go to the cinema or play games.*

---

**Person A:** Pryd wnes di weld dy deulu diwethaf?
*(preed OO-nest dee weld duh DAY-lee dee-WETH-av?)*
*When did you last see your family?*

**Person B:** Weles i nhw ddoe. Aethon ni am goffi. Pryd weles di dy deulu?
*(WEL-ess ee NHOO THOH-eh. EYE-thon nee am GOFF-ee. preed WEL-ess dee duh DAY-lee?)*
*I saw them yesterday. We went for coffee. When did you see your family?*

**Person A:** Weles i nhw wythnos diwethaf.
*(WEL-ess ee NHOO WEETH-noss dee-WETH-av)*
*I saw them last week.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n gweld dy deulu'n aml?
*(weet teen gweld duh DAY-lee-oon AM-l?)*
*Do you see your family often?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n gweld nhw bob wythnos. Wyt ti'n gweld dy deulu'n aml?
*(UH-doo, doo een gweld NHOO bob WEETH-noss. weet teen gweld duh DAY-lee-oon AM-l?)*
*Yes, I see them every week. Do you see your family often?*

**Person A:** Nac ydw, maen nhw'n byw'n bell i ffwrdd.
*(nak UH-doo, mine NHOO-oon BEEW-oon BELL ee FFOORH)*
*No, they live far away.*

## Talking About Friends

**Person A:** Oes ffrindiau da gyda ti?
*(oyss FFRIN-dee-eye DAH GUH-dah tee?)*
*Do you have good friends?*

**Person B:** Oes, mae llawer o ffrindiau da gyda fi. Beth amdanat ti?
*(oyss, my LLow-ER oh FFRIN-dee-eye DAH GUH-dah vee. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*Yes, I have lots of good friends. What about you?*

**Person A:** Oes, mae ychydig o ffrindiau agos gyda fi.
*(oyss, my UKH-uh-dig oh FFRIN-dee-eye AG-oss GUH-dah vee)*
*Yes, I have a few close friends.*

---

**Person A:** Sut wnes di gwrdd â dy ffrind gorau?
*(seet OO-nest dee GOORth ah duh FFREEND gor-EYE?)*
*How did you meet your best friend?*

**Person B:** Cwrddon ni yn yr ysgol. Dyn ni wedi bod yn ffrindiau ers ugain mlynedd! Sut gwnes di gwrdd â dy ffrindiau?
*(KOOR-thon nee un eer UHS-gol. deen nee WEH-dee bohd un FFRIN-dee-eye erss ee-GEYEN MLUN-eth! seet OO-nest dee GOORth ah duh FFRIN-dee-eye?)*
*We met at school. We've been friends for twenty years! How did you meet your friends?*

**Person A:** Cwrddes i â rhai yn y gwaith ac eraill trwy hobïau.
*(KOOR-thess ee ah RHIGH un uh GWEYTH ak er-EYELL TROO-ee hob-EE-eye)*
*I met some at work and others through hobbies.*

---

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti a dy ffrindiau'n hoffi gwneud?
*(beth weet tee ah duh FFRIN-dee-eye-een HOFF-ee GOO-nayd?)*
*What do you and your friends like to do?*

**Person B:** Dyn ni'n hoffi mynd i'r dafarn neu wylio pêl-droed. Beth amdanoch chi?
*(deen neen HOFF-ee meend eer DAV-arn nay GWIL-yoh PEHL-droyd. beth am-DAN-okh khee?)*
*We like to go to the pub or watch football. What about you (plural)?*

**Person A:** Dyn ni'n hoffi mynd am fwyd neu fynd i gerddoriaeth fyw.
*(deen neen HOFF-ee meend am VOOD nay veend ee ger-THOR-yeth VEEW)*
*We like to go for food or go to live music.*

## Making Plans with Friends

**Person A:** Wyt ti eisiau cwrdd yr wythnos nesaf?
*(weet tee AY-shah GOORth eer WEETH-noss NESS-av?)*
*Do you want to meet next week?*

**Person B:** Oes, syniad da! Pryd wyt ti'n rhydd?
*(oyss, SUN-yad dah! preed weet teen RHEETH?)*
*Yes, good idea! When are you free?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n rhydd ddydd Gwener. Ydy hynny'n iawn?
*(doo een RHEETH theeth GWEN-er. UH-dee HUN-een YOWN?)*
*I'm free Friday. Is that okay?*

**Person B:** Perffaith! Beth am saith o'r gloch?
*(per-FEYETH! beth am SAITH ohr GLOKH?)*
*Perfect! How about seven o'clock?*

---

**Person A:** Beth hoffet ti wneud?
*(beth HOFF-et tee OO-nayd?)*
*What would you like to do?*

**Person B:** Beth am fynd am bryd o fwyd?
*(beth am veend am BREED oh VOOD?)*
*How about going for a meal?*

**Person A:** Syniad gwych! Pa fath o fwyd?
*(SUN-yad GWEEKH! pah VATH oh VOOD?)*
*Excellent idea! What kind of food?*

**Person B:** Beth am fwyd Indiaidd?
*(beth am VOOD in-dee-EYETH?)*
*How about Indian food?*

---

**Person A:** Ble hoffet ti gwrdd?
*(bleh HOFF-et tee GOORth?)*
*Where would you like to meet?*

**Person B:** Beth am gwrdd yn y caffi yn y canol?
*(beth am GOORth un uh KAFF-ee un uh KAN-ol?)*
*How about meeting at the café in the centre?*

**Person A:** Iawn, gwela i di yno!
*(YOWN, GWEL-ah ee dee UN-oh!)*
*Okay, I'll see you there!*

## Describing People

**Person A:** Sut un ydy dy ffrind?
*(seet een UH-dee duh FFREEND?)*
*What is your friend like?*

**Person B:** Mae hi'n garedig iawn ac yn ddoniol. Mae'n hawdd siarad â hi.
*(my heen gar-EH-dig YOWN ak un THON-yol. mine HOWTH SHAHR-ad ah hee)*
*She's very kind and funny. She's easy to talk to.*

**Person A:** Mae'n swnio'n hyfryd!
*(mine SWON-yoh-oon HUV-rid!)*
*She sounds lovely!*

---

**Person A:** Beth mae dy frawd yn gwneud?
*(beth my duh VROWD un GOO-nayd?)*
*What does your brother do?*

**Person B:** Mae e'n athro. Mae e'n hoffi ei waith yn fawr iawn.
*(my een ATH-roh. my een HOFF-ee ay GWEYTH un VOW-r YOWN)*
*He's a teacher. He likes his work very much.*

**Person A:** Da iawn!
*(dah YOWN!)*
*Very good!*

## Practice Tips
- Talk about your real family and friends
- Practice possessive constructions (fy mrawd, dy chwaer)
- Use different tenses to talk about past and future plans
- Practice numbers when talking about siblings or ages
