# Supermarket and Shopping

## Entering the Supermarket

### Dialogue 1
**Person A:** Bore da! Wyt ti'n mynd i'r archfarchnad heddiw?
*(BOH-reh dah! weet teen mind eer arkh-varkh-nad heh-thyoo?)*
*Good morning! Are you going to the supermarket today?*

**Person B:** Ydw, rhaid i fi brynu bwyd yr wythnos. Wyt ti eisiau dod gyda fi?
*(uh-doo, rhah-eed ee vee bruh-nee boo-id eer weeth-noss. weet tee ay-shah dohd guh-dah vee?)*
*Yes, I have to buy food for the week. Do you want to come with me?*

**Person A:** Syniad da! Oes rhestr siopa gyda ti?
*(suhn-yad dah! oh-ess rhes-tr shop-ah guh-dah tee?)*
*Good idea! Do you have a shopping list?*

**Person B:** Oes, dw i wedi paratoi rhestr hir!
*(oh-ess, doo ee weh-dee par-at-oy rhes-tr heer!)*
*Yes, I've prepared a long list!*

---

### Dialogue 2
**Person A:** Ga i droli os gwelwch yn dda?
*(gah ee drol-ee oss gwel-ookh un thah?)*
*Can I have a trolley please?*

**Person B:** Wrth gwrs. Mae'r trolis wrth y fynedfa.
*(oorth goorss. mah-eer trol-ees oorth uh vun-ed-vah)*
*Of course. The trolleys are by the entrance.*

**Person A:** Diolch yn fawr. Wyt ti eisiau basged neu droli?
*(dee-olkh un vow-r. weet tee ay-shah bas-ged nay drol-ee?)*
*Thanks very much. Do you want a basket or trolley?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n mynd i gael troli heddiw. Mae gen i lawer i brynu.
*(doo een mind ee gah-el drol-ee heh-thyoo. mah-ee gen ee lah-oor ee bruh-nee)*
*I'm going to get a trolley today. I have a lot to buy.*

---

## Finding Items

### Dialogue 3
**Person A:** Ble mae'r llaeth os gwelwch yn dda?
*(bleh mah-eer LLah-eeth oss gwel-ookh un thah?)*
*Where is the milk please?*

**Person B:** Mae'r cynnyrch llaeth yn yr adran oer, yn y cefn.
*(mah-eer kuhn-irkh LLah-eeth uhn eer ad-ran oy-r, uhn uh kev-n)*
*The dairy products are in the chilled section, at the back.*

**Person A:** Diolch! A ble mae'r bara ffres?
*(dee-olkh! ah bleh mah-eer bar-ah ffress?)*
*Thanks! And where is the fresh bread?*

**Person B:** Mae'r becws ar y chwith, wrth ymyl y ffrwythau.
*(mah-eer bek-oos ar uh khweeth, oorth um-il uh ffroo-ee-thah-ee)*
*The bakery is on the left, next to the fruit.*

---

### Dialogue 4
**Person A:** Esgusodwch fi, ble mae'r adran lysiau?

*The fresh vegetables are by the entrance. What are you looking for?*

**Person A:** Dw i eisiau moron, tatws, a nionod.
*(doo ee ay-shah mor-on, tat-oos, ah nyon-od)*

**Person B:** Mae'r rhain i gyd wrth ymyl ei gilydd yn yr adran lysiau.
*(mah-eer rhine ee geed oorth um-il ay gil-ith uhn eer ad-ran luss-yah-ee)*
*These are all next to each other in the vegetable section.*

### Dialogue 5

## Returning or Exchanging Items

**Person B:** Mae hwn yn opsiwn da. Mae e hefyd yn is mewn calorïau.

*This one is a good option. It's also lower in calories.*

### Dialogue 30
**Person A:** Pa frand wyt ti'n brynu fel arfer?
*(pah vrand weet teen bruh-nee vel ar-ver?)*
*Which brand do you usually buy?*

**Person B:** Fel arfer, dw i'n prynu brand y siop. Mae'n rhatach.
*(vel ar-ver, doo een pruh-nee brand uh shop. mah-een rhat-akh)*
*Usually, I buy the store brand. It's cheaper.*

**Person A:** Ydy e crystal?
*(uh-dee eh kriss-tal?)*
*Is it as good?*

**Person B:** Ydy, yn fy marn i. Dw i ddim yn gallu gweld gwahaniaeth.
*(uh-dee, uhn vuh marn ee. doo ee thim uhn gaLl-ee gweld gwah-an-yeth)*
*Yes, in my opinion. I can't tell the difference.*

## Weekly Shopping Conversation

### Dialogue 31
**Person A:** Wyt ti wedi gwneud rhestr siopa'r wythnos?
*(weet tee weh-dee oo-nay-d rhes-tr shop-ar weeth-noss?)*
*Have you done the weekly shopping list?*

**Person B:** Ydw, rhaid i ni brynu llawer yr wythnos hon.
*(uh-doo, rhide ee nee bruh-nee lah-oor eer weeth-noss hon)*
*Yes, we have to buy a lot this week.*

**Person A:** Beth sydd ar y rhestr?
*(beth seeth ar uh rhes-tr?)*
*What's on the list?*

**Person B:** Llaeth, bara, wyau, caws, cig, llysiau, a ffrwythau.
*(LLah-eeth, bar-ah, wee-ah-ee, kah-uss, keeg, LLuhs-yah-ee, ah ffroo-ee-thah-ee)*
*Milk, bread, eggs, cheese, meat, vegetables, and fruit.*

---

### Dialogue 32
**Person A:** Oes angen unrhyw beth arall?
*(oh-ess ang-en uhn-rhew beth ar-aLl?)*
*Do we need anything else?*

**Person B:** Oes, mae angen papur tŷ bach, sebon golchi llestri, a phowdwr golchi.
*(oh-ess, mah-ee ang-en pap-eer tee bakh, seb-on golkh-ee LLess-tree, ah phow-door golkh-ee)*
*Yes, we need toilet paper, washing-up liquid, and washing powder.*

**Person A:** Beth am fwyd y gath?
*(beth am voo-id uh gath?)*
*What about cat food?*

**Person B:** O ie! Anghofies i hynny. Diolch am gofio!
*(oh yeh! ang-hov-yes ee huhn-ee. dee-olkh am gov-yoh!)*
*Oh yes! I forgot that. Thanks for remembering!*

---

### Dialogue 33
**Person A:** Faint wyt ti eisiau gwario heddiw?
*(vah-eent weet tee ay-shah gwar-yoh heh-thyoo?)*
*How much do you want to spend today?*

**Person B:** Tua saith deg punt, gobeithio. Dim mwy na hynny.
*(tee-ah saith deg peent, gob-ay-thee-oh. dim moy nah huhn-ee)*
*About seventy pounds, hopefully. No more than that.*

**Person A:** Iawn, rhaid i ni gadw at y gyllideb.
*(yown, rhide ee nee gad-oo at uh guLl-id-eb)*
*Okay, we have to stick to the budget.*

**Person B:** Ie, ond os gwelwn ni gynigion da, gallwn ni brynu mwy.
*(yeh, ond oss gwel-oon nee guhn-ig-yon dah, gaLl-oon nee bruh-nee moy)*
*Yes, but if we see good offers, we can buy more.*

---

### Dialogue 34
**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n mynd i goginio'r wythnos hon?
*(beth weet teen mind ee gog-in-yoh-r weeth-noss hon?)*
*What are you going to cook this week?*

**Person B:** Cawl ddydd Llun, cyw iâr dydd Mawrth, a physgod dydd Gwener.
*(kah-ool theeth LLeen, kew ee-ar theeth Mow-rth, ah phuss-god theeth Gwen-er)*
*Soup on Monday, chicken on Tuesday, and fish on Friday.*

**Person A:** Syniad da! Beth am weddill yr wythnos?
*(suhn-yad dah! beth am weth-iLl eer weeth-noss?)*
*Good idea! What about the rest of the week?*

**Person B:** Dw i ddim yn siŵr eto. Efallai pasta neu gyrri.
*(doo ee thim uhn shoo-r et-oh. ev-aLl-eye pass-tah nay guhr-ee)*
*I'm not sure yet. Maybe pasta or curry.*

---

### Dialogue 35
**Person A:** Oes angen i ni brynu bwyd ar gyfer y penwythnos?
*(oh-ess ang-en ee nee bruh-nee boo-id ar guh-ver uh pen-weeth-noss?)*
*Do we need to buy food for the weekend?*

**Person B:** Oes, mae ffrindiau'n dod draw nos Sadwrn.
*(oh-ess, mah-ee ffrin-dyah-een dod draw noss Sad-oorn)*
*Yes, friends are coming over Saturday night.*

**Person A:** Beth ddylen ni wneud?
*(beth thuh-len nee oo-nay-d?)*
*What should we make?*

**Person B:** Beth am farbeciw? Mae'r tywydd yn mynd i fod yn braf.
*(beth am var-bek-yoo? mah-eer tuh-with uhn mind ee vod uhn brahv)*
*How about a barbecue? The weather is going to be nice.*

---

### Dialogue 36
**Person A:** Oes unrhyw beth arbennig eisiau ei brynu?
*(oh-ess uhn-rhew beth ar-ben-ig ay-shah ay bruh-nee?)*
*Is there anything special that needs buying?*

**Person B:** Oes, mae pen-blwydd Mam ddydd Mercher. Rhaid i ni brynu cacen.
*(oh-ess, mah-ee pen-blooth mam theeth Mer-kher. rhide ee nee bruh-nee kak-en)*
*Yes, it's Mum's birthday on Wednesday. We have to buy a cake.*

**Person A:** Awn ni i'r becws arbenigol?
*(ah-oon nee eer bek-oos ar-ben-ig-ol?)*
*Shall we go to the specialist bakery?*

**Person B:** Syniad da! Mae eu cacennau nhw'n fendigedig.
*(suhn-yad dah! mah-ee ay kak-en-ah-ee hoon ven-dig-eye-dig)*
*Good idea! Their cakes are wonderful.*

## Practice Tips
- Practice with a partner, taking turns being the customer and shop assistant
- Use real shopping lists to practice vocabulary
- Visit Welsh supermarkets (if available) to practice in real situations
- Learn prices and numbers thoroughly - they're used constantly
- Practice both North Wales (gen i) and South Wales (gyda fi) forms
- Learn common supermarket section names (adran lysiau, adran gig, etc.)
- Practice asking polite questions with "os gwelwch yn dda"
- Role-play different scenarios: weekly shop, quick shop, returning items
- Listen to how Welsh speakers interact in shops if possible
- Remember mutations after "y" (the), "fy" (my), etc.
