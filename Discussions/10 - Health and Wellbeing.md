# Health and Wellbeing

## Asking How Someone Feels

**Person A:** Sut wyt ti'n teimlo heddiw?
*(seet weet teen tay-m-loh heh-thyoo?)*
*How are you feeling today?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n teimlo'n dda, diolch. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een tay-m-loh-oon thah, dee-olkh. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I'm feeling good, thanks. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n teimlo ychydig yn flinedig.
*(doo een tay-m-loh uhkh-uh-dig uhn vlin-ed-ig)*
*I'm feeling a bit tired.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n iawn?
*(weet teen yown?)*
*Are you okay?*

**Person B:** Ydw, diolch. Pam wyt ti'n gofyn?
*(uh-doo, dee-olkh. pam weet teen gov-in?)*
*Yes, thanks. Why are you asking?*

**Person A:** Rwyt ti'n edrych yn flinedig.
*(roo-eet teen ed-rikh uhn vlin-ed-ig)*
*You look tired.*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i heb gysgu'n dda neithiwr.
*(uh-doo, doo ee heb guhs-geen thah nay-thee-oor)*
*Yes, I haven't slept well last night.*

---

**Person A:** Sut wyt ti'n teimlo'r bore 'ma?
*(seet weet teen tay-m-lohr bor-eh mah?)*
*How are you feeling this morning?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n teimlo'n well heddiw, diolch. Roeddwn i'n sâl ddoe.
*(doo een tay-m-loh-oon well heh-thyoo, dee-olkh. roh-eed-oon een sahl thoh-eh)*
*I'm feeling better today, thanks. I was ill yesterday.*

**Person A:** Da iawn! Beth oedd yn bod?
*(dah yown! beth oh-eedh uhn bohd?)*
*Very good! What was wrong?*

## Talking About Illness

**Person A:** Dw i ddim yn teimlo'n dda.
*(doo ee thim uhn tay-m-loh-oon thah)*
*I don't feel well.*

**Person B:** O na! Beth sy'n bod?
*(oh nah! beth seen bohd?)*
*Oh no! What's wrong?*

**Person A:** Mae pen tost gyda fi a dw i'n teimlo'n oer.
*(mah-ee pen tost guh-dah vee ah doo een tay-m-loh-oon oh-er)*
*I have a headache and I'm feeling cold.*

**Person B:** Dyliet ti fynd adref ac ymlacio.
*(duhl-yet tee vind ad-rev ak uhm-lak-yoh)*
*You should go home and relax.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n iawn?
*(weet teen yown?)*
*Are you okay?*

**Person B:** Nac ydw, mae annwyd gyda fi.
*(nak uh-doo, mah-ee an-oo-id guh-dah vee)*
*No, I have a cold.*

**Person A:** Druan! Pryd ddechreuodd e?
*(dree-an! preed thekh-ray-oth eh?)*
*Poor thing! When did it start?*

**Person B:** Ddechreuodd e ddoe. Mae peswch a thrwyn rhedeg gyda fi.
*(thekh-ray-oth eh thoh-eh. mah-ee pess-ookh ah throo-in rhed-eg guh-dah vee)*
*It started yesterday. I have a cough and runny nose.*

---

**Person A:** Sut wyt ti'n teimlo nawr?
*(seet weet teen tay-m-loh now-r?)*
*How are you feeling now?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n teimlo'n well, diolch. Ond dw i dal yn wan.
*(doo een tay-m-loh-oon well, dee-olkh. ond doo ee dahl uhn wahn)*
*I'm feeling better, thanks. But I'm still weak.*

**Person A:** Cymera amser i wella'n iawn.
*(kuhm-er-ah am-ser ee weLl-ah-oon yown)*
*Take time to get well properly.*

---

**Person A:** Oedd rhaid i ti weld y meddyg?
*(oh-eedh rhah-eed ee tee weld uh meth-ig?)*
*Did you have to see the doctor?*

**Person B:** Oedd, es i bore 'ma. Rhoddodd e bresgripsiwn i fi.
*(oh-eedh, ess ee bor-eh mah. rhoth-oth eh bres-grip-see-oon ee vee)*
*Yes, I went this morning. He gave me a prescription.*

**Person A:** Gobeithio byddi di'n well yn fuan!
*(gob-ay-thyoh buh-thee deen well uhn vee-an!)*
*I hope you'll be better soon!*

## At the Doctor's

**Person A:** Beth sy'n bod?
*(beth seen bohd?)*
*What's wrong?*

**Person B:** Mae cur pen gyda fi ers dau ddiwrnod.
*(mah-ee keer pen guh-dah vee erss dah-ee thee-oor-nod)*
*I've had a headache for two days.*

**Person A:** Oes symptomau eraill gyda ti?
*(oh-ess simp-tom-ah-ee er-ah-eeLl guh-dah tee?)*
*Do you have other symptoms?*

**Person B:** Oes, dw i'n teimlo'n sâl hefyd.
*(oh-ess, doo een tay-m-loh-oon sahl heh-vid)*
*Yes, I'm feeling sick too.*

---

**Person A:** Ers pryd mae hyn wedi bod?
*(erss preed mah-ee heen weh-dee bohd?)*
*How long has this been?*

**Person B:** Ers wythnos diwethaf. Mae'n mynd yn waeth.
*(erss weeth-noss dee-weth-av. mah-een mind uhn wah-eeth)*
*Since last week. It's getting worse.*

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n cymryd unrhyw feddyginiaeth?
*(weet teen kuhm-rid uhn-rhew veth-ug-in-yah-eeth?)*
*Are you taking any medication?*

**Person B:** Nac ydw, ddim ar hyn o bryd.
*(nak uh-doo, thim ar heen oh breed)*
*No, not at the moment.*

---

**Person A:** Rhaid i ti gymryd y tabledi hyn.
*(rhah-eed ee tee kuhm-rid uh tab-led-ee heen)*
*You must take these tablets.*

**Person B:** Faint ddylwn i gymryd?
*(vah-eent thuhl-oon ee kuhm-rid?)*
*How many should I take?*

**Person A:** Un dabled deirgwaith y dydd gyda bwyd.
*(een tab-led day-r-gwah-eeth uh deeth guh-dah boo-id)*
*One tablet three times a day with food.*

**Person B:** Am faint o amser?
*(am vah-eent oh am-ser?)*
*For how long?*

**Person A:** Am wythnos. Os nad wyt ti'n gwella, dere yn ôl.
*(am weeth-noss. oss nad weet teen gweLl-ah, der-eh uhn ohl)*
*For a week. If you're not getting better, come back.*

## At the Pharmacy

**Person A:** Alla i dy helpu di?
*(aLl-ah ee duh hel-pee dee?)*
*Can I help you?*

**Person B:** Oes, oes rhywbeth gyda chi ar gyfer pen tost?
*(oh-ess, oh-ess rhew-beth guh-dah khee ar guh-ver pen tost?)*
*Yes, do you have anything for a headache?*

**Person A:** Oes, hoffet ti tabledi neu hylif?
*(oh-ess, hoff-et tee tab-led-ee nay huhl-iv?)*
*Yes, would you like tablets or liquid?*

**Person B:** Tabledi os gweli di'n dda.
*(tab-led-ee oss gwel-ee deen thah)*
*Tablets please.*

---

**Person A:** Dw i angen y presgripsiwn hwn os gweli di'n dda.
*(doo ee ang-en uh pres-grip-see-oon hoon oss gwel-ee deen thah)*
*I need this prescription please.*

**Person B:** Iawn, arhosa funud. Fydd e'n barod cyn bo hir.
*(yown, ar-hoss-ah vin-id. veeth een bar-od keen boh heer)*
*Okay, wait a minute. It'll be ready soon.*

**Person A:** Diolch. Faint ydy e?
*(dee-olkh. vah-eent uh-dee eh?)*
*Thanks. How much is it?*

**Person B:** Mae'n ddeg punt.
*(mah-een theg peent)*
*It's ten pounds.*

## Healthy Habits

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n edrych ar ôl dy iechyd?
*(weet teen ed-rikh ar ohl duh yekh-id?)*
*Do you look after your health?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n trio! Dw i'n bwyta'n iach ac yn ymarfer corff. Beth amdanat ti?
*(uh-doo, doo een tree-oh! doo een boo-tah-oon yakh ak uhn uhm-ar-ver korf. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I try! I eat healthily and exercise. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n trio gwneud mwy o ymarfer corff.
*(doo een tree-oh goo-nay-d moy oh uhm-ar-ver korf)*
*I try to do more exercise.*

---

**Person A:** Faint o ddŵr wyt ti'n yfed bob dydd?
*(vah-eent oh thoor weet teen uh-ved bob deeth?)*
*How much water do you drink every day?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n trio yfed dau litr. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een tree-oh uh-ved dah-ee leet-r. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I try to drink two litres. What about you?*

**Person A:** Ddylwn i yfed mwy! Dw i'n anghofio weithiau.
*(thuhl-oon ee uh-ved moy! doo een ang-hov-yoh way-thyah-ee)*
*I should drink more! I forget sometimes.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n cysgu'n dda?
*(weet teen kuhs-geen thah?)*
*Do you sleep well?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n cael wyth awr bob nos. Beth amdanat ti?
*(uh-doo, doo een kah-el weeth ah-oor bob noss. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I get eight hours every night. What about you?*

**Person A:** Nac ydw, dw i'n cael trafferth cysgu weithiau.
*(nak uh-doo, doo een kah-el traff-erth kuhs-gee way-thyah-ee)*
*No, I have trouble sleeping sometimes.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n bwyta pum llysiewyn a ffrwyth y dydd?
*(weet teen boo-tah peem LLuh-see-ew-in ah ffroo-eeth uh deeth?)*
*Do you eat five vegetables and fruit a day?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n trio, ond mae'n anodd weithiau. Wyt ti?
*(doo een tree-oh, ond mah-een an-oth way-thyah-ee. weet tee?)*
*I try, but it's difficult sometimes. Do you?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i'n bwyta salad bob dydd.
*(uh-doo, doo een boo-tah sal-ad bob deeth)*
*Yes, I eat salad every day.*

## Mental Health and Stress

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n teimlo'n stressed?
*(weet teen tay-m-loh-oon stress-d?)*
*Are you feeling stressed?*

**Person B:** Ydw, mae llawer o waith gyda fi ar hyn o bryd. Beth amdanat ti?
*(uh-doo, mah-ee LLah-oo-er oh wah-eeth guh-dah vee ar heen oh breed. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I have a lot of work at the moment. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n teimlo'r un peth. Mae'n anodd ymlacio.
*(doo een tay-m-lohr een peth. mah-een an-oth uhm-lak-yoh)*
*I feel the same. It's hard to relax.*

---

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n wneud i ymlacio?
*(beth weet teen oo-nay-d ee uhm-lak-yoh?)*
*What do you do to relax?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n meddylu ac yn darllen. Mae'n helpu. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een meth-uh-lee ak uhn dar-LLen. mah-een hel-pee. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I meditate and read. It helps. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n mynd am dro yn yr awyr agored.
*(doo een mind am droh uhn eer ah-oo-ir ag-or-ed)*
*I go for a walk in the fresh air.*

---

**Person A:** Sut wyt ti'n teimlo heddiw?
*(seet weet teen tay-m-loh heh-thyoo?)*
*How are you feeling today?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n teimlo'n isel heddiw. Mae'n ddiwrnod anodd.
*(doo een tay-m-loh-oon iss-el heh-thyoo. mah-een thee-oor-nod an-oth)*
*I'm feeling down today. It's a difficult day.*

**Person A:** Dw i'n deall. Oes unrhyw beth alla i wneud i helpu?
*(doo een deh-aLl. oh-ess uhn-rhew beth aLl-ah ee oo-nay-d ee hel-pee?)*
*I understand. Is there anything I can do to help?*

**Person B:** Diolch. Mae'n neis cael siarad.
*(dee-olkh. mah-een nay-ss kah-el shahr-ad)*
*Thanks. It's nice to talk.*

## Exercise and Fitness

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n cadw'n heini?
*(weet teen kad-oo-oon hay-nee?)*
*Do you keep fit?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n mynd i'r gampfa deirgwaith yr wythnos. Beth amdanat ti?
*(uh-doo, doo een mind eer gam-pfah day-r-gwah-eeth eer weeth-noss. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I go to the gym three times a week. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n rhedeg bob bore.
*(doo een rhed-eg bob bor-eh)*
*I run every morning.*

---

**Person A:** Pa fath o ymarfer corff wyt ti'n hoffi?
*(pah vahth oh uhm-ar-ver korf weet teen hoff-ee?)*
*What kind of exercise do you like?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n hoffi nofio ac ioga. Beth wyt ti'n hoffi?
*(doo een hoff-ee nov-yoh ak yog-ah. beth weet teen hoff-ee?)*
*I like swimming and yoga. What do you like?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n hoffi cerdded a beicio.
*(doo een hoff-ee ker-thed ah bay-kyoh)*
*I like walking and cycling.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n teimlo'n well ar ôl ymarfer corff?
*(weet teen tay-m-loh-oon well ar ohl uhm-ar-ver korf?)*
*Do you feel better after exercise?*

**Person B:** Ydw, mae'n gwneud i fi deimlo'n dda! Wyt ti'n teimlo'r un peth?
*(uh-doo, mah-een goo-nay-d ee vee day-m-loh-oon thah! weet teen tay-m-lohr een peth?)*
*Yes, it makes me feel good! Do you feel the same?*

**Person A:** Ydw, mae'n helpu gyda stress hefyd.
*(uh-doo, mah-een hel-pee guh-dah stress heh-vid)*
*Yes, it helps with stress too.*

## Offering Help and Support

**Person A:** Wyt ti angen unrhyw beth?
*(weet tee ang-en uhn-rhew beth?)*
*Do you need anything?*

**Person B:** Oes, allet ti brynu meddyginiaeth i fi?
*(oh-ess, aLl-et tee bruh-nee meth-ug-in-yah-eeth ee vee?)*
*Yes, could you buy medicine for me?*

**Person A:** Wrth gwrs! Beth wyt ti angen?
*(oorth goorss! beth weet tee ang-en?)*
*Of course! What do you need?*

---

**Person A:** Alla i wneud rhywbeth i helpu?
*(aLl-ah ee oo-nay-d rhew-beth ee hel-pee?)*
*Can I do something to help?*

**Person B:** Diolch, allet ti wneud paned i fi?
*(dee-olkh, aLl-et tee oo-nay-d pan-ed ee vee?)*
*Thanks, could you make me a cuppa?*

**Person A:** Dim problem!
*(dim prob-lem!)*
*No problem!*

---

**Person A:** Os wyt ti angen siarad, dw i yma.
*(oss weet tee ang-en shahr-ad, doo ee uh-mah)*
*If you need to talk, I'm here.*

**Person B:** Diolch yn fawr. Mae hynny'n golygu llawer.
*(dee-olkh uhn vah-oor. mah-ee huhn-een gol-uh-gee LLah-oo-er)*
*Thanks very much. That means a lot.*

## Practice Tips
- Practice symptoms vocabulary
- Learn body parts in Welsh
- Practice giving advice (dyliet ti, rhaid i ti)
- Be sensitive when practicing mental health topics
