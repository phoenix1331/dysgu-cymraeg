# Emergency Situations

> [!warning] Essential Safety Phrases
> In emergencies, knowing key Welsh phrases can be crucial. While most emergency services in Wales operate in English, many locals will help if you speak Welsh. These dialogues cover medical emergencies, police interactions, and urgent help situations.

**Emergency Numbers:**
- 999 or 112 - Police, Ambulance, Fire
- 111 - NHS Wales non-emergency medical advice

## Medical Emergencies

### Dialogue 1: Calling an Ambulance

**Person A:** Help! Mae angen ambiwlans!
*(HELP! my ANG-en am-bee-OO-lans!)*
*Help! We need an ambulance!*

**Operator:** Iawn, peidiwch â phoeni. Beth sy'n bod?
*(YOWN, PAYD-yookh ah PHOY-nee. beth seen BOHD?)*
*Okay, don't worry. What's wrong?*

**Person A:** Mae fy ffrind wedi cwympo. Mae hi wedi taro ei phen.
*(my vuh FFREEND WEH-dee KWIMP-oh. my hee WEH-dee TAR-oh ay PHEN)*
*My friend has fallen. She has hit her head.*

**Operator:** Ydy hi'n ymwybodol?
*(UH-dee heen um-wuh-BOD-ol?)*
*Is she conscious?*

**Person A:** Ydy, ond mae hi mewn poen.
*(UH-dee, ond my hee mewn POYN)*
*Yes, but she's in pain.*

**Operator:** Ble ydych chi'n union?
*(bleh UH-deekh kheen EEN-yon?)*
*Where exactly are you?*

**Person A:** Dyn ni ym Mharc y Rhath, wrth y llyn.
*(deen nee um MARK uh RHATH, oorth uh LHEEN)*
*We're in Roath Park, by the lake.*

**Operator:** Perffaith. Mae'r ambiwlans ar y ffordd. Arhoswch gyda hi.
*(per-FEYETH. my-r am-bee-OO-lans ar uh FFORDD. ar-HOS-ookh GUH-dah hee)*
*Perfect. The ambulance is on the way. Stay with her.*

### Dialogue 2: At the Hospital

**Nyrs:** Bore da. Beth yw'r broblem?
*(BOH-reh dah. beth you-r BROB-lem?)*
*Good morning. What's the problem?*

**Claf:** Mae pen tost ofnadwy gyda fi. A dw i'n teimlo'n sâl iawn.
*(my pen TOST ov-NAD-wee GUH-dah vee. ah doo een TAY-m-loh-oon SAHL YOWN)*
*I have a terrible headache. And I'm feeling very ill.*

**Nyrs:** Ers pryd?
*(erss PREED?)*
*Since when?*

**Claf:** Ers neithiwr. Mae'n gwaethygu.
*(erss NAYTH-eer. mine GWYTH-uh-gee)*
*Since last night. It's getting worse.*

**Nyrs:** Ydych chi wedi bod yn sâl? Chwydu?
*(UH-deekh khee WEH-dee bohd un SAHL? KHWEE-dee?)*
*Have you been sick? Vomiting?*

**Claf:** Ydw, sawl gwaith.
*(UH-doo, SOWL GWEYTH)*
*Yes, several times.*

**Nyrs:** Oes alergeddau gyda chi?
*(oyss al-er-GETH-eye GUH-dah khee?)*
*Do you have any allergies?*

**Claf:** Nac oes, dim alergeddau.
*(nak oyss, dim al-er-GETH-eye)*
*No, no allergies.*

**Nyrs:** Ydych chi'n cymryd unrhyw feddyginiaeth?
*(UH-deekh kheen KUM-rid uhn-RHEW veth-ug-IN-yeth?)*
*Are you taking any medication?*

**Claf:** Nac ydw, dim byd.
*(nak UH-doo, dim BEED)*
*No, nothing.*

**Nyrs:** Iawn. Bydd y meddyg yn eich gweld chi nawr.
*(YOWN. beeth uh METH-ig un aykh GWELD khee now-r)*
*Okay. The doctor will see you now.*

### Dialogue 3: Describing Symptoms

**Meddyg:** Sut alla i helpu chi heddiw?
*(seet ALL-ah ee HEL-pee khee HEH-thee-oo?)*
*How can I help you today?*

**Claf:** Mae fy stumog i'n brifo'n ofnadwy.
*(my vuh STUM-og een BREEV-ohn ov-NAD-wee)*
*My stomach hurts terribly.*

**Meddyg:** Ble mae'r boen yn union?
*(bleh my-r BOYN un EEN-yon?)*
*Where exactly is the pain?*

**Claf:** Fan hyn. Ar yr ochr dde.
*(van HEEN. ar eer OKH-r THEH)*
*Here. On the right side.*

**Meddyg:** Ydy'r boen yn waethach ar ôl bwyta?
*(UH-deer BOYN un GWYTH-akh ar OHL BOO-tah?)*
*Is the pain worse after eating?*

**Claf:** Ydy, yn enwedig ar ôl bwyd trwm.
*(UH-dee, un en-WED-ig ar OHL bood TROOM)*
*Yes, especially after heavy food.*

**Meddyg:** Oes tymheredd uchel gyda chi?
*(oyss tum-HER-eth EE-khel GUH-dah khee?)*
*Do you have a high temperature?*

**Claf:** Oes, dw i'n teimlo'n boeth iawn.
*(oyss, doo een TAY-m-lohn BOY-eth YOWN)*
*Yes, I feel very hot.*

**Meddyg:** Rhaid i fi archwilio chi. Gorweddwch fan hyn os gwelwch yn dda.
*(RHEID ee vee ar-KHWIL-yoh khee. gor-WETH-ookh van HEEN oss GWEL-ookh un THAH)*
*I need to examine you. Lie down here please.*

## Police Interactions

### Dialogue 4: Reporting a Crime

**Person A:** Esgusodwch fi, rhaid i fi riportio trosedd.
*(ess-giss-OD-ookh vee, RHEID ee vee ree-PORT-yoh TROSS-eth)*
*Excuse me, I need to report a crime.*

**Heddwas:** Wrth gwrs. Beth ddigwyddodd?
*(oorth GOORSS. beth thig-WITH-oth?)*
*Of course. What happened?*

**Person A:** Mae rhywun wedi dwyn fy mag i.
*(my RHEW-in WEH-dee DWEEN vuh MAG ee)*
*Someone has stolen my bag.*

**Heddwas:** Pryd ddigwyddodd hyn?
*(preed thig-WITH-oth HEEN?)*
*When did this happen?*

**Person A:** Tua hanner awr yn ôl, yn y ganolfan siopa.
*(tee-ah HAN-er OWR un OHL, un uh gan-OL-van SHOP-ah)*
*About half an hour ago, in the shopping centre.*

**Heddwas:** Beth oedd yn y bag?
*(beth OY-th un uh BAG?)*
*What was in the bag?*

**Person A:** Fy waled, fy ffôn, a fy allweddi.
*(vuh WAL-ed, vuh FFOHN, ah vuh aLL-weth-ee)*
*My wallet, my phone, and my keys.*

**Heddwas:** Weloch chi'r person?
*(WEL-okh kheer PER-son?)*
*Did you see the person?*

**Person A:** Do, dyn ifanc mewn hwdi du. Rhedodd e i ffwrdd.
*(doh, deen EEV-ank mewn HOO-dee DEE. RHED-oth eh ee FFOORH)*
*Yes, a young man in a black hoodie. He ran away.*

**Heddwas:** Iawn. Rhowch eich manylion i fi, ac fe wnawn ni ymchwilio.
*(YOWN. RHOW-kh aykh man-UL-yon ee vee, ak veh WNOWN nee um-KHWIL-yoh)*
*Okay. Give me your details, and we'll investigate.*

### Dialogue 5: Lost Item

**Person A:** Helô, dw i wedi colli fy mhlentyn!
*(hel-OH, doo ee WEH-dee KOLL-ee vuh MHLEN-tin!)*
*Hello, I've lost my child!*

**Swyddog:** Peidiwch â phoeni. Ble weloch chi nhw ddiwethaf?
*(PAYD-yookh ah PHOY-nee. bleh WEL-okh khee nhoo thee-WETH-av?)*
*Don't worry. Where did you last see them?*

**Person A:** Wrth y siop deganau, tua phum munud yn ôl.
*(oorth uh SHOP deg-AN-eye, tee-ah PHEEM MEEN-id un OHL)*
*By the toy shop, about five minutes ago.*

**Swyddog:** Faint yw oedran y plentyn?
*(VEYNT you OYD-ran uh PLEN-tin?)*
*How old is the child?*

**Person A:** Pump oed. Merch fach â gwallt brown a chôt goch.
*(PEEMP OYED. merkh VAKH ah GWALTH BROWN ah KHOHT GOKH)*
*Five years old. A little girl with brown hair and a red coat.*

**Swyddog:** Beth yw ei henw hi?
*(beth you ay HEN-oo hee?)*
*What's her name?*

**Person A:** Megan. Mae hi'n cario tedi brown.
*(MEG-an. my heen KAR-yoh TED-ee BROWN)*
*Megan. She's carrying a brown teddy.*

**Swyddog:** Iawn. Byddwn ni'n gwneud cyhoeddiad nawr. Arhoswch fan hyn.
*(YOWN. BEETH-oon neen GOO-nayd kuh-HOY-eth-yad now-r. ar-HOS-ookh van HEEN)*
*Okay. We'll make an announcement now. Wait here.*

## Accidents and Injuries

### Dialogue 6: Car Accident

**Person A:** Mae damwain wedi digwydd! Galwch ambiwlans!
*(my dam-WEYEN WEH-dee dig-WITH! GAL-ookh am-bee-OO-lans!)*
*There's been an accident! Call an ambulance!*

**Person B:** Beth ddigwyddodd?
*(beth thig-WITH-oth?)*
*What happened?*

**Person A:** Mae dau gar wedi gwrthdaro. Mae pobl wedi'u hanafu.
*(my DIE GAR WEH-dee GOORH-thar-oh. my POBL WEH-dee han-AV-ee)*
*Two cars have collided. People are injured.*

**Person B:** Ydyn nhw'n ymwybodol?
*(UH-deen nhoo-un um-wuh-BOD-ol?)*
*Are they conscious?*

**Person A:** Ydy un ohonyn nhw, ond mae'r llall yn anymwybodol.
*(UH-dee een oh-HON-in nhoo, ond my-r LHALL un an-um-wuh-BOD-ol)*
*One of them is, but the other is unconscious.*

**Person B:** Peidiwch â symud nhw. Dw i'n ffonio'r gwasanaethau brys nawr.
*(PAYD-yookh ah SUM-id nhoo. doo een FFON-yohr gwass-an-EYTH-eye BREESS now-r)*
*Don't move them. I'm calling the emergency services now.*

**Person A:** Iawn. Mae un gyrrwr yn gwaedu'n drwm o'i ben.
*(YOWN. my een GUH-roor un GWEY-deen TROOM oh-ee BEN)*
*Okay. One driver is bleeding heavily from his head.*

**Person B:** Oes rhywun gyda chi sy'n gallu rhoi cymorth cyntaf?
*(oyss RHEW-in GUH-dah khee seen GALL-ee RHOY KUM-orth KUN-tav?)*
*Is there anyone with you who can give first aid?*

### Dialogue 7: Seeking Help - Fire

**Person A:** Help! Tân! Mae tân yn y bloc fflatiau!
*(HELP! TAHN! my TAHN un uh BLOK FFLAT-yeye!)*
*Help! Fire! There's a fire in the block of flats!*

**Cymydog:** Ble mae'r tân?
*(bleh my-r TAHN?)*
*Where's the fire?*

**Person A:** Ar y trydydd llawr. Mae mwg du yn dod allan.
*(ar uh TRUH-ith LHOWR. my MOOG DEE un DOD aLL-AN)*
*On the third floor. Black smoke is coming out.*

**Cymydog:** Ydy pawb allan?
*(UH-dee POWB aLL-AN?)*
*Is everyone out?*

**Person A:** Dw i ddim yn siŵr. Dw i'n credu bod hen ddyn ar y pedwerydd llawr.
*(doo ee thim un SHEE-oor. doo een KRED-ee bohd hen THEEN ar uh ped-WER-ith LHOWR)*
*I'm not sure. I think there's an old man on the fourth floor.*

**Cymydog:** Rhaid i ni ffonio'r frigâd dân ar unwaith!
*(RHEID ee nee FFON-yohr vrig-AHD TAHN ar UN-weyth!)*
*We must call the fire brigade immediately!*

**Person A:** Dw i'n gwneud nawr. 999.
*(doo een GOO-nayd now-r. NINE NINE NINE)*
*I'm doing it now. 999.*

## Getting Help from Public

### Dialogue 8: Asking for Directions When Lost

**Person A:** Esgusodwch fi, dw i ar goll. Allwch chi helpu fi?
*(ess-giss-OD-ookh vee, doo ee ar GOLL. ALL-ookh khee HEL-pee vee?)*
*Excuse me, I'm lost. Can you help me?*

**Person B:** Wrth gwrs! Ble ydych chi eisiau mynd?
*(oorth GOORSS! bleh UH-deekh khee AY-shah MEEND?)*
*Of course! Where do you want to go?*

**Person A:** Dw i eisiau mynd i orsaf y trenau, ond dw i ddim yn gwybod ble mae hi.
*(doo ee AY-shah meend ee OR-sav uh TREN-eye, ond doo ee thim un GWUH-bod bleh my hee)*
*I want to go to the train station, but I don't know where it is.*

**Person B:** O, mae'n hawdd. Ewch yn syth ymlaen, yna troi i'r chwith wrth y goleuadau traffig.
*(oh, mine HOWTH. EH-ookh un SEETH um-LEYEN, UN-ah TROY eer KHWEETH oorth uh gol-AY-eye TRAFF-ig)*
*Oh, it's easy. Go straight ahead, then turn left at the traffic lights.*

**Person A:** Faint o bell yw e?
*(VEYNT oh BELL you eh?)*
*How far is it?*

**Person B:** Tua deng munud ar droed. Gallwch chi weld y cloc mawr.
*(tee-ah DENG MEEN-id ar DROYD. GALL-ookh khee weld uh KLOK MOW-r)*
*About ten minutes on foot. You can see the big clock.*

**Person A:** Diolch yn fawr iawn. Rydych chi wedi bod yn garedig iawn.
*(DEE-olkh un VOW-r YOWN. RUH-deekh khee WEH-dee bohd un gar-EH-dig YOWN)*
*Thank you very much. You've been very kind.*

### Dialogue 9: Asking for Language Help

**Person A:** Esgusodwch fi, dw i ddim yn siarad Cymraeg yn dda. Ydych chi'n siarad Saesneg?
*(ess-giss-OD-ookh vee, doo ee thim un SHAHR-ad kum-REYG un THAH. UH-deekh kheen SHAHR-ad SAYSS-neg?)*
*Excuse me, I don't speak Welsh well. Do you speak English?*

**Person B:** Ydw, wrth gwrs. Beth yw'r broblem?
*(UH-doo, oorth GOORSS. beth you-r BROB-lem?)*
*Yes, of course. What's the problem?*

**Person A:** Dw i angen cymorth. Mae fy ffrind yn sâl.
*(doo ee ANG-en KUM-orth. my vuh FFREEND un SAHL)*
*I need help. My friend is ill.*

**Person B:** Ble mae eich ffrind chi?
*(bleh my aykh FFREEND khee?)*
*Where is your friend?*

**Person A:** Yn y toiledau. Mae hi wedi llewygu.
*(un uh toy-LED-eye. my hee WEH-dee LHEH-wig-ee)*
*In the toilets. She has fainted.*

**Person B:** Iawn, dewch gyda fi. Awn ni nawr.
*(YOWN, DEH-ookh GUH-dah vee. OWN nee now-r)*
*Okay, come with me. We'll go now.*

## Practice Tips

- **Memorize key emergency phrases** - These could save a life
- **Practice pronunciation** - Clear speech is crucial in emergencies
- **Learn body parts** - Essential for describing injuries (see Module 03)
- **Know your address** - Be able to say where you are in Welsh
- **Don't panic** - Most emergency services in Wales speak English too
- **Stay calm** - Speaking slowly helps in stressful situations

## Important Notes

> [!tip] Language in Emergencies
> While these Welsh phrases are valuable, remember:
> - All emergency services (999/112) operate fully in English
> - Don't hesitate to speak English if you're more comfortable
> - The most important thing is getting help quickly
> - Many Welsh speakers will switch to English in emergencies if needed

> [!warning] When to Use Welsh
> Use Welsh emergency phrases when:
> - Speaking to members of the public
> - In Welsh-speaking communities
> - To demonstrate cultural respect
> - You're confident in your pronunciation
>
> But ALWAYS prioritize clear communication over language choice!
