# Discussion 15 - Social Events and Making Friends

> **Goal:** Practice extending and responding to invitations, making small talk at social gatherings, discussing Welsh cultural events, and building friendships in Welsh-speaking communities.

---

## Dialogue 1: Extending an Invitation

**Person A:** Helo! Dw i'n trefnu parti penblwydd nos Sadwrn. Hoffet ti ddod?
*(HEH-loh! doo een TREV-nee PAR-tee pen-BLOODH nohs SA-dorn. HOF-et tee thod?)*
*Hello! I'm organizing a birthday party on Saturday night. Would you like to come?*

**Person B:** Diolch am y gwahoddiad! Pryd mae'n dechrau?
*(DEE-olkh am uh gwah-HOD-yad! preed mine DEK-hry?)*
*Thanks for the invitation! When does it start?*

**Person A:** Mae'n dechrau am saith o'r gloch. Yn ein tŷ ni yn Gaerdydd.
*(mine DEK-hry am syth or glokh. uhn eyen tee nee uhn GY-er-deeth.)*
*It starts at seven o'clock. At our house in Cardiff.*

**Person B:** Gwych! Oes angen i fi ddod â rhywbeth?
*(gweekh! oys ANG-en ee vee thod ah REE-beth?)*
*Great! Do I need to bring anything?*

**Person A:** Dim ond ti dy hun! Ond os wyt ti eisiau, dere â diod.
*(deem ohnd tee duh heen! ohnd ohs weet tee AY-shy, DEH-reh ah DEE-od.)*
*Just yourself! But if you want, bring a drink.*

**Person B:** Iawn, wela i di nos Sadwrn 'te!
*(yown, WEH-la ee dee nohs SA-dorn teh!)*
*Okay, I'll see you Saturday night then!*

## Dialogue 2: RSVP - Accepting an Invitation

**Person A:** Gest ti'r e-bost am y cinio nos Wener?
*(gest TEER eh-bohst am uh KIN-yoh nohs WEN-er?)*
*Did you get the email about the dinner on Friday night?*

**Person B:** Do, diolch! Dw i'n edrych ymlaen. Faint o bobl sy'n dod?
*(doh, DEE-olkh! doo een ED-reekh uhm-LINE. vynt oh BOH-bul seen dohd?)*
*Yes, thanks! I'm looking forward to it. How many people are coming?*

**Person A:** Tua deg, mae'n debyg. Criw o ffrindiau o'r gwaith.
*(TEE-ah deg, mine DEH-big. KREE-oo oh FRIN-dy-eye or gwayth.)*
*About ten, probably. A bunch of friends from work.*

**Person B:** Perffaith! Oes unrhyw alergeddau bwyd ddylwn i wybod amdano?
*(per-FYTH! oys IN-hryo al-er-GEH-dy-eye boo-eed THUH-loon ee UH-bod am-DA-noh?)*
*Perfect! Are there any food allergies I should know about?*

**Person A:** Na, pawb yn bwyta popeth hyd y gwn i.
*(nah, powb uhn BOO-ita POH-peth heed uh goon ee.)*
*No, everyone eats everything as far as I know.*

**Person B:** Ardderchog. Wela i chi nos Wener!
*(ar-THER-khog. WEH-la ee khee nohs WEN-er!)*
*Excellent. See you Friday night!*

## Dialogue 3: RSVP - Declining Politely

**Person A:** Haia! Dan ni'n mynd i'r theatr nos Iau. Wyt ti'n ffansïo dod?
*(HY-ah! dan neen meend eer THEH-atr nohs YAH-ee. weet teen FAN-see-oh dohd?)*
*Hi! We're going to the theatre on Thursday night. Do you fancy coming?*

**Person B:** O, mae'n swnio'n wych, ond mae gen i ymrwymiad arall, yn anffodus.
*(oh, mine SOON-yohn weekh, ohnd my gen ee uhm-ROO-ee-myad AR-ahll, uhn an-THEE-dis.)*
*Oh, it sounds great, but I have another commitment, unfortunately.*

**Person A:** Dim problem! Efallai tro nesaf?
*(deem PROB-lem! eh-VAL-eye troh NEH-sav?)*
*No problem! Maybe next time?*

**Person B:** Yn bendant! Diolch am feddwl amdana i.
*(uhn ben-DANT! DEE-olkh am VETH-ool am-DAN-ah ee.)*
*Definitely! Thanks for thinking of me.*

**Person A:** Croeso! Mwynha dy noson.
*(KROY-soh! MOOYN-ha duh NOH-son.)*
*You're welcome! Enjoy your evening.*

## Dialogue 4: At a Party - Small Talk

**Person A:** Helo! Dw i ddim yn meddwl dan ni wedi cwrdd o'r blaen. Fi yw Siân.
*(HEH-loh! doo ee theem uhn METH-ool dan nee WEH-dee koo-erth or blyne. vee yoo SHAHN.)*
*Hello! I don't think we've met before. I'm Siân.*

**Person B:** Haia Siân! Carwyn dw i. Sut wyt ti'n nabod Megan?
*(HY-ah SHAHN! KAR-win doo ee. seet weet teen NAB-od MEG-an?)*
*Hi Siân! I'm Carwyn. How do you know Megan?*

**Person A:** Dan ni'n gweithio gyda'n gilydd. A ti?
*(dan neen GWAY-thyoh GUH-dahn GILL-eeth. ah tee?)*
*We work together. And you?*

**Person B:** Dan ni'n chwarae pêl-droed yn yr un tîm.
*(dan neen KHWA-ry pale-DROH-ed uhn uhr een teem.)*
*We play football on the same team.*

**Person A:** Gwych! Wyt ti'n byw yn yr ardal yma?
*(gweekh! weet teen beeo uhn uhr AR-dal UH-ma?)*
*Great! Do you live in this area?*

**Person B:** Ydw, ers tair blynedd bellach. Dw i wrth fy modd yma.
*(UH-doo, ers tire BLUH-neth BEL-lakh. doo ee oorth vuh mohth UH-ma.)*
*Yes, for three years now. I love it here.*

## Dialogue 5: Discussing Plans at a Gathering

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n ei wneud dros y penwythnos nesaf?
*(beth weet teen ay OONAY-d drohs uh pen-OOTH-nohs NEH-sav?)*
*What are you doing over the next weekend?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n mynd i Eisteddfod Genedlaethol ym Môn. Wyt ti erioed wedi bod?
*(doo een meend ee eye-STETH-vod gen-ed-LETH-ol uhm MOHN. weet tee ER-yod WEH-dee bohd?)*
*I'm going to the National Eisteddfod in Anglesey. Have you ever been?*

**Person A:** Naddo, ond dw i wedi clywed ei fod yn anhygoel!
*(NAD-oh, ohnd doo ee WEH-dee KLOO-ed ay vohd uhn an-HUH-gol!)*
*No, but I've heard it's amazing!*

**Person B:** Mae'n werth mynd! Lot o gerddoriaeth, celfyddyd, a bwyd. Ti eisiau dod gyda fi?
*(mine oo-erth meend! loht oh gerth-or-EYE-eth, kel-VUH-theed, ah boo-eed. tee AY-shy dohd GUH-dah vee?)*
*It's worth going! Lots of music, art, and food. Do you want to come with me?*

**Person A:** Hoffwn i'n fawr! Pryd wyt ti'n gadael?
*(HOF-oon een vowr! preed weet teen GAD-yl?)*
*I'd love to! When are you leaving?*

**Person B:** Bore Sadwrn, tua naw o'r gloch. Allet ti rannu'r gost petrol?
*(BOH-reh SA-dorn, TEE-ah now or glokh. AL-et tee HRAN-ee-r gohst PET-rol?)*
*Saturday morning, around nine o'clock. Could you share the petrol cost?*

**Person A:** Wrth gwrs! Diolch am y cynnig!
*(oorth goo-ers! DEE-olkh am uh KUHN-ig!)*
*Of course! Thanks for the offer!*

## Dialogue 6: Making Friends - Suggesting a Meetup

**Person A:** Dw i wedi mwynhau siarad â ti heno! Dylen ni gwrdd eto rhywbryd.
*(doo ee WEH-dee MOOYN-hy SY-arad ah tee HEH-noh! DUH-len nee koo-erth EH-toh HREEO-breed.)*
*I've enjoyed talking with you tonight! We should meet up again sometime.*

**Person B:** Baswn i'n hoffi hynny! Wyt ti'n ffansïo coffi rywbryd yr wythnos nesaf?
*(BAS-oon een HOF-fee HUH-nee! weet teen FAN-see-oh KOF-fee REEO-breed uhr OOTH-nohs NEH-sav?)*
*I'd like that! Do you fancy coffee sometime next week?*

**Person A:** Syniad da! Beth am ddydd Mercher? Dw i'n rhydd yn y prynhawn.
*(SUHN-yad dah! beth am theeth MER-kher? doo een hreeth uhn uh PRUHN-hown.)*
*Good idea! How about Wednesday? I'm free in the afternoon.*

**Person B:** Perffaith! Dw i'n gwybod lle da yn y dre. Tri o'r gloch?
*(per-FYTH! doo een GWUH-bod lleh dah uhn uh dreh. tree or glokh?)*
*Perfect! I know a good place in town. Three o'clock?*

**Person A:** Iawn. Anfona i destun i ti gyda'r manylion.
*(yown. an-VON-ah ee TES-tin ee tee GUH-dar man-UHL-yon.)*
*Okay. I'll send you a text with the details.*

**Person B:** Grêt! Edrych ymlaen!
*(grayt! ED-reekh uhm-LINE!)*
*Great! Looking forward to it!*

## Dialogue 7: At a Welsh Cultural Event

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n mwynhau'r cyngerdd?
*(weet teen MOOYN-hy-r kuhn-GERTH?)*
*Are you enjoying the concert?*

**Person B:** Ydw, mae'r côr yn anhygoel! Dw i ddim wedi clywed cerddoriaeth Gymreig o'r blaen.
*(UH-doo, my-r kohr uhn an-HUH-gol! doo ee theem WEH-dee KLOO-ed kerth-or-EYE-eth guhm-RAYG or blyne.)*
*Yes, the choir is amazing! I haven't heard Welsh music before.*

**Person A:** Mae gan Gymru draddodiad cerddorol cyfoethog iawn.
*(my gan GUHM-ree dra-THOD-yad kerth-OR-ol kuh-VOYTH-og yown.)*
*Wales has a very rich musical tradition.*

**Person B:** Dw i'n gallu gweld hynny! Oes mwy o ddigwyddiadau fel hyn?
*(doo een GAL-ee goo-eld HUH-nee! oys moy oh dig-with-YAD-eye vel heen?)*
*I can see that! Are there more events like this?*

**Person A:** Oes! Mae Dydd Gŵyl Dewi mis Mawrth, ac Eisteddfod yr Urdd yn yr haf.
*(oys! my deeth goo-eel DEH-wee mees MOWR-th, ahk eye-STETH-vod uhr EERTH uhn uhr hahv.)*
*Yes! There's St David's Day in March, and the Urdd Eisteddfod in summer.*

**Person B:** Rhaid i fi fynd! Sut alla i ddod o hyd i wybodaeth am ddigwyddiadau?
*(hryd ee vee veend! seet AL-ah ee thohd oh heed ee uh-BOH-deth am dig-with-YAD-eye?)*
*I must go! How can I find information about events?*

**Person A:** Edrycha ar wefan Cyngor Celfyddydau Cymru, neu dilyn cyfryngau cymdeithasol lleol.
*(ed-RUH-kha ar WEB-van KUHN-gor kel-VUH-theed-eye GUHM-ree, nay DIL-in kuh-VRUHN-guy kuhm-day-THAS-ol LLAY-ol.)*
*Check the Arts Council of Wales website, or follow local social media.*

## Dialogue 8: Exchanging Contact Information

**Person A:** Mae wedi bod yn bleser cwrdd â ti! Hoffet ti gyfnewid rhifau ffôn?
*(my WEH-dee bohd uhn BLEH-ser koo-erth ah tee! HOF-et tee guhv-NOO-id HREE-vy fohn?)*
*It's been a pleasure meeting you! Would you like to exchange phone numbers?*

**Person B:** Wrth gwrs! Beth yw dy rif di?
*(oorth goo-ers! beth yoo duh reev dee?)*
*Of course! What's your number?*

**Person A:** O saith pedwar dau wyth, tri pump un pedwar chwech.
*(oh syth PED-war dy oo-eeth, tree pimp een PED-war khoo-ekh.)*
*07428, 351466.*

**Person B:** Wedi'i gael. Anfonaf neges i ti nawr fel bod gen ti fy rhif i.
*(WEH-dee-eye gyl. an-VON-av NEG-es ee tee nowr vel bohd gen tee vuh hreev ee.)*
*Got it. I'll send you a message now so you have my number.*

**Person A:** Perffaith. Wyt ti ar gyfryngau cymdeithasol?
*(per-FYTH. weet tee ar guh-VRUHN-guy kuhm-day-THAS-ol?)*
*Perfect. Are you on social media?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i ar Instagram a Facebook. Chwilia am "@carwyn_jones".
*(UH-doo, doo ee ar IN-sta-gram ah FAYS-book. KHWIL-ya am "carwyn jones".)*
*Yes, I'm on Instagram and Facebook. Search for "@carwyn_jones".*

**Person A:** Gwych! Dilynaf di nawr. Cadwa mewn cysylltiad!
*(gweekh! dil-UH-nav dee nowr. KAD-wa mewn kuh-SUHL-tyad!)*
*Great! I'll follow you now. Keep in touch!*

## Dialogue 9: Following Up After Meeting

**Person A:** Haia! Gobeithio gest ti adref yn ddiogel neithiwr!
*(HY-ah! goh-BAY-thyoh gest tee AD-rev uhn thee-OG-el NAYTH-eer!)*
*Hi! Hope you got home safely last night!*

**Person B:** Do, diolch! Roedd yn noson wych. Diolch am y gwahoddiad!
*(doh, DEE-olkh! royd uhn NOH-son weekh. DEE-olkh am uh gwah-HOD-yad!)*
*Yes, thanks! It was a great evening. Thanks for the invitation!*

**Person A:** Croeso! Roedd yn braf dy gwrdd di. Rhaid i ni wneud rhywbeth eto cyn bo hir.
*(KROY-soh! royd uhn brahv duh koo-erth dee. hryd ee nee oo-nayd HREEO-beth EH-toh keen boh heer.)*
*You're welcome! It was nice to meet you. We must do something again soon.*

**Person B:** Yn bendant! Beth am bêl-droed neu gwis tafarn wythnos nesaf?
*(uhn ben-DANT! beth am pale-DROH-ed nay gwees TAV-arn OOTH-nohs NEH-sav?)*
*Definitely! How about football or a pub quiz next week?*

**Person A:** Syniad gwych! Mae cwis yn y Llew Coch nos Fawrth. Ti ffansïo hwnna?
*(SUHN-yad gweekh! my gwees uhn uh LLYEW kokh nohs VOWR-th. tee FAN-see-oh HOON-ah?)*
*Great idea! There's a quiz at the Red Lion on Tuesday night. Fancy that?*

**Person B:** Perffaith! Wela i di yno am wyth!
*(per-FYTH! WEH-la ee dee UH-noh am oo-eeth!)*
*Perfect! See you there at eight!*

## Cultural Notes: Welsh Social Customs

### 1. **Dydd Gŵyl Dewi (St David's Day) - March 1st**
- National day of Wales celebrating patron saint St David
- People wear daffodils or leeks
- Schools and communities hold Eisteddfodau (cultural competitions)
- Traditional foods: cawl (Welsh stew), Welsh cakes

### 2. **Eisteddfod**
- **National Eisteddfod**: Annual week-long festival (moves between North and South Wales)
- **Urdd Eisteddfod**: Youth festival in late May
- Competitions in poetry, music, dance, drama - all in Welsh
- Major social and cultural event for Welsh speakers

### 3. **Social Drinking Culture**
- Pub culture very important for socializing
- Common to meet friends at local pub ("tafarn leol")
- Pub quizzes ("cwis tafarn") popular midweek activity
- Rounds system: take turns buying drinks for the group

### 4. **Greetings and Farewells**
- Friendly and informal, especially in Welsh-speaking communities
- Common to hug or kiss on cheek among friends
- "Hwyl" and "Hwyl fawr" commonly used instead of "goodbye"
- "Pob lwc" (good luck) often used when parting

### 5. **Making Friends in Wales**
- Welsh communities generally warm and welcoming
- Showing interest in learning Welsh greatly appreciated
- Community events key to integration
- Sports (rugby, football) and music big social connectors

### 6. **Invitations**
- Generally relaxed and informal
- Not uncommon to be invited to someone's home early in friendship
- BYOB (bring your own bottle) common for house parties
- Punctuality more relaxed than some cultures (10-15 min late acceptable)

## Practice Tips

1. **Join Local Welsh Groups**: Look for "Clwb Cymraeg" or "Cymraeg i Oedolion" (Welsh for Adults) social groups
2. **Attend Cultural Events**: Check local listings for Eisteddfodau, concerts, Welsh film screenings
3. **Use Dating/Friend Apps**: Many have Welsh language options or Welsh-specific groups
4. **Visit Welsh Bookshops/Cafés**: Often hubs for Welsh speakers (e.g., Canolfan Gymraeg in Cardiff)
5. **Practice Small Talk**: Weather, rugby, local events - safe conversation starters
6. **Be Patient**: Welsh speakers appreciate efforts to learn, even if you make mistakes
7. **Exchange Languages**: Offer to help with English in exchange for Welsh practice
