# Food and Drink

## At a Café

**Person A:** Wyt ti eisiau mynd i gael coffi?
*(weet tee AY-shah meend ee GY-el KOFF-ee?)*
*Do you want to go get coffee?*

**Person B:** Syniad da! Ble hoffet ti fynd?
*(SUN-yad dah! bleh HOFF-et tee veend?)*
*Good idea! Where would you like to go?*

**Person A:** Beth am y caffi newydd yn y dre?
*(beth am uh KAFF-ee NEH-with un uh dreh?)*
*How about the new café in town?*

**Person B:** Iawn, awn ni!
*(YOWN, OWN nee!)*
*Okay, let's go!*

---

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n mynd i gael?
*(beth weet teen meend ee GY-el?)*
*What are you going to have?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n mynd i gael coffi du a theisen. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een meend ee GY-el KOFF-ee dee ah THAY-sen. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I'm going to have black coffee and cake. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i eisiau te a bisgedi.
*(doo ee AY-shah teh ah bis-GED-ee)*
*I want tea and biscuits.*

---

**Person A:** Ydy'r coffi'n dda?
*(uh-DEER koff-een THAH?)*
*Is the coffee good?*

**Person B:** Ydy, mae'n flasus iawn! Sut mae dy de di?
*(UH-dee, mine VLAS-iss YOWN! seet MY duh deh dee?)*
*Yes, it's very tasty! How is your tea?*

**Person A:** Mae'n hyfryd, diolch!
*(mine HUV-rid, DEE-olkh!)*
*It's lovely, thanks!*

## Meal Times

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n hoffi fwyta i ginio?
*(beth weet teen HOFF-ee VOO-tah ee KIN-yoh?)*
*What do you like to eat for lunch?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n hoffi brechdanau neu salad. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een HOFF-ee brekh-DAN-eye nay SAL-ad. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I like sandwiches or salad. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n hoffi cawl neu basta.
*(doo een HOFF-ee KOWL nay BASS-tah)*
*I like soup or pasta.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti wedi cael swper?
*(weet tee WEH-dee KY-el SOOP-er?)*
*Have you had supper?*

**Person B:** Ydw, ces i bysgod a sglodion. Oedd e'n flasus iawn! Wyt ti wedi bwyta?
*(UH-doo, kess ee BUSS-god ah SGLOD-yon. OY-th een VLAS-iss YOWN! weet tee WEH-dee BOO-tah?)*
*Yes, I had fish and chips. It was very tasty! Have you eaten?*

**Person A:** Nac ydw, dw i'n mynd i goginio nawr.
*(nak UH-doo, doo een meend ee gog-IN-yoh now-r)*
*No, I'm going to cook now.*

---

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n mynd i goginio heno?
*(beth weet teen meend ee gog-IN-yoh HEN-oh?)*
*What are you going to cook tonight?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n mynd i wneud cyrri. Wyt ti'n hoffi bwyd sbeis?
*(doo een meend ee OO-nayd KUH-ree. weet teen HOFF-ee boo-id SBAYSS?)*
*I'm going to make curry. Do you like spicy food?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i'n caru bwyd sbeis!
*(UH-doo, doo een KAR-ee boo-id SBAYSS!)*
*Yes, I love spicy food!*

## Food Preferences

**Person A:** Beth ydy dy hoff fwyd di?
*(beth UH-dee duh hoff VOO-id dee?)*
*What is your favourite food?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n caru pitsa! Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een KAR-ee PEET-sah! beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I love pizza! What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n hoffi bwyd Eidalaidd hefyd.
*(doo een HOFF-ee boo-id eye-dal-EYETH HEH-vid)*
*I like Italian food too.*

---

**Person A:** Oes alergeddau bwyd gyda ti?
*(oyss al-er-GETH-eye boo-id GUH-dah tee?)*
*Do you have food allergies?*

**Person B:** Oes, dw i'n alergaidd i gnau. Beth amdanat ti?
*(oyss, doo een al-er-GEYETH ee GNIGH. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*Yes, I'm allergic to nuts. What about you?*

**Person A:** Nac oes, does dim alergeddau gyda fi.
*(nak oyss, doyss dim al-er-GETH-eye GUH-dah vee)*
*No, I don't have any allergies.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n hoffi coginio?
*(weet teen HOFF-ee kog-IN-yoh?)*
*Do you like cooking?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n coginio bob dydd bron. Wyt ti'n hoffi coginio?
*(UH-doo, doo een kog-IN-yoh bob DEETH bron. weet teen HOFF-ee kog-IN-yoh?)*
*Yes, I cook almost every day. Do you like cooking?*

**Person A:** Ydw, ond does dim amser gyda fi'n aml.
*(UH-doo, ond doyss dim AM-ser GUH-dah veen am-l)*
*Yes, but I don't often have time.*

## At a Restaurant

**Person A:** Wyt ti eisiau mynd i fwyty heno?
*(weet tee AY-shah meend ee VOO-ee-tee HEN-oh?)*
*Do you want to go to a restaurant tonight?*

**Person B:** Oes syniad gyda ti ble?
*(oyss SUN-yad GUH-dah tee bleh?)*
*Do you have an idea where?*

**Person A:** Beth am y bwyty Tsieineaidd newydd?
*(beth am uh BOO-ee-tee chee-en-EYE-eyeth NEH-with?)*
*How about the new Chinese restaurant?*

**Person B:** Perffaith! Pryd?
*(per-FEYETH! preed?)*
*Perfect! When?*

---

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n mynd i archebu?
*(beth weet teen meend ee ar-KHEB-ee?)*
*What are you going to order?*

**Person B:** Dw i ddim yn siŵr. Beth wyt ti'n argymell?
*(doo ee thim un SHEE-oor. beth weet teen ar-GUM-eLl?)*
*I'm not sure. What do you recommend?*

**Person A:** Mae'r pysgod yma'n wych.
*(my-r PUSS-god UH-mahn WEEKH)*
*The fish here is excellent.*

**Person B:** Iawn, dw i'n mynd i drio hynny!
*(YOWN, doo een meend ee DREE-oh HUN-ee!)*
*Okay, I'm going to try that!*

## Practice Tips
- Practice ordering in Welsh
- Discuss your favorite foods
- Talk about dietary restrictions
- Use descriptive words (blasus, sych, poeth, oer)
