# Shopping and Errands

## At the Shop

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n mynd i'r siop heddiw?
*(weet teen meend eer SHOP HEH-thee-oo?)*
*Are you going to the shop today?*

**Person B:** Ydw, rhaid i fi brynu bwyd. Wyt ti angen unrhyw beth?
*(UH-doo, RHEID ee vee BRUH-nee BOOD. weet tee ANG-en uhn-RHEW beth?)*
*Yes, I have to buy food. Do you need anything?*

**Person A:** Oes, alli di brynu bara i fi os gweli di'n dda?
*(oyss, ALL-ee dee BRUH-nee BAR-ah ee vee oss GWEL-ee deen THAH?)*
*Yes, can you buy bread for me please?*

**Person B:** Wrth gwrs!
*(oorth GOORSS!)*
*Of course!*

---

**Person A:** Ble wyt ti'n siopa fel arfer?
*(bleh weet teen SHOP-ah vel AR-ver?)*
*Where do you shop usually?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n mynd i [supermarket name]. Mae'n rhad ac yn gyfleus. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een meend ee [supermarket name]. mine RHAD ak un GUV-layss. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I go to [supermarket name]. It's cheap and convenient. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n siopa ar-lein fel arfer. Mae'n haws!
*(doo een SHOP-ah ar-LEYEN vel AR-ver. mine HOWSS!)*
*I shop online usually. It's easier!*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti wedi bod yn siopa heddiw?
*(weet tee WEH-dee bohd un SHOP-ah HEH-thee-oo?)*
*Have you been shopping today?*

**Person B:** Ydw, es i i'r archfarchnad y bore 'ma. Roedd hi'n brysur iawn!
*(UH-doo, ess ee eer arkh-VARKH-nad uh BOR-eh mah. ROY-th heen BRUH-seer YOWN!)*
*Yes, I went to the supermarket this morning. It was very busy!*

**Person A:** Beth brynes di?
*(beth BRUH-ness dee?)*
*What did you buy?*

---

**Person A:** Faint gostiodd hynny?
*(VEYNT gost-YOTH HUN-ee?)*
*How much did that cost?*

**Person B:** Costiodd e ddeg punt. Oedd e'n fargen dda!
*(kost-YOTH eh THEG PEENT. OY-th een VAR-gen THAH!)*
*It cost ten pounds. It was a good bargain!*

**Person A:** Da iawn!
*(dah YOWN!)*
*Very good!*

## Making a Shopping List

**Person A:** Beth sydd angen i ni brynu?
*(beth seeth ang-en ee nee bruh-nee?)*
*What do we need to buy?*

**Person B:** Mae angen llaeth, bara, ac wyau. Beth arall?
*(mah-ee ang-en LLah-eeth, bar-ah, ak wee-ah-ee. beth ar-aLl?)*
*We need milk, bread, and eggs. What else?*

**Person A:** Oes caws gyda ni?
*(oh-ess kah-uss guh-dah nee?)*
*Do we have cheese?*

**Person B:** Nac oes, rhaid i ni brynu caws hefyd.
*(nak oh-ess, rhah-eed ee nee bruh-nee kah-uss heh-vid)*
*No, we have to buy cheese too.*

---

**Person A:** Oes rhestr siopa gyda ti?
*(oh-ess rhes-tr shop-ah guh-dah tee?)*
*Do you have a shopping list?*

**Person B:** Oes, dw i wedi sgrifennu popeth lawr. Wyt ti eisiau ychwanegu rhywbeth?
*(oh-ess, doo ee weh-dee sgriv-en-ee pop-eth lah-oor. weet tee ay-shah uhkh-wan-eg-ee rhew-beth?)*
*Yes, I've written everything down. Do you want to add anything?*

**Person A:** Oes, alli di ychwanegu coffi os gweli di'n dda?
*(oh-ess, aLl-ee dee uhkh-wan-eg-ee koff-ee oss gwel-ee deen thah?)*
*Yes, can you add coffee please?*

## Clothes Shopping

**Person A:** Dw i angen prynu dillad newydd.
*(doo ee ang-en pruh-nee diLl-ad neh-with)*
*I need to buy new clothes.*

**Person B:** Pa fath o ddillad?
*(pah vahth oh thiLl-ad?)*
*What kind of clothes?*

**Person A:** Dw i angen crys-t a jîns newydd. Wyt ti eisiau dod gyda fi?
*(doo ee ang-en kreess-tee ah jeens neh-with. weet tee ay-shah dohd guh-dah vee?)*
*I need a new t-shirt and jeans. Do you want to come with me?*

**Person B:** Iawn, pryd wyt ti'n mynd?
*(yown, preed weet teen mind?)*
*Okay, when are you going?*

---

**Person A:** Sut mae hwn yn edrych?
*(seet mah-ee hoon uhn ed-rikh?)*
*How does this look?*

**Person B:** Mae'n edrych yn dda! Wyt ti'n hoffi'r lliw?
*(mah-een ed-rikh uhn thah! weet teen hoff-eer LLee-oo?)*
*It looks good! Do you like the colour?*

**Person A:** Ydw, ond ydy e'n rhy fawr?
*(uh-doo, ond uh-dee een rhee vah-oor?)*
*Yes, but is it too big?*

**Person B:** Nac ydy, mae'n ffitio'n berffaith!
*(nak uh-dee, mah-een ffeet-yoh-oon ber-fah-eeth!)*
*No, it fits perfectly!*

---

**Person A:** Faint ydy hwn?
*(vah-eent uh-dee hoon?)*
*How much is this?*

**Person B:** Mae'n ddau ddeg punt. Wyt ti'n mynd i'w brynu?
*(mah-een thah-ee theg peent. weet teen mind ee-oo bruh-nee?)*
*It's twenty pounds. Are you going to buy it?*

**Person A:** Ydw, mae'n fargen dda!
*(uh-doo, mah-een var-gen thah!)*
*Yes, it's a good bargain!*

## At the Market

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n hoffi mynd i'r farchnad?
*(weet teen hoff-ee mind eer varkh-nad?)*
*Do you like going to the market?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n caru'r farchnad leol. Mae'r llysiau'n ffres iawn. Wyt ti'n mynd?
*(uh-doo, doo een kar-eer varkh-nad leh-ol. mah-eer LLuhs-yah-een ffress yown. weet teen mind?)*
*Yes, I love the local market. The vegetables are very fresh. Do you go?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i'n mynd bob wythnos.
*(uh-doo, doo een mind bob weeth-noss)*
*Yes, I go every week.*

---

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n mynd i brynu heddiw?
*(beth weet teen mind ee bruh-nee heh-thyoo?)*
*What are you going to buy today?*

**Person B:** Dw i eisiau ffrwythau a llysiau ffres. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo ee ay-shah ffroo-ee-thah-ee ah LLuhs-yah-ee ffress. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I want fresh fruit and vegetables. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n mynd i brynu caws a bara ffres.
*(doo een mind ee bruh-nee kah-uss ah bar-ah ffress)*
*I'm going to buy cheese and fresh bread.*

---

**Person A:** Ydy'r tomatos yn ffres?
*(uh-deer tom-at-oss uhn ffress?)*
*Are the tomatoes fresh?*

**Person B:** Ydyn, maen nhw wedi cael eu tyfu'n lleol.
*(uh-deen, mah-een nhoo weh-dee kah-el ay tuh-vee-oon LLeh-ol)*
*Yes, they've been grown locally.*

**Person A:** Gwych! Ga i bum tomato os gweli di'n dda?
*(gweekh! gah ee beem tom-at-oh oss gwel-ee deen thah?)*
*Great! Can I have five tomatoes please?*

## Running Errands

**Person A:** Oes pethau i wneud gyda ti heddiw?
*(oh-ess peth-ah-ee ee oo-nay-d guh-dah tee heh-thyoo?)*
*Do you have things to do today?*

**Person B:** Oes, rhaid i fi fynd i'r banc a'r swyddfa bost. Beth amdanat ti?
*(oh-ess, rhah-eed ee vee vind eer bank ahr swuh-thfah bost. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I have to go to the bank and the post office. What about you?*

**Person A:** Rhaid i fi fynd i'r fferyllfa a'r llyfrgell.
*(rhah-eed ee vee vind eer ffer-uhLl-fah ahr LLuh-vir-geLl)*
*I have to go to the pharmacy and the library.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti wedi mynd i'r banc eto?
*(weet tee weh-dee mind eer bank et-oh?)*
*Have you been to the bank yet?*

**Person B:** Nac ydw, dw i'n mynd yn nes ymlaen. Wyt ti wedi gorffen dy bethau?
*(nak uh-doo, doo een mind uhn ness uhm-lah-een. weet tee weh-dee gor-fen duh beth-ah-ee?)*
*No, I'm going later. Have you finished your things?*

**Person A:** Ydw, gorffennes i bopeth y bore 'ma.
*(uh-doo, gor-ffen-ess ee bop-eth uh bor-eh mah)*
*Yes, I finished everything this morning.*

---

**Person A:** Ble mae'r swyddfa bost agosaf?
*(bleh mah-eer swuh-thfah bost ag-oss-av?)*
*Where is the nearest post office?*

**Person B:** Mae un yn y stryd fawr. Wyt ti eisiau postio rhywbeth?
*(mah-ee een uhn uh streed vah-oor. weet tee ay-shah post-yoh rhew-beth?)*
*There's one in the high street. Do you want to post something?*

**Person A:** Ydw, rhaid i fi anfon parsel.
*(uh-doo, rhah-eed ee vee an-von par-sel)*
*Yes, I have to send a parcel.*

## Comparing Prices

**Person A:** Ydy hyn yn rhad?
*(uh-dee heen uhn rhahd?)*
*Is this cheap?*

**Person B:** Nac ydy, mae'n ddrud. Allet ti gael e'n rhatach yn rhywle arall.
*(nak uh-dee, mah-een thrid. aLl-et tee gah-el een rhat-akh uhn rhew-leh ar-aLl)*
*No, it's expensive. You could get it cheaper somewhere else.*

**Person A:** Ble ddylen i edrych?
*(bleh thuh-len ee ed-rikh?)*
*Where should I look?*

---

**Person A:** Pa un ydy'r rhataf?
*(pah een uh-deer rhat-av?)*
*Which one is the cheapest?*

**Person B:** Mae'r un yma'n rhatach na'r llall.
*(mah-eer een uh-mahn rhat-akh nahr LLaLl)*
*This one is cheaper than the other.*

**Person A:** Ond ydy'r ansawdd yr un peth?
*(ond uh-deer an-sah-ooth eer een peth?)*
*But is the quality the same?*

**Person B:** Ydy, mae'r ddau'n dda.
*(uh-dee, mah-eer thah-een thah)*
*Yes, both are good.*

---

**Person A:** Oes sêl ymlaen?
*(oh-ess sehl uhm-lah-een?)*
*Is there a sale on?*

**Person B:** Oes, mae popeth hanner pris heddiw!
*(oh-ess, mah-ee pop-eth han-er preess heh-thyoo!)*
*Yes, everything is half price today!*

**Person A:** Gwych! Gawn ni brynu mwy!
*(gweekh! gah-oon nee bruh-nee moy!)*
*Great! Let's buy more!*

## Online Shopping

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n siopa ar-lein yn aml?
*(weet teen shop-ah ar-lay-een uhn am-l?)*
*Do you shop online often?*

**Person B:** Ydw, mae'n gyfleus iawn. Wyt ti'n prynu pethau ar-lein?
*(uh-doo, mah-een guhv-lay-ss yown. weet teen pruh-nee peth-ah-ee ar-lay-een?)*
*Yes, it's very convenient. Do you buy things online?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i'n prynu popeth ar-lein bron!
*(uh-doo, doo een pruh-nee pop-eth ar-lay-een bron!)*
*Yes, I buy almost everything online!*

---

**Person A:** Pryd mae'r parsel yn cyrraedd?
*(preed mah-eer par-sel uhn kuhr-ah-eedh?)*
*When is the parcel arriving?*

**Person B:** Mae'n cyrraedd yfory, gobeithio. Wyt ti'n disgwyl parsel?
*(mah-een kuhr-ah-eedh uh-vor-ee, gob-ay-thyoh. weet teen dis-goo-il par-sel?)*
*It's arriving tomorrow, hopefully. Are you expecting a parcel?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dylai e gyrraedd heddiw.
*(uh-doo, duh-lah-ee eh guhr-ah-eedh heh-thyoo)*
*Yes, it should arrive today.*

## Practice Tips
- Use this at actual shops to practice
- Practice numbers and prices
- Learn vocabulary for items you regularly buy
- Practice polite requests (os gweli di'n dda, diolch yn fawr)
