# Talking About Your Day

## Morning Routines

**Person A:** Sut mae dy fore di wedi bod?
*(SEET my duh VOR-eh dee WEH-dee bohd?)*
*How has your morning been?*

**Person B:** Da iawn! Dw i wedi cael brecwast da. A ti?
*(dah YOWN! doo ee WEH-dee KY-el BREK-wast dah. ah tee?)*
*Very good! I've had a good breakfast. And you?*

**Person A:** Dw i wedi bod yn brysur. Beth wnest ti gael i frecwast?
*(doo ee WEH-dee bohd un BRUH-seer. beth OO-nest tee GY-el ee VREK-wast?)*
*I've been busy. What did you have for breakfast?*

**Person B:** Ces i dost a choffi. Beth am ti?
*(kess ee dost ah KHOFF-ee. beth am tee?)*
*I had toast and coffee. What about you?*

---

**Person A:** Pryd wnes di ddeffro bore 'ma?
*(preed OO-nest dee THEFF-roh BOR-eh mah?)*
*When did you wake up this morning?*

**Person B:** Deffres i am saith o'r gloch. Beth amdanat ti?
*(DEFF-ress ee am SAITH ohr GLOKH. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I woke up at seven o'clock. What about you?*

**Person A:** Deffres i am chwech. Roedd rhaid i fi fynd i'r gwaith yn gynnar.
*(DEFF-ress ee am KHWEKH. ROY-th RHEID ee vee veend eer GWEYTH un GUN-ar)*
*I woke up at six. I had to go to work early.*

## Daily Activities

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n mynd i wneud heddiw?
*(beth weet teen meend ee OO-nayd HEH-thee-oo?)*
*What are you going to do today?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n mynd i'r siop ac wedyn dw i'n mynd i weld ffrind. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een meend eer SHOP ak WEH-din doo een meend ee weld FFREEND. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I'm going to the shop and then I'm going to see a friend. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n gweithio tan bump, wedyn dw i'n mynd adref.
*(doo een GWAY-thee-oh tan BIMP, WEH-din doo een meend AD-rev)*
*I'm working until five, then I'm going home.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti wedi bod yn brysur?
*(weet tee WEH-dee bohd un BRUH-seer?)*
*Have you been busy?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i wedi bod yn glanhau'r tŷ drwy'r bore. Wyt ti wedi bod yn gwneud rhywbeth?
*(UH-doo, doo ee WEH-dee bohd un glan-HY-eer tee DROO-eer BOR-eh. weet tee WEH-dee bohd un GOO-nayd RHEW-beth?)*
*Yes, I've been cleaning the house all morning. Have you been doing anything?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i wedi bod yn siopa.
*(UH-doo, doo ee WEH-dee bohd un SHOP-ah)*
*Yes, I've been shopping.*

## Evening Check-in

**Person A:** Sut oedd dy ddiwrnod di?
*(seet OY-th duh THEE-oor-nod dee?)*
*How was your day?*

**Person B:** Roedd e'n dda, ond dw i wedi blino nawr. Beth am dy ddiwrnod di?
*(ROY-th een THAH, ond doo ee WEH-dee BLEE-noh now-r. beth am duh THEE-oor-nod dee?)*
*It was good, but I'm tired now. What about your day?*

**Person A:** Roedd hi'n brysur iawn, ond roedd e'n dda.
*(ROY-th heen BRUH-seer YOWN, ond ROY-th een THAH)*
*It was very busy, but it was good.*

---

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n mynd i wneud heno?
*(beth weet teen meend ee OO-nayd HEN-oh?)*
*What are you going to do tonight?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n mynd i ymlacio a gwylio'r teledu. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een meend ee um-LACH-oh ah gwil-YOHR tel-EH-dee. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I'm going to relax and watch television. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n mynd i ddarllen llyfr.
*(doo een meend ee THAR-LLen LLU-veer)*
*I'm going to read a book.*

---

**Person A:** Pryd wyt ti'n mynd i'r gwely?
*(preed weet teen meend eer GWEL-ee?)*
*When are you going to bed?*

**Person B:** Fel arfer, dw i'n mynd tua deg o'r gloch. A ti?
*(vel AR-ver, doo een meend tee-ah DEG ohr GLOKH. ah tee?)*
*Usually, I go around ten o'clock. And you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n mynd tua un ar ddeg fel arfer.
*(doo een meend tee-ah een ar THEG vel AR-ver)*
*I go around eleven usually.*

## Practice Tips
- Use past, present, and future tenses
- Add details about your actual day
- Practice asking follow-up questions
