# Work and Study

## Talking About Work

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n wneud i waith?
*(beth weet teen OO-nayd ee WEYTH?)*
*What do you do for work?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n athro. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een ATH-roh. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I'm a teacher. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n gweithio mewn swyddfa.
*(doo een GWAY-thee-oh mew-in SWUH-thfah)*
*I work in an office.*

---

**Person A:** Ble wyt ti'n gweithio?
*(bleh weet teen GWAY-thee-oh?)*
*Where do you work?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n gweithio yn [place]. Ers pryd wyt ti yn dy swydd?
*(doo een GWAY-thee-oh un [place]. erss preed weet tee un duh SWOYTH?)*
*I work in [place]. How long have you been in your job?*

**Person A:** Dw i wedi bod yno ers tair blynedd.
*(doo ee WEH-dee bohd UN-oh erss TEYER BLUN-eth)*
*I've been there for three years.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n mwynhau dy waith?
*(weet teen MWIN-high duh WEYTH?)*
*Do you enjoy your work?*

**Person B:** Ydw, mae'n ddiddorol iawn. Ond weithiau mae'n brysur iawn! Wyt ti'n hoffi dy waith?
*(UH-doo, mine THEE-thor-ol YOWN. ond way-THEE-eye mine BRUH-seer YOWN! weet teen HOFF-ee duh WEYTH?)*
*Yes, it's very interesting. But sometimes it's very busy! Do you like your work?*

**Person A:** Ydw, mae'n iawn. Mae fy nghyd-weithwyr yn neis.
*(UH-doo, mine YOWN. my vuh NGHUD-way-thee-oor un NAYSS)*
*Yes, it's okay. My colleagues are nice.*

---

**Person A:** Pa fath o waith wyt ti'n gwneud?
*(pah VATH oh WEYTH weet teen GOO-nayd?)*
*What kind of work do you do?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n gweithio gyda chyfrifiaduron. Dw i'n datblygu meddalwedd.
*(doo een GWAY-thee-oh GUH-dah khuh-vriv-yad-EER-on. doo een dat-BLUH-gee meth-AL-weth)*
*I work with computers. I develop software.*

**Person A:** Swnio'n ddiddorol!
*(SWON-yoh-oon THEE-thor-ol!)*
*Sounds interesting!*

## Daily Work Routine

**Person A:** Pryd wyt ti'n dechrau gweithio?
*(preed weet teen DEKH-rye GWAY-thee-oh?)*
*When do you start working?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n dechrau am naw o'r gloch. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een DEKH-rye am NOW ohr GLOKH. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I start at nine o'clock. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n dechrau am wyth.
*(doo een DEKH-rye am WEETH)*
*I start at eight.*

---

**Person A:** Faint o oriau wyt ti'n gweithio?
*(VEYNT oh OR-yeye weet teen GWAY-thee-oh?)*
*How many hours do you work?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n gweithio saith awr y dydd. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een GWAY-thee-oh SAITH OWR uh DEETH. beth am-DAN-at tee?)*
*I work seven hours a day. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n gweithio wyth awr fel arfer.
*(doo een gway-thyoh weeth ah-oor vel ar-ver)*
*I work eight hours usually.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n gweithio o gartref?
*(weet teen gway-thyoh oh gar-trev?)*
*Do you work from home?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n gweithio o gartref ddau ddiwrnod yr wythnos. Beth amdanat ti?
*(uh-doo, doo een gway-thyoh oh gar-trev thah-ee thee-oor-nod eer weeth-noss. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I work from home two days a week. What about you?*

**Person A:** Nac ydw, dw i'n mynd i'r swyddfa bob dydd.
*(nak uh-doo, doo een mind eer swuh-thfah bob deeth)*
*No, I go to the office every day.*

---

**Person A:** Sut oedd dy ddiwrnod gwaith heddiw?
*(seet oh-eedh duh thee-oor-nod gwah-eeth heh-thyoo?)*
*How was your work day today?*

**Person B:** Roedd e'n brysur iawn! Roedd llawer o gyfarfodydd gyda fi. Sut oedd dy ddiwrnod di?
*(roh-eedh een bruh-sir yown! roh-eedh LLah-oo-er oh guh-var-vod-eeth guh-dah vee. seet oh-eedh duh thee-oor-nod dee?)*
*It was very busy! I had lots of meetings. How was your day?*

**Person A:** Roedd e'n dawel heddiw, diolch byth!
*(roh-eedh een dah-oo-el heh-thyoo, dee-olkh beeth!)*
*It was quiet today, thank goodness!*

## Colleagues and Work Environment

**Person A:** Ydy dy gyd-weithwyr yn neis?
*(uh-dee duh guhd-way-thyoor uhn nay-ss?)*
*Are your colleagues nice?*

**Person B:** Ydyn, maen nhw'n wych! Dyn ni'n tîm da. Beth am dy gyd-weithwyr di?
*(uh-deen, mah-een nhoo-oon weekh! deen neen teem dah. beth am duh guhd-way-thyoor dee?)*
*Yes, they're great! We're a good team. What about your colleagues?*

**Person A:** Maen nhw'n iawn. Rhai yn well nag eraill!
*(mah-een nhoo-oon yown. rhah-ee uhn well nag er-ah-eeLl!)*
*They're okay. Some better than others!*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n hoffi dy fos?
*(weet teen hoff-ee duh voss?)*
*Do you like your boss?*

**Person B:** Ydw, mae hi'n dda iawn. Mae hi'n gefnogol. Beth am dy fos di?
*(uh-doo, mah-ee heen thah yown. mah-ee heen gev-nog-ol. beth am duh voss dee?)*
*Yes, she's very good. She's supportive. What about your boss?*

**Person A:** Mae e'n iawn, ond mae'n galed weithiau.
*(mah-ee een yown, ond mah-een gal-ed way-thyah-ee)*
*He's okay, but he's tough sometimes.*

---

**Person A:** Oes digwyddiadau cymdeithasol yn y gwaith?
*(oh-ess dig-wuh-thyad-yah-ee kuhm-day-ee-thas-ol uhn uh gwah-eeth?)*
*Are there social events at work?*

**Person B:** Oes, dyn ni'n mynd am fwyd bob mis. Wyt ti'n gwneud pethau cymdeithasol gyda dy gyd-weithwyr?
*(oh-ess, deen neen mind am voo-id bob meess. weet teen goo-nay-d peth-ah-ee kuhm-day-ee-thas-ol guh-dah duh guhd-way-thyoor?)*
*Yes, we go for food every month. Do you do social things with your colleagues?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dyn ni'n mynd i'r dafarn ar nos Wener weithiau.
*(uh-doo, deen neen mind eer dav-arn ar noss wen-er way-thyah-ee)*
*Yes, we go to the pub on Friday nights sometimes.*

## Career and Ambitions

**Person A:** Beth oeddet ti eisiau bod pan oeddet ti'n blentyn?
*(beth oh-eed-et tee ay-shah bohd pan oh-eed-et teen plen-tin?)*
*What did you want to be when you were a child?*

**Person B:** Roeddwn i eisiau bod yn feddyg! Beth amdanat ti?
*(roh-eed-oon ee ay-shah bohd uhn veth-ig! beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I wanted to be a doctor! What about you?*

**Person A:** Roeddwn i eisiau bod yn athro.
*(roh-eed-oon ee ay-shah bohd uhn ath-roh)*
*I wanted to be a teacher.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti eisiau newid swydd?
*(weet tee ay-shah neh-wid swoith?)*
*Do you want to change jobs?*

**Person B:** Nac ydw, dw i'n hapus lle dw i. Beth amdanat ti?
*(nak uh-doo, doo een hap-iss LLeh doo ee. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*No, I'm happy where I am. What about you?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i'n edrych am swydd newydd ar hyn o bryd.
*(uh-doo, doo een ed-rikh am swoith neh-with ar heen oh breed)*
*Yes, I'm looking for a new job at the moment.*

---

**Person A:** Beth hoffet ti wneud yn y dyfodol?
*(beth hoff-et tee oo-nay-d uhn uh duh-vod-ol?)*
*What would you like to do in the future?*

**Person B:** Hoffwn i gael fy musnes fy hun un diwrnod. Beth am ti?
*(hoff-oon ee gah-el vuh miss-ness vuh heen een dee-oor-nod. beth am tee?)*
*I'd like to have my own business one day. What about you?*

**Person A:** Hoffwn i deithio mwy gyda fy ngwaith.
*(hoff-oon ee day-thyoh moy guh-dah vuh ngwah-eeth)*
*I'd like to travel more with my work.*

## Studying and Learning

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n astudio ar hyn o bryd?
*(weet teen ast-id-yoh ar heen oh breed?)*
*Are you studying at the moment?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n gwneud cwrs gyda'r nos. Beth amdanat ti?
*(uh-doo, doo een goo-nay-d koorss guh-daar noss. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I'm doing an evening course. What about you?*

**Person A:** Nac ydw, ond dw i eisiau dechrau cwrs Cymraeg.
*(nak uh-doo, ond doo ee ay-shah dekh-rah-ee koorss kuhm-rah-eeg)*
*No, but I want to start a Welsh course.*

---

**Person A:** Beth wnes di astudio yn y brifysgol?
*(beth oo-ness dee ast-id-yoh uhn uh briv-uhs-gol?)*
*What did you study at university?*

**Person B:** Astudies i fusnes. Beth astudies di?
*(ast-id-yess ee viss-ness. beth ast-id-yess dee?)*
*I studied business. What did you study?*

**Person A:** Astudies i gyfrifiadureg.
*(ast-id-yess ee guh-vriv-yad-eer-eg)*
*I studied computer science.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n dysgu iaith arall?
*(weet teen duhs-gee yah-eeth ar-aLl?)*
*Are you learning another language?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n dysgu Cymraeg! Wyt ti'n siarad ieithoedd eraill?
*(uh-doo, doo een duhs-gee kuhm-rah-eeg! weet teen shahr-ad yay-thoh-eedh er-ah-eeLl?)*
*Yes, I'm learning Welsh! Do you speak other languages?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i'n siarad ychydig o Ffrangeg.
*(uh-doo, doo een shahr-ad uhkh-uh-dig oh ffrang-eg)*
*Yes, I speak a little French.*

---

**Person A:** Sut mae'r dysgu'n mynd?
*(seet mah-eer duhs-geen mind?)*
*How is the learning going?*

**Person B:** Mae'n mynd yn dda, ond mae'n anodd weithiau! Sut mae'n mynd gyda ti?
*(mah-een mind uhn thah, ond mah-een an-oth way-thyah-ee! seet mah-een mind guh-dah tee?)*
*It's going well, but it's difficult sometimes! How is it going with you?*

**Person A:** Yr un peth! Ond dw i'n mwynhau!
*(eer een peth! ond doo een moy-nhah-ee!)*
*Same! But I'm enjoying it!*

## Work-Life Balance

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n cael digon o amser i ymlacio?
*(weet teen kah-el dig-on oh am-ser ee uhm-lak-yoh?)*
*Do you get enough time to relax?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n lwcus. Mae fy ngwaith yn hyblyg. Beth amdanat ti?
*(uh-doo, doo een loo-kiss. mah-ee vuh ngwah-eeth uhn huhb-lig. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I'm lucky. My work is flexible. What about you?*

**Person A:** Nac ydw, dw i'n gweithio'n hwyr yn aml.
*(nak uh-doo, doo een gway-thyoh-oon hoo-eer uhn am-l)*
*No, I work late often.*

---

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n gwneud ar ôl gwaith?
*(beth weet teen goo-nay-d ar ohl gwah-eeth?)*
*What do you do after work?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n mynd i'r gampfa neu'n coginio. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een mind eer gam-pfah nay-oon kog-in-yoh. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I go to the gym or cook. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n ymlacio o flaen y teledu fel arfer!
*(doo een uhm-lak-yoh oh vlah-een uh tel-eh-dee vel ar-ver!)*
*I relax in front of the TV usually!*

## Practice Tips
- Adapt conversations to your real job or studies
- Practice job-related vocabulary in your field
- Discuss work challenges and achievements
- Use past tense to talk about previous jobs or education
