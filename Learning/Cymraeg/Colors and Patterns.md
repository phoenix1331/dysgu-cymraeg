# Colors and Patterns (Lliwiau a Phatrymau)

> [!tip] Mutations with Colors
> Colors are adjectives and follow nouns in Welsh. They undergo soft mutation after feminine nouns!

## Basic Colors (Lliwiau Sylfaenol)

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| coch | KOKH | red |
| glas | GLAHS | blue |
| gwyrdd | GOO-eerth | green |
| melyn | MEL-in | yellow |
| oren | OR-en | orange |
| piws | PEWS | purple |
| pinc | PINK | pink |
| brown | BROWN | brown |
| du | DEE | black |
| gwyn | GWIN | white |
| llwyd | HLOOYD | grey |

## With Masculine Nouns

When a color comes after a masculine noun, it stays the same:

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| crys coch | KRISS KOKH | red shirt |
| bag du | BAG DEE | black bag |
| het las | HET LAHS | blue hat (exception - 'glas' softens to 'las') |
| car gwyn | KAR GWIN | white car |

## With Feminine Nouns

After feminine nouns, colors undergo **soft mutation** (if they can):

| Welsh | Phonetic | English | Mutation |
|-------|----------|---------|----------|
| côt goch | KOHT GOKH | red coat | coch → goch |
| sgert las | SGERT LAHS | blue skirt | glas → las |
| cadair frown | KAD-eye-r VROWN | brown chair | brown → frown |
| ffrog wen | FROG WEN | white dress | gwyn → wen |
| siwmper ddu | SHOOM-per THEE | black jumper | du → ddu |

> [!important] Common Mutations
> - coch → goch (c → g)
> - glas → las (g → disappears)
> - gwyrdd → wyrdd (gw → w)
> - melyn → felyn (m → f)
> - brown → frown (b → f)
> - gwyn → wen (gw → w)
> - du → ddu (d → dd)
> - piws → biws (p → b)
> - pinc → binc (p → b)

## Patterns (Patrymau)

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| patrwm | PAT-room | pattern |
| streipiau | STRAY-pee-eye | stripes |
| dotiau | DOT-yee | dots / polka dots |
| blodau | BLOD-eye | flowers / floral |
| sieciau | SHEK-yee | checks |
| plaen | PLINE | plain |
| patrymog | pat-RUH-mog | patterned |

### Examples

```
crys streipiau
KRISS STRAY-pee-eye
striped shirt

ffrog flodau
FROG VLOD-eye
floral dress (blodau → flodau after feminine)

trowsus plaen
TROW-siss PLINE
plain trousers
```

## Useful Phrases

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Pa liw yw hwn? | PAH HLEEW you HOON? | What color is this? (m) |
| Pa liw yw hon? | PAH HLEEW you HON? | What color is this? (f) |
| Pa liw wyt ti'n hoffi? | PAH HLEEW weet teen HOF-fee? | What color do you like? |
| Dw i'n hoffi coch | DOO een HOF-fee KOKH | I like red |
| Beth yw dy hoff liw? | BEHTH you dee HOFF HLEEW? | What's your favorite color? |
| Fy hoff liw yw... | VUH HOFF HLEEW you... | My favorite color is... |
| Oes gennych chi hwn mewn glas? | OYSS gen-ukh khee HOON MEWN GLAHS? | Do you have this in blue? (m) |
| Oes gennych chi hon mewn coch? | OYSS gen-ukh khee HON MEWN KOKH? | Do you have this in red? (f) |

## Color Idioms & Expressions

| Welsh | Phonetic | English | Literal |
|-------|----------|---------|---------|
| glas y bore | GLAHS uh BOR-eh | dawn | blue of the morning |
| coch fel tomato | KOKH vel tom-AH-toh | red as a tomato | red like tomato |
| du fel y nos | DEE vel uh NOHS | black as night | black like the night |
| gwyn fel eira | GWIN vel AY-ra | white as snow | white like snow |

## Color + Body = Adjective

Some colors combine with body parts to make adjectives:

| Welsh | Phonetic | English | Meaning |
|-------|----------|---------|---------|
| gwyrddlas | goo-eerth-LAHS | green-blue | turquoise |
| gwinau | gwin-EYE | white (hair) | grey/white-haired |
| llwyd | HLOOYD | grey | also means "sad" |

## Asking About Preferences

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Wyt ti'n hoffi coch? | OO-eet teen HOF-fee KOKH? | Do you like red? |
| Ydw, dw i'n hoffi coch | UH-doo, DOO een HOF-fee KOKH | Yes, I like red |
| Nac ydw, dw i ddim yn hoffi coch | NAK UH-doo, DOO ee THEEM un HOF-fee KOKH | No, I don't like red |
| Pa un wyt ti'n hoffi? Y glas neu'r gwyrdd? | PAH EEN weet teen HOF-fee? Uh GLAHS nair GOO-eerth? | Which do you like? The blue or the green? |
