# Travel and Transport

## Daily Commute

**Person A:** Sut wyt ti'n mynd i'r gwaith?
*(seet weet teen mind eer gwah-eeth?)*
*How do you get to work?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n mynd ar y bws. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een mind ar uh buss. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I go on the bus. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n gyrru fy nghar.
*(doo een guhr-ee vuh ngh-ar)*
*I drive my car.*

---

**Person A:** Faint o amser mae'n cymryd?
*(vah-eent oh am-ser mah-een kuhm-rid?)*
*How much time does it take?*

**Person B:** Mae'n cymryd hanner awr fel arfer. Beth am dy daith di?
*(mah-een kuhm-rid han-er ah-oor vel ar-ver. beth am duh dah-eeth dee?)*
*It takes half an hour usually. What about your journey?*

**Person A:** Mae'n cymryd ugain munud os does dim traffig.
*(mah-een kuhm-rid ee-gah-een min-id oss doh-ess dim traff-ig)*
*It takes twenty minutes if there's no traffic.*

---

**Person A:** Oedd y traffig yn wael heddiw?
*(oh-eedh uh traff-ig uhn wah-el heh-thyoo?)*
*Was the traffic bad today?*

**Person B:** Oedd, roedd e'n ofnadwy! Roeddwn i'n hwyr. Sut oedd dy daith di?
*(oh-eedh, roh-eedh een ov-nad-wee! roh-eed-oon een hoo-eer. seet oh-eedh duh dah-eeth dee?)*
*Yes, it was terrible! I was late. How was your journey?*

**Person A:** Roedd hi'n iawn, diolch byth.
*(roh-eedh heen yown, dee-olkh beeth)*
*It was okay, thank goodness.*

## Public Transport

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n defnyddio trafnidiaeth gyhoeddus?
*(weet teen dev-nuh-thyoh trav-nid-yah-eeth guh-hoy-eth-iss?)*
*Do you use public transport?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n mynd ar y trên bob dydd. Wyt ti?
*(uh-doo, doo een mind ar uh tren bob deeth. weet tee?)*
*Yes, I go on the train every day. Do you?*

**Person A:** Nac ydw, does dim gwasanaeth da yma.
*(nak uh-doo, doh-ess dim gwas-an-ah-eeth dah uh-mah)*
*No, there's no good service here.*

---

**Person A:** Ble mae'r orsaf fysiau agosaf?
*(bleh mah-eer or-sav vuhs-yah-ee ag-oss-av?)*
*Where is the nearest bus station?*

**Person B:** Mae hi ar waelod y stryd. Pa fws wyt ti angen?
*(mah-ee hee ar wah-ee-lod uh streed. pah vuss weet tee ang-en?)*
*It's at the bottom of the street. Which bus do you need?*

**Person A:** Dw i angen y rhif tri. Pryd mae'n cyrraedd?
*(doo ee ang-en uh rheev tree. preed mah-een kuhr-ah-eedh?)*
*I need number three. When does it arrive?*

**Person B:** Mewn pum munud, dw i'n meddwl.
*(mew-in peem min-id, doo een meth-ool)*
*In five minutes, I think.*

---

**Person A:** Faint ydy tocyn bws?
*(vah-eent uh-dee tok-in buss?)*
*How much is a bus ticket?*

**Person B:** Mae'n ddwy bunt am un ffordd. Wyt ti eisiau tocyn dydd?
*(mah-een thoo-ee beent am een ffordd. weet tee ay-shah tok-in deeth?)*
*It's two pounds for one way. Do you want a day ticket?*

**Person A:** Oes, faint ydy tocyn dydd?
*(oh-ess, vah-eent uh-dee tok-in deeth?)*
*Yes, how much is a day ticket?*

**Person B:** Mae'n bum punt.
*(mah-een beem peent)*
*It's five pounds.*

---

**Person A:** Ydy'r trên yn brydlon fel arfer?
*(uh-deer tren uhn bruhd-lon vel ar-ver?)*
*Is the train on time usually?*

**Person B:** Ydy, fel arfer. Ond weithiau mae'n hwyr.
*(uh-dee, vel ar-ver. ond way-thyah-ee mah-een hoo-eer)*
*Yes, usually. But sometimes it's late.*

**Person A:** Ydy e'n hwyr heddiw?
*(uh-dee een hoo-eer heh-thyoo?)*
*Is it late today?*

**Person B:** Nac ydy, mae ar amser.
*(nak uh-dee, mah-ee ar am-ser)*
*No, it's on time.*

## Planning a Journey

**Person A:** Sut galla i fynd i [place]?
*(seet gaLl-ah ee vind ee [place]?)*
*How can I get to [place]?*

**Person B:** Gallet ti fynd ar y trên neu'r bws. Mae'r trên yn gyflymach.
*(gaLl-et tee vind ar uh tren nay-oor buss. mah-eer tren uhn guhv-luhm-akh)*
*You could go on the train or the bus. The train is faster.*

**Person A:** Faint mae'n costio?
*(vah-eent mah-een kost-yoh?)*
*How much does it cost?*

---

**Person A:** Pryd mae'r trên nesaf?
*(preed mah-eer tren ness-av?)*
*When is the next train?*

**Person B:** Gad i fi edrych... Mae'n gadael am ddeg o'r gloch.
*(gahd ee vee ed-rikh... mah-een gad-ah-el am theg or glokh)*
*Let me look... It leaves at ten o'clock.*

**Person A:** Faint o amser mae'n cymryd?
*(vah-eent oh am-ser mah-een kuhm-rid?)*
*How much time does it take?*

**Person B:** Tua awr a hanner.
*(tee-ah ah-oor ah han-er)*
*About an hour and a half.*

---

**Person A:** Oes rhaid i fi newid trenau?
*(oh-ess rhah-eed ee vee neh-wid tren-ah-ee?)*
*Do I have to change trains?*

**Person B:** Oes, rhaid i ti newid yng Nghaerdydd.
*(oh-ess, rhah-eed ee tee neh-wid uhng ngah-eer-deeth)*
*Yes, you have to change in Cardiff.*

**Person A:** Faint o amser sydd gyda fi i newid?
*(vah-eent oh am-ser seeth guh-dah vee ee neh-wid?)*
*How much time do I have to change?*

**Person B:** Deg munud. Ddylet ti fod yn iawn.
*(deg min-id. thuh-let tee vohd uhn yown)*
*Ten minutes. You should be okay.*

## Talking About Holidays

**Person A:** Wyt ti wedi bod ar wyliau eleni?
*(weet tee weh-dee bohd ar wuhl-yah-ee el-en-ee?)*
*Have you been on holiday this year?*

**Person B:** Ydw, es i i Sbaen fis diwethaf. Wyt ti wedi bod i unrhyw le?
*(uh-doo, ess ee ee sbah-een veess dee-weth-av. weet tee weh-dee bohd ee uhn-rhew leh?)*
*Yes, I went to Spain last month. Have you been anywhere?*

**Person A:** Nac ydw, ddim eto. Ond dw i'n mynd i'r Alban fis nesaf.
*(nak uh-doo, thim et-oh. ond doo een mind eer al-ban veess ness-av)*
*No, not yet. But I'm going to Scotland next month.*

---

**Person A:** Ble est ti ar dy wyliau diwethaf?
*(bleh est tee ar duh wuhl-yah-ee dee-weth-av?)*
*Where did you go on your last holiday?*

**Person B:** Es i i Ffrainc. Roedd e'n hyfryd! Ble est ti?
*(ess ee ee ffrahn-k. roh-eedh een huh-vrid! bleh est tee?)*
*I went to France. It was lovely! Where did you go?*

**Person A:** Es i i Iwerddon. Roedd y tywydd yn wych.
*(ess ee ee ee-wer-thon. roh-eedh uh tuh-with uhn weekh)*
*I went to Ireland. The weather was great.*

---

**Person A:** Sut oedd dy wyliau?
*(seet oh-eedh duh wuhl-yah-ee?)*
*How were your holidays?*

**Person B:** Roedden nhw'n wych! Gwnes i lawer o bethau. Beth wnes di ar dy wyliau?
*(roh-eed-en nhoo-oon weekh! goo-ness ee LLah-oo-er oh beth-ah-ee. beth oo-ness dee ar duh wuhl-yah-ee?)*
*They were great! I did lots of things. What did you do on your holidays?*

**Person A:** Ymlaciais i ar y traeth a darllenais i lyfr.
*(uhm-lak-yah-eess ee ar uh trah-eeth ah dar-LLen-ah-eess ee LLuh-vir)*
*I relaxed on the beach and read a book.*

---

**Person A:** Ble hoffet ti fynd ar wyliau nesaf?
*(bleh hoff-et tee vind ar wuhl-yah-ee ness-av?)*
*Where would you like to go on holiday next?*

**Person B:** Hoffwn i fynd i Japan. Beth amdanat ti?
*(hoff-oon ee vind ee ja-pan. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I'd like to go to Japan. What about you?*

**Person A:** Hoffwn i fynd i Seland Newydd.
*(hoff-oon ee vind ee sel-and neh-with)*
*I'd like to go to New Zealand.*

## At the Airport

**Person A:** Pryd mae dy awyren yn gadael?
*(preed mah-ee duh ah-oo-eer-en uhn gad-ah-el?)*
*When is your plane leaving?*

**Person B:** Mae'n gadael am bump o'r gloch. Beth am dy awyren di?
*(mah-een gad-ah-el am bimp or glokh. beth am duh ah-oo-eer-en dee?)*
*It's leaving at five o'clock. What about your plane?*

**Person A:** Mae fy awyren i am saith.
*(mah-ee vuh ah-oo-eer-en ee am sah-eeth)*
*My plane is at seven.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti wedi pacio popeth?
*(weet tee weh-dee pak-yoh pop-eth?)*
*Have you packed everything?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n meddwl! Wyt ti wedi cofio dy basbort?
*(uh-doo, doo een meth-ool! weet tee weh-dee kov-yoh duh bas-port?)*
*Yes, I think so! Have you remembered your passport?*

**Person A:** Ydw, mae gyda fi!
*(uh-doo, mah-ee guh-dah vee!)*
*Yes, I have it!*

---

**Person A:** Sut oedd yr hediad?
*(seet oh-eedh eer hed-yad?)*
*How was the flight?*

**Person B:** Roedd e'n iawn, ond roedd ychydig o dyrbolyns. Sut oedd dy hediad di?
*(roh-eedh een yown, ond roh-eedh uhkh-uh-dig oh duhr-bol-ins. seet oh-eedh duh hed-yad dee?)*
*It was okay, but there was a bit of turbulence. How was your flight?*

**Person A:** Roedd e'n llyfn iawn.
*(roh-eedh een LLuhv-n yown)*
*It was very smooth.*

## Directions

**Person A:** Ble mae'r orsaf drenau?
*(bleh mah-eer or-sav dren-ah-ee?)*
*Where is the train station?*

**Person B:** Cer yn syth ymlaen, wedyn tro i'r chwith. Mae hi ar y dde.
*(kehr uhn seeth uhm-lah-een, weh-din troh eer khweeth. mah-ee hee ar uh theh)*
*Go straight ahead, then turn left. It's on the right.*

**Person A:** Faint o amser mae'n cymryd i gerdded yno?
*(vah-eent oh am-ser mah-een kuhm-rid ee ger-thed uhn-oh?)*
*How much time does it take to walk there?*

**Person B:** Tua pum munud.
*(tee-ah peem min-id)*
*About five minutes.*

---

**Person A:** Ydw i'n mynd y ffordd gywir i'r ganolfan siopa?
*(uh-doo een mind uh ffordd guh-wir eer gan-ol-van shop-ah?)*
*Am I going the right way to the shopping centre?*

**Person B:** Wyt, cer yn syth ymlaen am ddwy stryd.
*(weet, kehr uhn seeth uhm-lah-een am thoo-ee streed)*
*Yes, go straight ahead for two streets.*

**Person A:** Diolch yn fawr!
*(dee-olkh uhn vah-oor!)*
*Thanks very much!*

---

**Person A:** Dw i ar goll. Allet ti helpu fi?
*(doo ee ar goLl. aLl-et tee hel-pee vee?)*
*I'm lost. Could you help me?*

**Person B:** Wrth gwrs! Ble wyt ti eisiau mynd?
*(oorth goorss! bleh weet tee ay-shah mind?)*
*Of course! Where do you want to go?*

**Person A:** Dw i eisiau mynd i'r llyfrgell.
*(doo ee ay-shah mind eer LLuh-vir-geLl)*
*I want to go to the library.*

**Person B:** Tro yn ôl a cher i'r dde ar y goleuadau traffig.
*(troh uhn ohl ah kher eer theh ar uh gol-ay-ad-ah-ee traff-ig)*
*Turn back and go right at the traffic lights.*

## Cars and Driving

**Person A:** Oes car gyda ti?
*(oh-ess kar guh-dah tee?)*
*Do you have a car?*

**Person B:** Oes, mae car bach gyda fi. Beth amdanat ti?
*(oh-ess, mah-ee kar bakh guh-dah vee. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I have a small car. What about you?*

**Person A:** Nac oes, dw i'n defnyddio beic.
*(nak oh-ess, doo een dev-nuh-thyoh bay-k)*
*No, I use a bike.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n gallu gyrru?
*(weet teen gaLl-ee guhr-ee?)*
*Can you drive?*

**Person B:** Ydw, ces i fy nhrwydded y llynedd. Wyt ti'n gyrru?
*(uh-doo, kess ee vuh nhroo-ee-thed uh LLuhn-eedh. weet teen guhr-ee?)*
*Yes, I got my licence last year. Do you drive?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i wedi bod yn gyrru ers deng mlynedd.
*(uh-doo, doo ee weh-dee bohd uhn guhr-ee erss deng mluhn-eedh)*
*Yes, I've been driving for ten years.*

---

**Person A:** Allet ti fy ngyrru i yno?
*(aLl-et tee vuh nguhr-ee ee uhn-oh?)*
*Could you drive me there?*

**Person B:** Gallwn, dim problem. Pryd wyt ti eisiau mynd?
*(gaLl-oon, dim prob-lem. preed weet tee ay-shah mind?)*
*Yes, no problem. When do you want to go?*

**Person A:** Am ddau o'r gloch, os ydy hynny'n iawn?
*(am thah-ee or glokh, oss uh-dee huhn-een yown?)*
*At two o'clock, if that's okay?*

**Person B:** Ydy, perffaith!
*(uh-dee, per-fah-eeth!)*
*Yes, perfect!*

## Practice Tips
- Practice directions in your local area
- Use real journey times and prices
- Discuss actual trips you've taken
- Practice time expressions (am + time)
