# Hobbies and Interests

## Talking About Hobbies

**Person A:** Beth wyt ti'n hoffi gwneud yn dy amser sbâr?
*(beth weet teen hoff-ee goo-nay-d uhn duh am-ser sbahr?)*
*What do you like to do in your spare time?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n hoffi darllen a mynd am dro. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een hoff-ee dar-LLen ah mind am droh. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I like reading and going for walks. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n hoffi chwarae gemau fideo a gwrando ar gerddoriaeth.
*(doo een hoff-ee khwah-rah-ee gem-ah-ee vid-ee-oh ah gwran-doh ar ger-thor-yah-eeth)*
*I like playing video games and listening to music.*

---

**Person A:** Oes hobïau gyda ti?
*(oh-ess hob-ee-ah-ee guh-dah tee?)*
*Do you have hobbies?*

**Person B:** Oes, dw i'n chwarae gitâr ac yn peintio. Beth am ti?
*(oh-ess, doo een khwah-rah-ee git-ahr ak uhn pay-nt-yoh. beth am tee?)*
*Yes, I play guitar and paint. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n hoffi coginio ac yn dysgu Cymraeg!
*(doo een hoff-ee kog-in-yoh ak uhn duhs-gee kuhm-rah-eeg!)*
*I like cooking and learning Welsh!*

---

**Person A:** Ers pryd wyt ti'n gwneud hynny?
*(erss preed weet teen goo-nay-d huhn-ee?)*
*How long have you been doing that?*

**Person B:** Dw i wedi bod yn chwarae gitâr ers pum mlynedd. Ers pryd wyt ti'n dysgu Cymraeg?
*(doo ee weh-dee bohd uhn khwah-rah-ee git-ahr erss peem mluhn-eedh. erss preed weet teen duhs-gee kuhm-rah-eeg?)*
*I've been playing guitar for five years. How long have you been learning Welsh?*

**Person A:** Dw i wedi bod yn dysgu ers blwyddyn.
*(doo ee weh-dee bohd uhn duhs-gee erss bloo-ith-in)*
*I've been learning for a year.*

## Sports and Exercise

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n chwarae unrhyw chwaraeon?
*(weet teen khwah-rah-ee uhn-rhew khwah-rah-ee-on?)*
*Do you play any sports?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n chwarae rygbi bob wythnos. Wyt ti'n chwarae unrhyw beth?
*(uh-doo, doo een khwah-rah-ee ruhg-bee bob weeth-noss. weet teen khwah-rah-ee uhn-rhew beth?)*
*Yes, I play rugby every week. Do you play anything?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i'n chwarae pêl-droed a dw i'n mynd i'r gampfa.
*(uh-doo, doo een khwah-rah-ee pehl-droy-ed ah doo een mind eer gam-pfah)*
*Yes, I play football and I go to the gym.*

---

**Person A:** Pa dîm wyt ti'n gefnogi?
*(pah deem weet teen gev-nog-ee?)*
*Which team do you support?*

**Person B:** Dw i'n gefnogi [team name]. Wyt ti'n hoffi pêl-droed?
*(doo een gev-nog-ee [team name]. weet teen hoff-ee pehl-droy-ed?)*
*I support [team name]. Do you like football?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i'n gefnogi [team name] hefyd!
*(uh-doo, doo een gev-nog-ee [team name] heh-vid!)*
*Yes, I support [team name] too!*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n cadw'n heini?
*(weet teen kad-oo-oon hay-nee?)*
*Do you keep fit?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n rhedeg bob bore. Beth amdanat ti?
*(uh-doo, doo een rhed-eg bob bor-eh. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I run every morning. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n nofio ddwywaith yr wythnos.
*(doo een nov-yoh thoo-ee-wah-eeth eer weeth-noss)*
*I swim twice a week.*

---

**Person A:** Pryd wnes di ddechrau chwarae [sport]?
*(preed oo-ness dee thekh-rah-ee khwah-rah-ee [sport]?)*
*When did you start playing [sport]?*

**Person B:** Dechreuais i pan oeddwn i'n blentyn. Dw i'n ei garu e!
*(dekh-ray-ah-eess ee pan oh-eed-oon een plen-tin. doo een ay gar-ee eh!)*
*I started when I was a child. I love it!*

**Person A:** Mae hynny'n wych!
*(mah-ee huhn-een weekh!)*
*That's great!*

## Arts and Culture

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n hoffi cerddoriaeth?
*(weet teen hoff-ee ker-thor-yah-eeth?)*
*Do you like music?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n caru cerddoriaeth! Pa fath o gerddoriaeth wyt ti'n hoffi?
*(uh-doo, doo een kar-ee ker-thor-yah-eeth! pah vahth oh ger-thor-yah-eeth weet teen hoff-ee?)*
*Yes, I love music! What kind of music do you like?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n hoffi roc a phop. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een hoff-ee rok ah pop. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I like rock and pop. What about you?*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti wedi bod i gyngerdd yn ddiweddar?
*(weet tee weh-dee bohd ee guhn-gertth uhn thee-wed-ar?)*
*Have you been to a concert recently?*

**Person B:** Ydw, es i weld [band name] fis diwethaf. Oedd e'n anhygoel! Wyt ti wedi bod i gyngerdd?
*(uh-doo, ess ee weld [band name] veess dee-weth-av. oh-eedh een an-huh-goy-el! weet tee weh-dee bohd ee guhn-gertth?)*
*Yes, I went to see [band name] last month. It was incredible! Have you been to a concert?*

**Person A:** Nac ydw, ddim ers blwyddyn.
*(nak uh-doo, thim erss bloo-ith-in)*
*No, not for a year.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n hoffi mynd i'r sinema?
*(weet teen hoff-ee mind eer sin-em-ah?)*
*Do you like going to the cinema?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n mynd yn aml. Pa fath o ffilmiau wyt ti'n hoffi?
*(uh-doo, doo een mind uhn am-l. pah vahth oh ffil-mee-ah-ee weet teen hoff-ee?)*
*Yes, I go often. What kind of films do you like?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n hoffi ffilmiau arswyd ac antur. Beth amdanat ti?
*(doo een hoff-ee ffil-mee-ah-ee ars-wid ak an-teer. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*I like horror and adventure films. What about you?*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n darllen llawer?
*(weet teen dar-LLen LLah-oo-er?)*
*Do you read a lot?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n darllen bob nos cyn mynd i gysgu. Beth wyt ti'n darllen ar hyn o bryd?
*(uh-doo, doo een dar-LLen bob noss keen mind ee guhs-gee. beth weet teen dar-LLen ar heen oh breed?)*
*Yes, I read every night before going to sleep. What are you reading at the moment?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n darllen nofel ddetectif.
*(doo een dar-LLen nov-el theh-tek-tiv)*
*I'm reading a detective novel.*

## Creative Hobbies

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n greadigol?
*(weet teen grey-ad-ig-ol?)*
*Are you creative?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n hoffi tynnu lluniau. Wyt ti'n gwneud unrhyw beth creadigol?
*(uh-doo, doo een hoff-ee tuhn-ee LLin-yah-ee. weet teen goo-nay-d uhn-rhew beth grey-ad-ig-ol?)*
*Yes, I like drawing. Do you do anything creative?*

**Person A:** Ydw, dw i'n sgrifennu straeon byrion.
*(uh-doo, doo een sgriv-en-ee strah-ee-on buhr-yon)*
*Yes, I write short stories.*

---

**Person A:** Ers pryd wyt ti'n peintio?
*(erss preed weet teen pay-nt-yoh?)*
*How long have you been painting?*

**Person B:** Dw i wedi bod yn peintio ers deng mlynedd. Dw i'n ei garu e!
*(doo ee weh-dee bohd uhn pay-nt-yoh erss deng mluhn-eedh. doo een ay gar-ee eh!)*
*I've been painting for ten years. I love it!*

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n gwerthu dy waith?
*(weet teen gwer-thee duh wah-eeth?)*
*Do you sell your work?*

**Person B:** Ydw, weithiau. Mae'n hwyl!
*(uh-doo, way-thyah-ee. mah-een hoo-il!)*
*Yes, sometimes. It's fun!*

## Technology and Gaming

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n chwarae gemau fideo?
*(weet teen khwah-rah-ee gem-ah-ee vid-ee-oh?)*
*Do you play video games?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n eu caru nhw! Pa gemau wyt ti'n hoffi?
*(uh-doo, doo een ay kar-ee nhoo! pah gem-ah-ee weet teen hoff-ee?)*
*Yes, I love them! What games do you like?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n hoffi gemau antur a phosau.
*(doo een hoff-ee gem-ah-ee an-teer ah poss-ah-ee)*
*I like adventure games and puzzles.*

---

**Person A:** Pa gonsol sydd gyda ti?
*(pah gon-sol seeth guh-dah tee?)*
*What console do you have?*

**Person B:** Mae PlayStation gyda fi. Beth am ti?
*(mah-ee play-stay-shun guh-dah vee. beth am tee?)*
*I have PlayStation. What about you?*

**Person A:** Mae Xbox gyda fi.
*(mah-ee eks-boks guh-dah vee)*
*I have Xbox.*

---

**Person A:** Wyt ti'n dilyn technoleg newydd?
*(weet teen dil-in tek-nol-eg neh-with?)*
*Do you follow new technology?*

**Person B:** Ydw, dw i'n caru dyfeisiau newydd. Beth amdanat ti?
*(uh-doo, doo een kar-ee duh-vay-shah-ee neh-with. beth am-dan-at tee?)*
*Yes, I love new devices. What about you?*

**Person A:** Dw i'n ddiddordeb mewn technoleg hefyd!
*(doo een thee-thor-deb mew-in tek-nol-eg heh-vid!)*
*I'm interested in technology too!*

## Practice Tips
- Share your real hobbies and passions
- Ask follow-up questions to keep conversation flowing
- Practice describing how long you've done activities
- Use "yn hoffi," "yn caru," "yn mwynhau" to express preferences
