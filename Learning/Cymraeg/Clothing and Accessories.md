# Clothing and Accessories (Dillad ac Ategolion)

> [!info] Gender Matters
> Welsh nouns have gender (masculine/feminine), which affects mutations. Gender is shown as (m) or (f).

## Basic Clothing

| Welsh | Phonetic | English | Gender |
|-------|----------|---------|--------|
| dillad | DIH-lad | clothes | (plural) |
| crys | KRISS | shirt | (m) |
| trowsus | TROW-siss | trousers | (m) |
| sgert | SGERT | skirt | (f) |
| ffrog | FROG | dress | (f) |
| siwmper | SHOOM-per | jumper / sweater | (f) |
| côt | KOHT | coat | (f) |
| siaced | SHAH-ked | jacket | (f) |
| tei | TAY | tie | (m) |
| gwregys | GWREG-iss | belt | (m) |

### Examples

```
Dw i'n gwisgo crys
DOO een GWISS-goh KRISS
I am wearing a shirt

Mae hi'n prynu ffrog newydd
MIGH heen PRUH-nee FROG NEH-with
She is buying a new dress

Dych chi'n hoffi'r gôt?
DEEKH kheen HOF-feer GOHT?
Do you like the coat?
```

## Useful Phrases

|-------|----------|---------|
| Beth wyt ti'n gwisgo? | BEHTH weet teen GWISS-goh? | What are you wearing? |
| Dw i'n gwisgo... | DOO een GWISS-goh... | I am wearing... |
| Pa faint yw hwn? | PAH VIGHNT you HOON? | What size is this? (m) |
| Pa faint yw hon? | PAH VIGHNT you HON? | What size is this? (f) |

## Getting Dressed

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| gwisgo | GWISS-goh | to wear / to dress / to put on |
| tynnu | TUH-nee | to take off / to remove |
| newid | NEH-wid | to change |

### Examples

```
Dw i'n gwisgo fy nghot
DOO een GWISS-goh vuh NKHOT
I am putting on my coat

Tynnwch eich esgidiau!
TUH-nookh EYKH es-GID-yee!
Take off your shoes!

Mae'n rhaid i fi newid
MINE HRYDE ee vee NEH-wid
I need to change
```
