## 🔗 Quick Reference

### Common Phrases
- Bore da - Good morning (BOH-reh DAH)
- Diolch - Thank you (DEE-olkh)
- Croeso - You're welcome (KROY-so)
- Sut dych chi? - How are you? (SEET deekh KHEE)
- Dw i'n dysgu Cymraeg - I'm learning Welsh (DOO een DUH-sgee cum-RIGH-g)

### Essential Patterns
- Dw i'n + verb-noun = I am [doing]
- Mae e/hi'n + verb-noun = He/She is [doing]
- Dyn ni'n + verb-noun = We are [doing]

### Days of the Week
- Llun (Monday), Mawrth (Tuesday), Mercher (Wednesday), Iau (Thursday), Gwener (Friday), Sadwrn (Saturday), Sul (Sunday)

### Numbers 1-10
- un, dau/dwy, tri/tair, pedwar/pedair, pump, chwech, saith, wyth, naw, deg
