# The Verb "Bod" (To Be)

> [!info] Most Important Verb
> "Bod" (to be) is the most important verb in Welsh. You'll use it constantly, and it's irregular - so it needs special attention!

## Present Tense - Positive

### Full Forms

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| (Rydw) i / Dw i | (RUH-doo) EE / DOO EE | I am |
| (Rwyt) ti / Wyt ti | (ROO-eet) TEE / OO-eet TEE | You are (informal) |
| Mae e | MIGH EH | He is |
| Mae hi | MIGH HEE | She is |
| (Rydyn) ni / Dyn ni | (RUH-din) NEE / DIN NEE | We are |
| (Rydych) chi / Dych chi | (RUH-deekh) KHEE / DEEKH KHEE | You are (formal/plural) |
| Maen nhw | MINE NHOO | They are |

### Usage Notes

> [!tip] Short Forms
> In spoken Welsh, the short forms (Dw i, Wyt ti, Dyn ni, Dych chi) are much more common than the long forms in brackets.

### Examples

```
Dw i'n dysgu Cymraeg
DOO een DUH-sgee cum-RIGH-g
I am learning Welsh

Wyt ti'n mynd?
OO-eet teen MIND?
Are you going?

Mae hi'n dda
MIGH heen THAH
She is good

Dyn ni'n hapus
DIN neen HA-piss
We are happy

Dych chi'n barod?
DEEKH kheen BAH-rod?
Are you ready?

Maen nhw'n dod
MINE nhoon DOD
They are coming
```

## Present Tense - Negative

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Dw i ddim | DOO ee THEEM | I am not |
| Dwyt ti ddim | DOO-eet tee THEEM | You are not (informal) |
| Dyw e ddim / Dydy e ddim | DEW eh THEEM / DUH-dee eh THEEM | He is not |
| Dyw hi ddim / Dydy hi ddim | DEW hee THEEM / DUH-dee hee THEEM | She is not |
| Dyn ni ddim | DIN nee THEEM | We are not |
| Dych chi ddim | DEEKH khee THEEM | You are not (formal/plural) |
| Dyn nhw ddim / Dydyn nhw ddim | DIN nhoo THEEM / DUH-din nhoo THEEM | They are not |

### Examples

```
Dw i ddim yn mynd
DOO ee THEEM un MIND
I am not going

Dyw hi ddim yn dda
DEW hee THEEM un THAH
She is not good

Dyn ni ddim yn barod
DIN nee THEEM un BAH-rod
We are not ready
```

## Question Forms

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Ydw i...? | UH-doo EE...? | Am I...? |
| Wyt ti...? | OO-eet TEE...? | Are you...? (informal) |
| Ydy e...? | UH-dee EH...? | Is he...? |
| Ydy hi...? | UH-dee HEE...? | Is she...? |
| Ydyn ni...? | UH-din NEE...? | Are we...? |
| Ydych chi...? | UH-deekh KHEE...? | Are you...? (formal/plural) |
| Ydyn nhw...? | UH-din NHOO...? | Are they...? |

### Answers to Questions

**Positive:**
- Ydw (UH-doo) - Yes (I am)
- Wyt (OO-eet) - Yes (you are)
- Ydy (UH-dee) - Yes (he/she is)
- Ydyn (UH-din) - Yes (we/they are)
- Ydych (UH-deekh) - Yes (you are - formal)

**Negative:**
- Nac ydw (NAK UH-doo) - No (I'm not)
- Nac wyt (NAK OO-eet) - No (you're not)
- Nac ydy (NAK UH-dee) - No (he/she isn't)
- Nac ydyn (NAK UH-din) - No (we/they aren't)
- Nac ydych (NAK UH-deekh) - No (you're not - formal)

### Examples

```
Wyt ti'n dysgu Cymraeg?
OO-eet teen DUH-sgee cum-RIGH-g?
Are you learning Welsh?

Ydw! / Nac ydw
UH-doo! / NAK UH-doo
Yes! / No

Ydy hi'n dod?
UH-dee heen DOD?
Is she coming?

Ydy / Nac ydy
UH-dee / NAK UH-dee
Yes / No
```

## Using "yn" with Bod

> [!important] The Pattern: Subject + Bod + yn + Verb/Adjective
> - **'yn' + verb-noun** = continuous action (I am doing)
> - **'yn' + adjective** = state of being (I am happy)

### With Verbs (Continuous Tense)

```
Dw i'n mynd = I am going
Dw i'n dod = I am coming
Dw i'n dysgu = I am learning
Mae hi'n siarad = She is speaking
Maen nhw'n bwyta = They are eating
```

### With Adjectives

```
Dw i'n hapus = I am happy
Mae e'n dda = He is good
Maen nhw'n brysur = They are busy
```

## Past Tense (Simple)

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Roeddwn i / O'n i | ROY-thoon EE / OHN EE | I was |
| Roeddet ti / O't ti | ROY-thet TEE / OHT TEE | You were (informal) |
| Roedd e/hi | ROY-th eh/hee | He/She was |
| Roedden ni / O'n ni | ROY-then NEE / OHN NEE | We were |
| Roeddech chi / O'ch chi | ROY-thekh KHEE / OHKH KHEE | You were (formal) |
| Roedden nhw / O'n nhw | ROY-then NHOO / OHN NHOO | They were |

### Examples

```
Roeddwn i'n dysgu
ROY-thoon een DUH-sgee
I was learning

O'n i'n mynd
OHN een MIND
I was going
```

## Future Tense (Simple)

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Bydda i | BUH-thah EE | I will be |
| Byddi di | BUH-thee DEE | You will be (informal) |
| Bydd e/hi | BEETH eh/hee | He/She will be |
| Byddwn ni | BUH-thoon NEE | We will be |
| Byddwch chi | BUH-thookh KHEE | You will be (formal) |
| Byddan nhw | BUH-than NHOO | They will be |

### Examples

```
Bydda i'n mynd yfory
BUH-thah een MIND uh-VOR-ee
I will be going tomorrow

Bydd hi'n dda
BEETH heen THAH
She will be good
```
