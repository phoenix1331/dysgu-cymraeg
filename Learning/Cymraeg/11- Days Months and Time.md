# Days, Months, and Time (Dyddiau, Misoedd, ac Amser)

> [!info] Using Time Expressions
> Days and months in Welsh are used with specific prepositions. Pay attention to the patterns!

## Days of the Week (Dyddiau'r Wythnos)

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| dydd Llun | DEETH HLEEN | Monday |
| dydd Mawrth | DEETH MOW-rth | Tuesday |
| dydd Mercher | DEETH MER-kher | Wednesday |
| dydd Iau | DEETH YEE-eye | Thursday |
| dydd Gwener | DEETH GWEN-er | Friday |
| dydd Sadwrn | DEETH SAD-oorn | Saturday |
| dydd Sul | DEETH SEEL | Sunday |

### Informal/Short Forms

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Llun | HLEEN | Monday |
| Mawrth | MOW-rth | Tuesday |
| Mercher | MER-kher | Wednesday |
| Iau | YEE-eye | Thursday |
| Gwener | GWEN-er | Friday |
| Sadwrn | SAD-oorn | Saturday |
| Sul | SEEL | Sunday |

### Usage Notes

> [!tip] "Dydd" means "day"
> Use "dydd" + day name for formal situations. Drop "dydd" in casual speech.

### Examples

```
Dw i'n mynd ar ddydd Llun
DOO een MIND ar THEETH HLEEN
I'm going on Monday

Wyt ti'n gweithio dydd Sadwrn?
OO-eet teen GWEYE-thee-oh DEETH SAD-oorn?
Are you working on Saturday?

Mae'r cyfarfod ddydd Mercher
MINE uh kuh-VAR-vod THEETH MER-kher
The meeting is on Wednesday
```

## Months (Misoedd)

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Ionawr | YON-owr | January |
| Chwefror | KHWEV-ror | February |
| Mawrth | MOW-rth | March |
| Ebrill | EB-rih-ll | April |
| Mai | MIGH | May |
| Mehefin | meh-HEV-in | June |
| Gorffennaf | gor-FEN-av | July |
| Awst | OWST | August |
| Medi | MED-ee | September |
| Hydref | HUH-drev | October |
| Tachwedd | TAKH-weth | November |
| Rhagfyr | HRAG-veer | December |

### Using Months

```
ym mis Ionawr
um MEESS YON-owr
in January

mis Mehefin
meess meh-HEV-in
(the month of) June

yn ystod mis Awst
un UH-stod meess OWST
during August
```

### Examples

```
Dw i'n mynd ar fy ngwyliau ym mis Awst
DOO een MIND ar vuh NG-will-yee um meess OWST
I'm going on my holidays in August

Mae fy mhen-blwydd ym mis Mai
MIGH vuh MEN-blooith um meess MIGH
My birthday is in May

Mae hi'n oer ym mis Ionawr
MIGH heen OY-r um meess YON-owr
It's cold in January
```

## Time Expressions

### General Time Words

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| amser | AM-ser | time |
| heddiw | HEH-thee-oo | today |
| ddoe | THOY | yesterday |
| yfory | UH-vor-ee | tomorrow |
| echdoe | EKH-doy | the day before yesterday |
| trennydd | TREN-eeth | the day after tomorrow |
| nawr | NOW-r | now |
| rŵan | ROO-an | now (North Wales) |

### Parts of the Day

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| bore | BOH-reh | morning |
| prynhawn | PRIN-hown | afternoon |
| nos / noswaith | NOHS / NOS-wighth | night / evening |
| canol dydd | KAN-ol DEETH | midday / noon |
| canol nos | KAN-ol NOHS | midnight |

### Time Phrases

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| bore 'ma | BOH-reh MAH | this morning |
| prynhawn 'ma | PRIN-hown MAH | this afternoon |
| heno | HEN-oh | tonight |
| neithiwr | NAYTH-eer | last night |
| bore ddoe | BOH-reh THOY | yesterday morning |
| bore yfory | BOH-reh UH-vor-ee | tomorrow morning |

### Examples

```
Dw i'n mynd heddiw
DOO een MIND HEH-thee-oo
I'm going today

Beth wnest ti ddoe?
BEHTH oo-NEST tee THOY?
What did you do yesterday?

Wela i di yfory
WEL-ah ee dee UH-vor-ee
I'll see you tomorrow

Dw i'n brysur bore 'ma
DOO een BRUH-sir BOH-reh MAH
I'm busy this morning

Beth wyt ti'n wneud heno?
BEHTH weet teen OO-neyd HEN-oh?
What are you doing tonight?
```

## Telling Time (Dweud yr Amser)

### O'clock

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| un o'r gloch | EEN or GLOKH | one o'clock |
| dau o'r gloch | DIGH or GLOKH | two o'clock |
| tri o'r gloch | TREE or GLOKH | three o'clock |
| pedwar o'r gloch | PED-war or GLOKH | four o'clock |
| pump o'r gloch | PIMP or GLOKH | five o'clock |
| chwech o'r gloch | KHWEKH or GLOKH | six o'clock |
| saith o'r gloch | SIGHTH or GLOKH | seven o'clock |
| wyth o'r gloch | OO-eeth or GLOKH | eight o'clock |
| naw o'r gloch | NOW or GLOKH | nine o'clock |
| deg o'r gloch | DEG or GLOKH | ten o'clock |
| un ar ddeg o'r gloch | EEN ar THEG or GLOKH | eleven o'clock |
| deuddeg o'r gloch | DAY-theg or GLOKH | twelve o'clock |

### Half Past

```
hanner awr wedi pump
HAN-er OW-r WED-ee PIMP
half past five

hanner awr wedi tri
HAN-er OW-r WED-ee TREE
half past three
```

### Quarter Past/To

```
chwarter wedi dau
KHWAR-ter WED-ee DIGH
quarter past two

chwarter i dri
KHWAR-ter ee TREE
quarter to three
```

### Minutes Past/To

```
pum munud wedi wyth
PIM MIN-id WED-ee OO-eeth
five minutes past eight

deg munud i naw
DEG MIN-id ee NOW
ten minutes to nine

ugain munud wedi pedwar
EEG-ine MIN-id WED-ee PED-war
twenty minutes past four
```

### Asking the Time

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Faint o'r gloch yw hi? | VIGHNT or GLOKH you HEE? | What time is it? |
| Am faint o'r gloch? | AM VIGHNT or GLOKH? | At what time? |
| Mae hi'n... | MIGH heen... | It is... |

### Examples

```
Faint o'r gloch yw hi?
VIGHNT or GLOKH you HEE?
What time is it?

Mae hi'n dri o'r gloch
MIGH heen TREE or GLOKH
It's three o'clock

Dw i'n gadael am saith o'r gloch
DOO een GAD-ile am SIGHTH or GLOKH
I'm leaving at seven o'clock
```
