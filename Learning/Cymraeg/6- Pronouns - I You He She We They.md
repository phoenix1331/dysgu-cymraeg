# Pronouns (Rhagenwau)

> [!info] Essential Building Blocks
> Pronouns are fundamental to Welsh sentences. You'll use them constantly with the verb "bod" (to be) and other verbs.

## Subject Pronouns

| Welsh | Phonetic | English | Notes |
|-------|----------|---------|-------|
| i / fi | EE / VEE | I / me | "i" before verbs, "fi" for emphasis |
| ti | TEE | you (informal) | Used with friends, family, children |
| e / fe / o | EH / VEH / OH | he / him | "e/fe" South Wales, "o" North Wales |
| hi | HEE | she / her | |
| ni | NEE | we / us | |
| chi | KHEE | you (formal/plural) | Formal or multiple people |
| nhw | NHOO | they / them | |

## Using Pronouns with "Bod" (To Be)

### I Am - Dw i / Rydw i

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Dw i | DOO EE | I am |
| Dw i'n mynd | DOO een MIND | I am going |
| Dw i'n dysgu | DOO een DUH-sgee | I am learning |
| Dw i'n hapus | DOO een HA-piss | I am happy |
| Dw i'n byw yma | DOO een BEEW UH-mah | I live here (I am living here) |

**Negative:**
```
Dw i ddim
DOO ee THEEM
I am not

Dw i ddim yn mynd
DOO ee THEEM un MIND
I am not going
```

**Question:**
```
Ydw i...?
UH-doo EE...?
Am I...?

Ydw i'n hwyr?
UH-doo een HOOR?
Am I late?
```

### You Are (Informal) - Wyt ti / Rwyt ti

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Wyt ti | OO-eet TEE | You are |
| Wyt ti'n mynd? | OO-eet teen MIND? | Are you going? |
| Wyt ti'n iawn? | OO-eet teen YOWN? | Are you OK? |
| Wyt ti'n dysgu Cymraeg? | OO-eet teen DUH-sgee cum-RIGH-g? | Are you learning Welsh? |

**Negative:**
```
Dwyt ti ddim
DOO-eet tee THEEM
You are not

Dwyt ti ddim yn deall
DOO-eet tee THEEM un DEH-ah-ll
You don't understand
```

**Statement Form:**
```
Rwyt ti'n dda
ROO-eet teen THAH
You are good
```

### He Is - Mae e / Mae fe / Mae o

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Mae e | MIGH EH | He is (most common) |
| Mae fe | MIGH VEH | He is (South Wales) |
| Mae o | MIGH OH | He is (North Wales) |
| Mae e'n mynd | MIGH en MIND | He is going |
| Mae e'n gweithio | MIGH en GWEYE-thee-oh | He is working |
| Mae e'n dda | MIGH en THAH | He is good |

**Negative:**
```
Dyw e ddim / Dydy e ddim
DEW eh THEEM / DUH-dee eh THEEM
He is not

Dyw e ddim yn dod
DEW eh THEEM un DOD
He is not coming
```

**Question:**
```
Ydy e...?
UH-dee EH...?
Is he...?

Ydy e'n barod?
UH-dee en BAH-rod?
Is he ready?
```

### You Are (Formal/Plural) - Dych chi / Rydych chi

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Dych chi | DEEKH KHEE | You are (formal/plural) |
| Dych chi'n mynd? | DEEKH kheen MIND? | Are you going? |
| Dych chi'n iawn? | DEEKH kheen YOWN? | Are you OK? (polite) |
| Dych chi'n siarad Cymraeg? | DEEKH kheen SHAH-rad cum-RIGH-g? | Do you speak Welsh? |

**Negative:**
```
Dych chi ddim
DEEKH khee THEEM
You are not

Dych chi ddim yn deall
DEEKH khee THEEM un DEH-ah-ll
You don't understand
```

**Statement Form:**
```
Rydych chi'n garedig
RUH-deekh kheen gar-EYE-dig
You are kind
```

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Maen nhw | MINE NHOO | They are |

| Maen nhw'n chwarae | MINE nhoon KHWAH-rye | They are playing |
| Maen nhw'n hapus | MINE nhoon HA-piss | They are happy |
| Maen nhw'n byw yma | MINE nhoon BEEW UH-mah | They live here |

**Negative:**
```
Dyn nhw ddim / Dydyn nhw ddim
DIN nhoo THEEM / DUH-din nhoo THEEM
They are not

Dyn nhw ddim yn dod
DIN nhoo THEEM un DOD

```

**Question:**
```
Ydyn nhw...?
UH-din NHOO...?
Are they...?

Ydyn nhw'n barod?
UH-din nhoon BAH-rod?
```

## Emphasizing Pronouns

Use the emphatic forms when stressing WHO is doing something:

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Fi sy'n mynd | VEE seen MIND | I am going (emphasis: ME) |
| Ti sy'n iawn | TEE seen YOWN | You are right (YOU, not them) |
| Fe/Fo sy'n gweithio | VEH/VOH seen GWEYE-thee-oh | HE is working |
| Hi sy'n siarad | HEE seen SHAH-rad | SHE is speaking |
| Ni sy'n dysgu | NEE seen DUH-sgee | WE are learning |
| Chi sy'n ennill | KHEE seen EN-ih-ll | YOU are winning |
| Nhw sy'n chwarae | NHOO seen KHWAH-rye | THEY are playing |

## Conversation Examples

### Meeting Someone

```
Person A: Shwmae! Sut wyt ti?
SHOO-my! SEET weet TEE?
Hello! How are you?

Person B: Dw i'n dda, diolch. A ti?
DOO een THAH, DEE-olkh. Ah TEE?
I'm good, thanks. And you?

Person A: Dw i'n iawn. Beth wyt ti'n wneud heddiw?
DOO een YOWN. BEHTH weet teen OO-neyd HEH-thee-oo?
I'm fine. What are you doing today?

Person B: Dw i'n mynd i'r dre. Wyt ti'n dod?
DOO een MIND eer DREH. OO-eet teen DOD?
I'm going to town. Are you coming?
```

### Talking About Others

```
Mae hi'n dysgu Cymraeg
MIGH heen DUH-sgee cum-RIGH-g
She is learning Welsh

Mae e'n gweithio yn Llundain
MIGH en GWEYE-thee-oh un HLIN-dine
He works in London

Maen nhw'n byw yng Nghymru
MINE nhoon BEEW ung NKHUM-ree
They live in Wales

Dyn ni'n mynd i'r sinema heno
DIN neen MIND eer sin-EM-ah HEN-oh
We're going to the cinema tonight
```

### Questions and Answers

```
Q: Ydych chi'n siarad Cymraeg?
DEEKH kheen SHAH-rad cum-RIGH-g?
Do you speak Welsh?

A: Ydw, dw i'n dysgu
UH-doo, DOO een DUH-sgee
Yes, I'm learning

Q: Ydy hi'n dod?
UH-dee heen DOD?
Is she coming?

A: Nac ydy, dyw hi ddim yn dod
NAK UH-dee, DEW hee THEEM un DOD
No, she's not coming

Q: Ydyn nhw'n hapus?
UH-din nhoon HA-piss?
Are they happy?

A: Ydyn, maen nhw'n hapus iawn
UH-din, MINE nhoon HA-piss YOWN
Yes, they're very happy
```
