# Sentence Structure and Word Order

> [!info] Different from English!
> Welsh word order is different from English. The verb usually comes FIRST, then the subject, then the object. Once you understand the patterns, it becomes natural.

## Basic Word Order: VSO

Welsh follows **VSO** order:
- **V**erb
- **S**ubject
- **O**bject

English follows **SVO** order:
- **S**ubject
- **V**erb
- **O**bject

### Example Comparison

| Welsh (VSO) | English (SVO) |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Mae** hi'n **darllen** **llyfr** | She **is reading** **a book** |
| Verb - Subject - Object | Subject - Verb - Object |

## Present Continuous: Dw i'n + Verb-Noun

The most common pattern you'll use:

**Pattern: Subject + "yn" + Verb-noun**

| Welsh | Literal | English |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Dw i'**n** mynd | I am in going | I am going |
| Mae hi'**n** bwyta | She is in eating | She is eating |
| Dyn ni'**n** dysgu | We are in learning | We are learning |

> [!tip] "yn" becomes 'n
> After a vowel, "yn" shortens to 'n: "Dw i'n" not "Dw i yn"

### Examples

```
Dw i'n siarad Cymraeg
DOO een SHAH-rad cum-RIGH-g
I am speaking Welsh

Mae e'n gweithio yn y swyddfa
MIGH en GWEYE-thee-oh un uh SWUH-thvah
He is working in the office

Maen nhw'n chwarae pêl-droed
MINE nhoon KHWAH-rye PEHL-droyd
They are playing football
```

## Simple Statements with "Mae"

For "there is/are" or stating facts:

**Pattern: Mae + Subject + yn + Adjective/Location**

```
Mae'r gath yn ddu
MINE uh GATH un THEE
The cat is black
(Literally: Is the cat in black)

Mae'r tŷ yn fawr
MINE uh TEE un VOW-r
The house is big

Mae Cymru yn brydferth
MIGH CUM-ree un BRUD-verth
Wales is beautiful
```

## Question Formation

### With "Bod" (to be)

Questions put the question form of "bod" first:

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| Mae hi'n dod | **Ydy** hi'n dod? |
| She is coming | Is she coming? |
| Dw i'n hwyr | **Ydw** i'n hwyr? |
| I am late | Am I late? |

### With Question Words

Question words come FIRST:

| Welsh | Phonetic | English | Word Order |
|-------|----------|---------|------------|
| Beth wyt ti'n wneud? | BEHTH weet teen OO-neyd? | What are you doing? | What - are - you - doing? |
| Ble mae'r tŷ? | BLEH MINE uh TEE? | Where is the house? | Where - is - the house? |
| Pwy sy'n dod? | PWEE seen DOD? | Who is coming? | Who - is - coming? |
| Pryd wyt ti'n mynd? | PRID weet teen MIND? | When are you going? | When - are - you - going? |
| Pam mae e'n hwyr? | PAM MIGH en HOOR? | Why is he late? | Why - is - he - late? |
| Sut mae? | SEET MIGH? | How are you? | How - is? |

### Common Question Words

| Welsh | Phonetic | English |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Beth? | BEHTH? | What? |
| Pwy? | PWEE? | Who? |
| Ble? | BLEH? | Where? |
| Pryd? | PRID? | When? |
| Pam? | PAM? | Why? |
| Sut? | SEET? | How? |
| Faint? | VIGHNT? | How much/many? |
| Pa? | PAH? | Which? |

## Negation

Add "ddim" after the subject:

**Pattern: Subject + ddim + yn + Verb/Adjective**

```
Dw i ddim yn mynd
DOO ee THEEM un MIND
I am not going

Dyw hi ddim yn hapus
DEW hee THEEM un HA-piss
She is not happy

Dyn ni ddim yn deall
DIN nee THEEM un DEH-ah-ll
We don't understand
```

## Adjectives Come After Nouns

Unlike English, adjectives follow the noun:

| Welsh (Noun-Adj) | English (Adj-Noun) |
|------------------|-------------------|
| car **coch** | **red** car |
| tŷ **mawr** | **big** house |
| cath **ddu** | **black** cat |
| llyfr **da** | **good** book |

### Examples

```
Mae gen i gar coch
MIGH gen ee GAR KOKH
I have a red car
(Literally: Is with me car red)

Mae hi'n gwisgo ffrog las
MIGH heen GWISS-goh FROG LAHS
She is wearing a blue dress
```

## The Verb "Bod" in Different Positions

### As Main Verb

```
Dw i'n athro
DOO een ATH-roh
I am a teacher

Mae hi'n hapus
MIGH heen HA-piss
She is happy
```

### "There is/are" Construction

```
Mae car yn y garej
MIGH KAR un uh GAR-ej
There is a car in the garage

Oes ci gyda ti?
OYSS KEE GUH-dah tee?
Do you have a dog? (Is dog with you?)
```

### Emphasizing with "sy'n"

To emphasize WHO is doing something, use "sy'n":

```
Fi sy'n mynd
VEE seen MIND
I am going (emphasis: ME, not them)

Hi sy'n siarad
HEE seen SHAH-rad
SHE is speaking (not someone else)

Y gath sy'n ddu
uh GATH seen THEE
It's the cat that is black (not the dog)
```

## Prepositions and Objects

Prepositions come before their objects (like English):

```
yn y tŷ
un uh TEE
in the house

ar y bwrdd
ar uh BOORTH
on the table

i'r ysgol
eer UH-sgol
to the school

gyda fy ffrind
GUH-dah vuh FRIND
with my friend
```

## Common Sentence Patterns

### Pattern 1: I am [doing something]

**Dw i'n + verb-noun**

```
Dw i'n mynd i'r dre
DOO een MIND eer DREH
I am going to town

Dw i'n bwyta cinio
DOO een BOO-ee-tah KIN-ee-oh
I am eating lunch
```

### Pattern 2: I am [adjective]

**Dw i'n + adjective**

```
Dw i'n hapus
DOO een HA-piss
I am happy

Dw i'n brysur
DOO een BRUH-sir
I am busy
```

### Pattern 3: I have [something]

**Mae + preposition "gen/gyda" + pronoun + noun**

```
Mae gen i gar
MIGH gen ee GAR
I have a car
(Literally: Is with me car)

Oes plant gyda chi?
OYSS PLANT GUH-dah khee?
Do you have children?
(Literally: Are children with you?)
```

### Pattern 4: I like [something]

**Dw i'n + hoffi + noun**

```
Dw i'n hoffi coffi
DOO een HOF-fee KOH-fee
I like coffee

Dw i'n caru Cymru
DOO een KAH-ree CUM-ree
I love Wales
```

### Pattern 5: I want [to do something]

**Dw i eisiau/moyn + verb-noun**

```
Dw i eisiau mynd
DOO ee EYESS-yee MIND
I want to go

Dw i moyn bwyta
DOO ee MOYN BOO-ee-tah
I want to eat (South Wales)
```

## Compound Sentences

### Using "a" (and)

```
Dw i'n hoffi te a choffi
DOO een HOF-fee TEH ah KHOF-fee
I like tea and coffee

Mae e'n dysgu Cymraeg a Ffrangeg
MIGH en DUH-sgee cum-RIGH-g ah FRANG-eg
He is learning Welsh and French
```

### Using "ond" (but)

```
Dw i'n hoffi te ond dw i ddim yn hoffi coffi
DOO een HOF-fee TEH ond DOO ee THEEM un HOF-fee KOH-fee
I like tea but I don't like coffee
```

### Using "achos" (because)

```
Dw i'n hapus achos mae'n braf
DOO een HA-piss AKH-oss MINE BRAHV
I am happy because it's nice (weather)
```

## Time Expressions in Sentences

Time expressions usually go at the end:

```
Dw i'n mynd i'r gwaith yfory
DOO een MIND eer GWIGHTH uh-VOR-ee
I'm going to work tomorrow

Mae hi'n dod heno
MIGH heen DOD HEN-oh
She's coming tonight

Dyn ni'n bwyta nawr
DIN neen BOO-ee-tah NOW-r
We're eating now
```

## Building Complex Sentences

### Combining Ideas

```
Dw i'n mynd i'r ysgol bob dydd achos dw i'n dysgu Cymraeg
DOO een MIND eer UH-sgol bob DEETH AKH-oss DOO een DUH-sgee cum-RIGH-g
I go to school every day because I'm learning Welsh

Mae hi'n hoffi coffi ond mae e'n hoffi te
MIGH heen HOF-fee KOH-fee ond MIGH en HOF-fee TEH
She likes coffee but he likes tea
```

## Common Errors to Avoid

### Don't Forget "yn"

❌ Dw i mynd (missing yn)
✅ Dw i'n mynd

### Don't Put Adjective Before Noun

❌ coch car
✅ car coch

### Don't Forget Mutations with Possessives

❌ fy car (should mutate)
✅ fy nghar

### Remember VSO Order in Formal Sentences

❌ Y gath mae yn ddu (wrong order)
✅ Mae'r gath yn ddu (correct order)
