## 💡 Key Concepts at a Glance

|Concept        |One-liner                                          |
|---------------|---------------------------------------------------|
|**Table**      |A structured collection of rows and columns        |
|**Primary Key**|Unique ID for each row                             |
|**Foreign Key**|A column that points to another table’s primary key|
|**Index**      |A lookup structure that speeds up queries          |
|**Transaction**|A group of queries that succeed or fail together   |
|**JOIN**       |Combining rows from two or more tables             |
